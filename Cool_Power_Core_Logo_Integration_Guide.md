# Cool Power Core™ Brand Emblem & Playable Browser Game Integration
**Inventor & Architect:** Thomas Gabriel Coleman (Corona, California)  
**Target Build:** `Cool_Power_Core_Horizons_Playable_Browser_Game.html`

## 1. Visual Brand Identity (`Cool_Power_Core_Brand_Logo.svg`)
The **Cool Power Core™ (CPC)** brand emblem encodes the three physical pillars of the solid-state thermodynamic engine into a scalable vector mark:
1. **Hexagonal `Ti-6Al-4V` Containment Housing:** Represents the hermetically sealed, zero-emission structural chassis.
2. **Dual Counter-Rotating Thermal & Plasma Rings (Cyan `#00E5FF` & Amber `#F59E0B`):** Symbolize the `90°C` CVD-graphene cavitation + `33.6 kV` ZVS nanosecond plasma loop operating against a cryogenic/ambient cold sink.
3. **Central Solid-State Energy Bolt (Emerald `#10B981`):** Represents net continuous electrical export (`+344.0 We/core` from `382.93 Wth` low-grade enthalpy offset) and pure `<1 ppm TDS` distilled water output.

## 2. Game Integration Points
* **Header Brand Lockup:** Animated dual-ring SVG logo (`44 × 44 px`) positioned beside the *Cool Power Core™: Horizons Unbound* title bar.
* **Browser Tab Favicon:** Embedded inline SVG data-URI favicon for desktop, console, and Android mobile bookmarks.
* **60 FPS Viewport Watermark:** Real-time procedural vector emblem rendered at the bottom-right corner of the WebGL/Canvas simulation viewport across all four hardware tiers (`Tier 0` Android Mobile through `Tier 3` Full-Body Haptic Suit + Brain Hat).
