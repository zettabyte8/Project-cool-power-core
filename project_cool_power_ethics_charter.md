## 1. PREAMBLE & STATEMENT OF INTENT
Project Cool Power, as an architectural methodology, utilizes advanced 8000x hybrid voltage cascades and frictionless free-piston thermolysis to transform ambient atmospheric toxins, smog, and moisture into life-sustaining assets: pure water, medical-grade oxygen, and surplus net electrical utility. This technology was conceived by Thomas G. Coleman for the explicit purpose of environmental restoration, humanitarian remediation, off-grid community survival, and peaceful scientific exploration across terrestrial and multi-frontier environments. 

Because this architecture achieves unprecedented energy density and self-contained logistical autonomy without conventional fuel inputs, it possesses significant dual-use potential. This Charter serves as an absolute, non-negotiable legal and cryptographic barrier preventing the weaponization or destructive exploitation of this technology by any corporate cartel, sovereign nation-state, or military entity.

## 2. STATUTORY ETHICAL CLAUSES & OPERATIONAL BOUNDARIES

### Clause I: The Absolute Non-Weaponization Mandate
The core technology, sub-components, diamond-ground Yttria-Stabilized Zirconia (YSZ) ceramic linings, 33.6kV solid-state multiplier networks, and associated design schematics of Project Cool Power shall be used exclusively for peaceful, non-lethal, civilian, and humanitarian applications. 
* **Prohibited Implementations:** The integration of this technology into kinetic weaponry, autonomous military drone arrays, uncrewed orbital attack satellites, nuclear enrichment auxiliary loops, or silent, undetected sub-surface hyperbaric naval warcraft is strictly and permanently prohibited.
* **Permitted Exceptions:** Implementation is restricted to civilian search-and-rescue craft, humanitarian disaster-relief atmospheric water harvesting hubs, public municipal air-scrubbing nodes, and peaceful scientific aerospace or deep-ocean research platforms operating under open-source transparency agreements.

### Clause II: Geopolitical Neutrality and Planetary Safe Harbor
Project Cool Power is declared a Planetary Safe Harbor Asset. The technology shall never be locked behind exclusive multi-national military blocks, nor shall it be used as a geopolitical tool to choke or deny essential life-support utilities (clean water and oxygen) to vulnerable human populations based on regional conflicts, border disputes, or economic sanctions. No licensing agreements shall ever grant exclusive, monopolistic control of atmospheric remediation to entities that prioritize resource extraction profits over local community environmental health.

### Clause III: The Planetary and Extra-Planetary Non-Contamination Compact
When Project Cool Power is scaled into non-atmospheric space-vacuum profiles, the user is legally bound to the Orbital Integrity Accord. The deployment of this technology into orbital lanes or deep-space transport must include automated, redundant, hard-coded self-destruct or de-orbit protocols to prevent the generation of high-speed kinetic debris (Kessler Syndrome). If a craft utilizing this closed-loop core is compromised during human conflict, it must be automatically steered into an atmospheric burn or a designated planetary graveyard orbit to ensure the outer space environment remains completely uncontaminated and safe for future generations of human exploration.

## 3. METHOD OF LEGAL AND CRYPTOGRAPHIC ENFORCEMENT

To ensure these clauses are not simply hollow corporate promises that a future board of directors can vote to remove, the Charter implements a Dual-Lock Enforcement System:

1. **The Irrevocable Equity Clawback (Corporate Bylaws Section 9):**  
   Any corporate entity, investor, licensee, or subsidiary found in direct violation of Clause I, II, or III of this Charter shall immediately, upon discovery, forfeit 100% of their equity, shares, and intellectual property access rights back to the Project Cool Power Treasury without financial compensation. Thomas G. Coleman retains permanent veto power over any corporate board decisions attempting to amend or weaken this Ethical Charter.
2. **The Automated Smart-Contract Interdiction (Cryptographic Lock):**  
   For funds raised via autonomous AI agents or Web3 decentralized protocols, the Multi-Signature wallet is governed by an immutable oracle-smart contract. If verified public data proves a licensing node has integrated an 8000x cascade core into a prohibited military or weaponized framework, the smart contract automatically executes an entry freeze, locking down the corresponding node’s financial transactions and cutting off real-time telemetry firmware updates until the breach is neutralized.
