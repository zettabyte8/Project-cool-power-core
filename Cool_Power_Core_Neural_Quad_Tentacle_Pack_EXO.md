# Cool Power Core™ Neural Quad-Tentacle Backpack + Exoskeleton Legs (`CPC-OCTO-EXO`)
**Inventor:** Thomas Gabriel Coleman | **Docket Reference:** `CPC-PAT-2026-014-CIP` (Case `#1,862`) | **Date:** November 27, 2026

## 1. Executive System Architecture
The **`CPC-OCTO-EXO`** integrates a self-powered dorsal continuum-robotics pack (**four `2.45 m`, 9-DOF hyper-redundant tentacles**) with **load-bearing `12-DOF` `Ti-6Al-4V` exoskeleton legs (`420.0 kg` structural rating)** and an **air-gapped Neural-Command Hat (`CPC-NEURO-CAP-SEC`)**. By routing both the **`27.80 kg` pack mass** and the **`340.0 kg` combined four-arm lifting load (`85.0 kg` per tentacle)** through a rigid lumbar-pelvic truss directly to the ground, the operator experiences **`<2.5 kg` effective spinal load**.

## 2. Closed-Loop Cool Power Core™ Power, Water & Oxygen Generation
* **Dual Dorsal Micro-Cores (`2 × µCPC-OCTO-2`):** Harvest `90°C` pneumatic-muscle/electro-hydrostatic actuator waste heat (`+765.86 Wth`) against ambient air or water sinks, exporting **`+548.0 to +682.0 We` continuous net power** (`18.5 kW` graphene supercapacitor burst), **`14.8–19.8 L/day` of `<1 ppm TDS` pure water**, and **`260–350 g/hr` USP oxygen** for indefinite off-grid disaster rescue, orbital EVA, and industrial construction.

## 3. Quad Hyper-Redundant Continuum Tentacles & Omni-Claws
* **Kinematics & Reach:** Four segmented `CFRP` / shape-memory titanium vertebrae spines (`2.45 m` extended, `0.48 m` stowed coil) driven by antagonistic electro-hydrostatic micro-actuators (`0.15 mm` repeatability, `3.2 m/s` tip velocity).
* **4-Fingered Omni-Claws & Integrated Tools:** Each end-effector features four opposable tactile fingers (`85.0 kg` vertical lift, `420 N` grip force), stereo-LiDAR cameras, an integrated `1,200 W` micro-plasma cutting/welding torch, and a `120-bar` pure-water/oxygen lance.

## 4. Load-Bearing `12-DOF` Exoskeleton Legs & Air-Gapped Brain Hat (`CPC-NEURO-CAP-SEC`)
* **Exoskeleton Lower-Body Chassis (`14.20 kg`):** Quasi-direct-drive (QDD) brushless hip, knee, and ankle actuators (`180 Nm` peak torque) with `16 mm` NASA viscoelastic heel-strike dampers (`96.2%` shock-to-heat recovery) automatically counter-balance center-of-mass shifts when all four tentacles hoist `340.0 kg` overhead.
* **Air-Gapped Neural-Command Hat (`325 g`):** Combines `64-ch` dry hdEEG, `32-ch` TD-fNIRS, and `16-ch` mechanomyography (`<18 ms` latency) protected by **zero RF radios, `Mu-Metal` Faraday lining, unidirectional optical fiber tether, NIST `ML-KEM-1024` biometric key, and `0.4 ms` somatic kill-switch**.
