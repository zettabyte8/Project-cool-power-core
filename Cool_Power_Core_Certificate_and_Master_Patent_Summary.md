## 1. CERTIFICATE OF INVENTION ATTESTATION

This document formally records and consolidates the architectural specifications, thermodynamic models, hardware designs, embedded control firmware, and patent claim sets conceived and authored by **Thomas Gabriel Coleman** for the **Cool Power Core™ (CPC)** closed-loop thermochemical, electro-cavitation, and plasma energy-and-resource recovery platform.

* **Core Operating Principle:** Coupling a `90°C` waste-heat/ambient-gradient thermal reservoir (`382.93 Wth/core` sensible + latent enthalpy offset) with a **CVD graphene-coated fluidic vortex cavitator** (`Ø1.800 mm` throat), a **33.6 kV Zero-Voltage-Switching (ZVS) nanosecond pulsed plasma stage**, an **8YSZ oxygen-ion conducting liner** (`Ø25.000 mm`), an **opposed-piston free-piston linear alternator** (`5.0 µm` oil-free air bearings, `Sm₂Co₁₇` magnets), and **Bi₂Te₃ / PMN-PT thermoelectric-pyroelectric cogeneration**.
* **Baseline & Specialized Output Envelope:** `+286.0 We` to `+371.0 We` net continuous electrical export per core (`+337.9 We/core` portfolio mean across 1,850 modeled deployments), `12.96 L/core/day` (`0.54 L/hr`) ultra-pure distillate recovery, `480.0 g/hr` medical/breathing-grade `O₂` in closed-loop ECLSS mode, and `6-Log (99.9999%)` mineralization of PFAS, dioxins, `CH₄`, `N₂O`, `SF₆`, and hazardous effluents.
