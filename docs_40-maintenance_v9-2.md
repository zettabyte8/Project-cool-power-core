# 40 — Maintenance

Annual-touch, not weekly.

| Interval | Action | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Daily | Glance at halo. Cyan = fine | — |
| 250 h | Softener cartridge | 2 min |
| 500 h | Self-clean (auto) | — |
| 1000 h | Bore-scope, Mo spot test 5 fittings, descale | 40 min |
| 2000 h | Crown inserts, reservoir descale | 2 h |
| 4000 h | Cavitator orifice, recuperator flow test | 1 h |
| 8000 h | Teardown, rotor, seals, re-baseline | 8 h |
| **12 months** | **Cartridge + ash drawer** | **5 min, no tools** |

No-tool items: ash drawer · cartridge · softener · product bottle. All front access.

## Descale
Isolate, cool, 5% citric 30 min, flush 3×, pH >6. Skip it and the recuperator blinds in 1500 h.

## What kills it
Skipped descale · skipped softener · bearing air neglect.
