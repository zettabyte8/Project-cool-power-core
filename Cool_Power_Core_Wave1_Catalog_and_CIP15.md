## 2. Flagship Anchor Architectures (`Wave 1`)

### 2.1 `CPC-AIRLIFT-48` (`#1,863`) — C-130 Airliftable 48-Hour Disaster-Relief Sanctuary Pod
* **Deployment Profile:** Two `463L` palletized modules (`4,850 kg` total) deployable via C-130/C-17 or heavy-lift tiltrotor into earthquake, hurricane, or wildfire zones; self-erects a `420 m²` triple-layer ETFE climate-controlled dome (`21.5°C / 48% RH`) housing **120 displaced residents** within **48 hours** of touchdown.
* **60-Core Closed-Loop Performance:** Bridges `90°C` CVD-graphene cavitation and `33.6 kV` ZVS plasma waste-mineralization heat (`382.93 Wth/core`) against ambient or subsurface ground-probe cold sinks—exporting **`+20.08 kWe`** continuous microgrid power, distilling **`810 L/day`** of `<1 ppm TDS` sterile drinking water from floodwater/sewage (`98.5%` closed-loop grey/blackwater recovery), and producing **`18.5 kg/day`** of `99.5%` USP medical oxygen.

### 2.2 `CPC-WELL-GRAPH` (`#1,913`) — Orphan Oil/Gas Wellhead Methane-to-Graphite Collar
* **Deployment Profile:** Clamps directly onto uncapped leaking orphan oil and natural gas wellheads (`50–850 m³/day` fugitive `CH₄`, `GWP-20 = 84`) with zero external grid power required.
* **25-Core Closed-Loop Performance:** Uses a `10%` slipstream catalytic micro-oxidizer to sustain `382.93 Wth/core` while `33.6 kV` nanosecond pulsed plasma cracks the remaining `90%` methane (`CH₄ -> C(s) + 2H₂`) into **`142–480 kg/day` of battery-grade turbostratic graphite/graphene** and **`47–160 kg/day` of green `H₂`**, exporting **`+8.71 kWe`** continuous power (`+348.2 We/core`) and achieving a **0.62-year (7.4-month) CapEx payback** via carbon credits and graphite sales.

### 2.3 `CPC-RIVER-BARGE` (`#1,963`) — Autonomous River-Mouth Plastic-to-Graphene & Struvite Barge
* **Deployment Profile:** `28 m` solar-assisted catamaran drone operating at major river estuaries (`Ganges, Yangtze, Mekong, Niger, Mississippi`) to intercept macro-plastics, microplastics, and agricultural fertilizer runoff (`N/P`) before ocean entry.
* **120-Core Closed-Loop Performance:** Shreds and pyrolyzes `3.6 TPD` of wet mixed river plastics via `90°C` cavitation and `33.6 kV` ZVS plasma without sorting or drying—yielding **`680 kg/day` of flash graphene**, **`1,694 L/day`** of `<1 ppm TDS` distilled water, **`1.15 TPD` of slow-release magnesium ammonium phosphate (struvite) fertilizer** recovered from eutrophic river water, and **`+40.73 kWe`** continuous propulsion and export power (**0.96-year** payback).
