## PART I: FLOATING OCEAN SANCTUARY CITY & MAGLEV TRANSFER HUB (`CPC-CITY-OCEAN-HUB`)

### 1. Strategic Architecture & Oceanic Crossroads Concept
The **Cool Power Core™ Floating Ocean Sanctuary City (`CPC-CITY-OCEAN-HUB`)** integrates a **2,500-resident climate-controlled artisanal sanctuary city** directly above a **submerged transoceanic pneumatic-maglev transfer terminal** (`150–250 m` depth) at major mid-ocean crossroads (e.g., **Mid-Pacific Hawaii/North-Pacific Gyre Node** on the SoCal–Tokyo corridor, or **Mid-Atlantic Azores Node** on the NYC–London corridor).

* **Modular Hexagonal Spar-Pontoon Hulls:** Eighteen pre-tensioned marine-concrete and carbon-fiber-reinforced polymer (CFRP) semi-submersible modules (`8.5 hectares` deck footprint) are tension-leg moored to deep-ocean suction caissons, decoupling the deck from `18 m` rogue waves and Category-5 (`185 mph`) hurricanes.
* **Year-Round `21.5°C (71°F)` Biospheric Canopy:** A triple-layer low-E **ETFE geodesic dome** encloses pedestrian orchard promenades, 2,500 private `28 m²` marine-composite/CLT studio homes, vertical aeroponic/aquaponic farms (`3,900 kg/day` harvest), and six tuition-free artisanal academies.
* **Unconditional Sanctuary Charter:** Open entry and exit via transoceanic maglev shuttles or marine vessels, zero curfews, unlimited stay duration, free housing/food/clinic care, and an **85% / 15% maker revenue split** for resident artisans.
