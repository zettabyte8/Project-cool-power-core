## 1. Executive System Architecture (`CPC-RIFLE-ATMOS-B`)
The **`CPC-RIFLE-ATMOS-B` ("Vortex-Bayonet" Mk. II)** integrates a quick-detach, magnetically confined **`0.38 m` (`15.0 in`) plasma bayonet (`CPC-BAYO-380`)** directly beneath the linear-induction barrel of the **`CPC-RIFLE-ATMOS`** atmospheric-condensing tri-mode weapon (`5.24 kg` total system mass).

* **Quad-Mode Engagement (`ATMOS-SLUG`, `PLASMA-BOLT`, `LASER-PULSE`, `BAYO-LANCE`):** Eliminates external chemical ammunition and separate melee breaching tools by harvesting ambient atmospheric gases (`CO2`, `N2`, `H2O`) and airborne particulates into cryo-sintered solid slugs (`1,420 m/s`), toroidal plasma bolts (`1,850 m/s`), `1,064 nm` pulsed Yb:YAG laser bursts, and a sustained `4,450 K` (`5,600 K` breach overdrive) chisel-point plasma blade.
* **Bidirectional Armor & Thermal Feedback Loop:** Connects via a `48 VDC` magnetic-inductive smart-grip bus to **`CPC-PA-MK3`** kinetic/DEW-harvesting power armor while routing `+620 Wth` of Bitter-coil waste heat into the internal **`µCPC-WPN`** core (`+318.4 We` regeneration + `-78°C` cryo-mold chilling).

## 3. USPTO Continuation-in-Part Specification & Claims (`CPC-PAT-2026-013-CIP`)

### Supplemental Specification Paragraphs
* **[0101]** Referring to **FIG. 26**, an integrated atmospheric-condensing quad-mode weapon and close-quarters breaching system (`2600`) comprises an upper atmospheric-condensing barrel assembly (`2602`) coupled to a quick-detach under-barrel magnetically confined plasma bayonet (`2610`).
* **[0102]** The under-barrel plasma bayonet (`2610`) comprises a `0.38 T` `Sm2Co17` Halbach permanent-magnet ring (`2612`) concentrically nested with a `+0.24 T` water-cooled `CuCrZr` Bitter electromagnet (`2614`) generating a `0.62 T` throat field and a `0.64 T` distal magnetic mirror pinch (`2616`) that shapes an ionized working gas into a chisel-point `0.38 m` plasma blade (`2618`) at `4,450 K` (`5,600 K` breach overdrive).
* **[0103]** A coaxial aspiration shroud (`2620`) recovers dissociated plasma species back into the cryogenic condensation mold (`2604`), while waste heat (`+620 Wth`) from the Bitter electromagnet (`2614`) is routed to the onboard Cool Power Core™ (`2606`) to accelerate slug sintering and maintain `-78°C` cryo-mold cooling. Capacitive grip sensors (`2622`) trigger a solid-state GaN crowbar quench in `<0.9 ms` upon grip release.

### Patent Claims (`Claims 74–77` | 37 CFR § 1.75 & PCT Rule 6.2(b))
* **Claim 74 (Independent):** An atmospheric-condensing directed-energy and projectile weapon (`2600`), comprising: an upper barrel assembly (`2602`) configured to aspirate ambient atmospheric gases and particulates, solidify said gases and particulates inside a cryogenic plasma-sintering mold (`2604`) cooled by a closed-loop Cool Power Core™ (`2606`), and launch solidified slugs (`2608`), plasma bolts, or laser pulses; and a quick-detach under-barrel plasma bayonet (`2610`) comprising a hybrid `Sm2Co17` Halbach array (`2612`) and electromagnet (`2614`) generating a distal magnetic mirror pinch (`2616`) to confine a `0.38 m` chisel-point plasma blade (`2618`).
* **Claim 75 (Dependent on Claim 74):** The weapon (`2600`) of claim 74, wherein waste thermal energy from the under-barrel electromagnet (`2614`) is thermally coupled to a `90°C` graphene-cavitated reservoir of the Cool Power Core™ (`2606`) to generate continuous electrical power (`+318.4 We` to `+348.0 We`) and sustain sub-zero cooling of the cryogenic mold (`2604`).
* **Claim 76 (Dependent on Claim 74):** The weapon (`2600`) of claim 74, further comprising a coaxial vapor aspiration shroud (`2620`) surrounding a proximal base of the plasma blade (`2618`) to recycle dissociated plasma gases back into the cryogenic mold (`2604`) and reduce Earth-atmosphere sustainment power by `38%`.
* **Claim 77 (Dependent on Claim 74):** The weapon (`2600`) of claim 74, further comprising a `48 VDC` inductive smart-grip interface (`2622`) receiving harvested ballistic and directed-energy surge power from an exoskeleton power armor suit and executing a `<0.9 ms` capacitive deadman magnetic quench upon loss of operator contact.
