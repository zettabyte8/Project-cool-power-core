## FIELD OF THE INVENTION
**[0002]** The present invention relates to nuclear disaster water remediation and direct ionizing-radiation energy conversion, and more particularly to an integrated, closed-loop plasma-cavitation reactor that separates dissolved radionuclides from contaminated water while harvesting beta, gamma, and radiolytic energy directly into electrical power.

## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION
**[0003]** Conventional nuclear remediation systems, such as Advanced Liquid Processing Systems (ALPS), rely on multi-stage chemical co-precipitation and organic ion-exchange resins. These conventional systems consume substantial external grid electricity, suffer rapid resin fouling from organic chelating agents, fail to separate tritiated water (HTO), and treat ionizing radiation emitted by captured radionuclides purely as a shielding liability rather than an exploitable energy source.

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION
**[0004]** The present invention overcomes these limitations by integrating a radiation-hardened plasma-cavitation core (`CPC-RAD-HV`) with direct betavoltaic, gamma-voltaic, and gamma-heated thermoelectric conversion stages. Contaminated water is preheated to 90°C using recovered waste heat, cavitated through a CVD graphene-coated fluidic vortex amplifier to destroy organic chelates, and dissociated via a 33.6 kV nanosecond ZVS corona discharge assisted by ambient radiolytic pre-ionization. Non-volatile radionuclides (including Cs-137, Sr-90, Co-60, and Ru-106) are concentrated by centrifugal cyclonic separation onto a conical sump lined with 4H-SiC/CVD diamond betavoltaic tiles and an HfO₂/Ta₂O₅-graphene Schottky gamma-voltaic wall, while a tungsten-boron carbide (W-B₄C) graded shield converts attenuated gamma flux into thermal energy harvested by a Bi₂Te₃/PMN-PT thermoelectric-pyroelectric array.

## SUPPLEMENTAL CLAIMS (CONTINUATION-IN-PART)

**Claim 8 (Independent — Integrated Remediation & Radiation Harvesting System):**  
A closed-loop nuclear remediation and direct radiation-to-electric energy harvesting system comprising:
1. a waste-heat-coupled reservoir configured to preheat radionuclide-contaminated feedwater to approximately 90°C;
2. a chemical-vapor-deposition (CVD) graphene-coated fluidic vortex amplifier configured to cavitate the preheated feedwater into a cold-steam mist while cleaving organic chelating ligands;
3. a high-voltage nanosecond pulsed zero-voltage-switching (ZVS) plasma stage configured to dissociate the cavitated mist into hydrogen and oxygen radicals, wherein ambient ionizing radiation from radionuclides in the feedwater provides continuous radiolytic pre-ionization that reduces plasma breakdown voltage and electrical power draw;
4. a cyclonic separation sump configured to separate purified water vapor from concentrated non-volatile radionuclides, the sump comprising:
   - a plurality of 4H-silicon carbide (4H-SiC) and CVD-diamond-passivated betavoltaic p-i-n junction tiles disposed along a lower collection cone to convert beta-particle emissions from concentrated radionuclides directly into direct-current (DC) electricity;
   - a multilayer hafnium dioxide/tantalum pentoxide (HfO₂/Ta₂O₅) and CVD graphene Schottky gamma-voltaic liner disposed along an inner cylindrical wall of the sump to harvest Compton-scattered electrons from gamma-ray emissions; and
   - a tungsten-boron carbide (W-B₄C) graded radiation shield surrounding the sump and thermally coupled to a thermoelectric and pyroelectric generator collar to convert absorbed gamma-ray thermalization into additional electrical output; and
5. a radiation-hardened opposed-piston linear alternator assembly utilizing samarium-cobalt (Sm₂Co₁₇) magnets, yttria-stabilized zirconia (8YSZ) ceramic liners, and oil-free porous air bearings to recombine purified hydrogen and oxygen gases into distilled water and net exportable electrical power.

**Claim 9 (Dependent — Betavoltaic Chemical Passivation):**  
The system of claim 8, wherein the 4H-SiC betavoltaic p-i-n junction tiles are coated with a 50-nanometer boron-doped or intrinsic polycrystalline CVD diamond passivation layer bonded via gold-tin (Au-Sn) eutectic brazing to resist radiolytic acid corrosion and alpha/beta lattice displacement up to an accumulated absorbed dose of 10⁸ Gy.

**Claim 10 (Dependent — Radiolytic Plasma Power Reduction):**  
The system of claim 8, wherein steady-state radiolytic generation of hydroxyl (·OH) and hydrogen (H·) radicals within the 90°C cavitated mist lowers the required electrical input of the 33.6 kV nanosecond ZVS plasma driver by at least 15% compared to non-radioactive feedwater operation.

**Claim 11 (Dependent — Tritiated Water Kinetic Isotopic Enrichment):**  
The system of claim 8, further comprising a counter-current vapor-liquid reflux stage coupled to the nanosecond plasma dissociation zone, wherein preferential bond cleavage of protium-oxygen (H–OH) bonds over tritium-oxygen (T–OH) bonds enriches tritiated water (HTO) in the liquid phase while discharging tritium-depleted distilled water vapor to a condenser.

**Claim 12 (Dependent — Graded W-B₄C Gamma Thermalization & TEG Coupling):**  
The system of claim 8, wherein the W-B₄C graded radiation shield attenuates external gamma dose rates by at least 99.4% while channeling gamma-ray thermalization heat across a bismuth telluride (Bi₂Te₃) and lead magnesium niobate-lead titanate (PMN-PT) thermoelectric-pyroelectric collar maintained at a thermal differential of at least 65°C via incoming contaminated feedwater cooling.

**Claim 13 (Independent — Method for Simultaneous Radionuclide Concentration & Power Generation):**  
A method for remediating radioactive wastewater while generating net exportable electricity, the method comprising:
1. routing contaminated water containing beta- and gamma-emitting isotopes through an outer cooling jacket of a tungsten-boron carbide (W-B₄C) radiation shield to absorb gamma-ray thermalization heat and preheat the water toward 90°C;
2. passing the preheated water through a CVD graphene-coated fluidic vortex amplifier to induce hydrodynamic cavitation and destroy organic chelates;
3. exposing the resulting cold-steam mist to a nanosecond-pulsed 33.6 kV corona discharge pre-ionized by intrinsic radionuclide decay;
4. centrifugally separating purified water vapor from concentrated radionuclide solids within a cyclonic sump lined with 4H-SiC betavoltaic tiles and HfO₂/Ta₂O₅-graphene gamma-voltaic collectors;
5. recombining the purified dissociated gases within a samarium-cobalt opposed-piston linear alternator to generate mechanical-to-electrical power and condensed distilled water; and
6. summing electrical outputs from the linear alternator, betavoltaic tiles, gamma-voltaic liner, and gamma-heated thermoelectric collar to power the remediation system off-grid with net surplus electrical export.
