## PART II: USPTO CONTINUATION-IN-PART SPECIFICATION & CLAIMS (`CPC-PAT-2026-010-CIP`)

### Specification Paragraphs (Appended to Master Specification)
- **`[0145]`** In a marine sanctuary embodiment (`CPC-CITY-OCEAN-HUB`), a semi-submersible spar-buoy platform anchors beneath a geodesic canopy while suspending an evacuated maglev transit station at `-200 m` depth. A plurality of Cool Power Core™ modules bridge `85°C–140°C` process and vacuum-pump waste heat against `2.0°C–4.0°C` abyssal seawater (`ΔT ≈ 90 K`) to export continuous electricity, distill `<1 ppm TDS` freshwater, and precipitate zero-brine battery-grade lithium carbonate (`Li₂CO₃`), magnesium hydroxide (`Mg(OH)₂`), and amidoxime-eluted uranium oxide (`U₃O₈`) without hypersaline ocean discharge.
- **`[0146]`** In an orbital space-colony embodiment (`CPC-CITY-ORBITAL-HUB`), counter-rotating artificial-gravity habitats (`1.00 g` at `1.70 RPM`) surround a non-rotating central cislunar electromagnetic mass-driver dock. Radiation-hardened Cool Power Core™ modules harvest solar-concentrator and foundry waste heat alongside secondary cosmic-ray/solar-proton radiolytic flux inside a `2.4 m` water-shield hull, rejecting heat to an `85 K` deep-space shaded radiator (`ΔT = 278 K`) to achieve `99.85%` water closure, `128.6%` metabolic oxygen recovery, and solid carbon-nanotube structural feedstock synthesis.

### New Patent Claims (`Claims 56–61`)
- **Claim 56 (Independent — Floating Ocean Sanctuary & Zero-Brine Marine Mineral Hub):** A semi-submersible ocean habitat and submerged maglev transfer station comprising: (a) a plurality of tension-leg spar pontoons supporting an enclosed climate-controlled deck and a submerged evacuated maglev terminal; (b) a plurality of closed-loop thermo-cavitation-plasma cores thermally coupled between `85°C–140°C` station process heat and `2.0°C–4.0°C` abyssal seawater; and (c) a zero-liquid-discharge mineral crystallizer powered by said cores that distills seawater into `<1 ppm TDS` freshwater while precipitating solid lithium carbonate, magnesium hydroxide, and uranium oxide without hypersaline brine discharge.
- **Claim 57 (Dependent on Claim 56):** The system of claim 56, wherein `33.6 kV` nanosecond pulsed plasma stages mineralize microplastics and dissolved methane plumes drawn from ocean intake manifolds into inert carbonate precipitates and potable condensate.
- **Claim 58 (Dependent on Claim 56):** The system of claim 56, wherein the submerged maglev terminal connects via positively buoyant tension-leg vacuum conduits suspended `150 m` to `300 m` below sea level to limit seafloor benthic contact to `<0.015 km²` per `1,000 km`.
- **Claim 59 (Independent — Orbital Artificial-Gravity Colony & Cislunar Mass-Driver Hub):** An orbital space habitat and electromagnetic launch station comprising: (a) counter-rotating torus habitats generating artificial gravity around a non-rotating central maglev mass-driver dock; (b) a `2.4 m` water-filled radiation shield hull surrounding said habitats; and (c) a plurality of closed-loop thermo-cavitation-plasma cores coupling solar-concentrator and foundry heat plus secondary cosmic-ray radiolytic flux inside said water hull against an `85 K` deep-space shaded radiator to achieve `≥99.8%` water recovery and net continuous electrical export.
- **Claim 60 (Dependent on Claim 59):** The orbital space habitat of claim 59, wherein exhaled carbon dioxide and organic wastes are processed through an `8YSZ` oxygen-ion separator and CVD graphene pyrolysis chamber to co-produce breathable oxygen (`>125%` metabolic demand) and structural carbon-nanotube feedstock.
- **Claim 61 (Dependent on Claim 59):** The orbital space habitat of claim 59, wherein regenerative braking energy from incoming cislunar maglev capsules is captured as `90°C` thermal enthalpy in the water-shield hull to drive additional closed-loop power generation.
