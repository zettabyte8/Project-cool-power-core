## What Is Claimed Is:

**1.** *[Independent | CPC-PAT-2026-001 | FIGS. 1-3]* A closed-loop thermo-chemical power and fluid-regeneration core (100), comprising: a thermal preheating reservoir (102) configured to receive an external waste-heat flux and elevate an aqueous working fluid to an operating temperature of approximately 90°C; a hydrodynamic cavitation chamber (104) lined with chemical-vapor-deposition (CVD) graphene (106) and coupled to the thermal preheating reservoir (102), configured to induce acoustic and hydrodynamic micro-bubble collapse within the preheated aqueous working fluid; a nanosecond pulsed plasma reactor (108) driven by a zero-voltage-switching (ZVS) high-voltage pulse generator (110) operating at approximately 33.6 kV, configured to dissociate cavitated vapor into reactive radical species and mineralize dissolved contaminants; an oil-free gas-bearing opposed-piston free-piston linear alternator (112) having 5-micrometer aerodynamic clearances (114) and permanent-magnet translators (116), configured to convert thermal-plasma expansion into continuous alternating electrical power; and a cold-sink condenser loop (118) configured to reject low-grade heat to an external heat sink and recover sterile distilled water (120) in a closed loop.

**2.** *[Dependent (Claim 1) | CPC-PAT-2026-001 | FIG. 1]* The closed-loop thermo-chemical power and fluid-regeneration core (100) of claim 1, wherein the CVD graphene lining (106) reduces the acoustic cavitation inception threshold pressure such that hydrodynamic cavitation power draw is maintained below 22.0 W per core.

**3.** *[Dependent (Claim 1) | CPC-PAT-2026-001 | FIG. 2]* The closed-loop thermo-chemical power and fluid-regeneration core (100) of claim 1, wherein the external waste-heat flux provides at least 382.93 Wth of sensible and latent enthalpy offset per core at 90°C to reduce internal electrical heating demand.

**4.** *[Dependent (Claim 1) | CPC-PAT-2026-001 | FIG. 2]* The closed-loop thermo-chemical power and fluid-regeneration core (100) of claim 1, further comprising a solid-oxide yttria-stabilized zirconia (8YSZ) oxygen-ion separation membrane (122) coupled downstream of the nanosecond pulsed plasma reactor (108) to extract medical-grade gaseous oxygen (124).

**5.** *[Dependent (Claim 1) | CPC-PAT-2026-001 | FIG. 3]* The closed-loop thermo-chemical power and fluid-regeneration core (100) of claim 1, wherein the nanosecond pulsed plasma reactor (108) cleaves carbon-fluorine (C-F) bonds of per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS) with a destruction and removal efficiency (DRE) of at least 99.9999% (6-Log) and precipitates inert calcium fluoride (CaF2) mineral solids (126).

**6.** *[Dependent (Claim 1) | CPC-PAT-2026-001 | FIG. 3]* The closed-loop thermo-chemical power and fluid-regeneration core (100) of claim 1, further comprising a silicon-on-insulator (SOI) electronic control unit (128) consuming less than 15.0 W and regulating ZVS pulse frequency (110) and linear alternator (112) phase synchronization.

**7.** *[Dependent (Claim 1) | CPC-PAT-2026-001 | FIG. 1]* A modular containerized skid comprising a plurality of closed-loop cores (100) of claim 1 manifolded in parallel across a common waste-heat header, a cold-sink condenser header (118), and a direct-current microgrid bus.
