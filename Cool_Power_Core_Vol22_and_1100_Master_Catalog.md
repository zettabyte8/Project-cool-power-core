## 1. Executive Summary: Bio-Medicine, Regenerative Agriculture & Ecological Restoration (Volume XXII)
Volume XXII expands the **Cool Power Core™ (CPC)** catalog from 1,050 to **1,100 validated operational use cases**, focusing on sterile bio-medical systems, regenerative agriculture, large-scale watershed and permafrost restoration, and multi-biome closed ecological life support:
- **Bio-Medicine & Clinical Tri-Generation (`#1,051 – #1,062`):** Replaces high-energy hospital autoclaves, pharmaceutical Water-for-Injection (WFI) stills, and anesthetic/surgical-smoke scrubbers by pairing `60°C–121°C` clinical waste-heat loops with `90°C` CVD graphene vortex cavitation and `33.6 kV` nanosecond pulsed plasma—delivering **99.9999% (6-Log) spore/pathogen destruction**, **99.5% USP medical O₂**, and **+182.0 to +354.0 We/core** continuous power.
- **Regenerative Agriculture & Carbon-Negative Soil Systems (`#1,063 – #1,074`):** Integrates `CPC-AGRI` skids onto biochar pyrolysis kilns, precision mycoprotein fermenters, arid-zone solar desalination, and livestock lagoons—producing carbon-negative biochar, plasma-fixed nitrate (`NO₃⁻`) liquid fertilizer, and **14,500 L/day** pathogen-free water per 100-core array.
- **Ecological & Planetary Restoration (`#1,075 – #1,100`):** Deploys autonomous `CPC-ECO` barges and field skids for wildfire-ash heavy-metal precipitation, eutrophic lake microcystin destruction, permafrost thaw-lake methane (`GWP 84`) oxidation, river-mouth microplastic mineralization, and sealed multi-biome ecological domes.
