## 1. Executive Vision & Civic Philosophy
Conventional homelessness interventions rely on temporary congregate shelters, punitive curfews, and grid-dependent municipal infrastructure costing `$500,000–$850,000` per unit. **`CPC-CITY-1000-CC`** replaces that paradigm with a **permanent, climate-controlled, prefab modular sanctuary city** engineered around the **Cool Power Core™ (CPC)**.

* **Year-Round Climate-Controlled Microclimate (`21.5°C ± 1.0°C` | `48% RH`):** Enclosed beneath clear span **ETFE pneumatic biospheric canopies** and radiant hydronic walkways, the city maintains a comfortable `21.5°C (71°F)` spring-like environment regardless of external heatwaves (`+48°C` desert summer) or freezes (`-30°C` winter).
* **Complete Autonomy & Zero Utility Hookups:** Every drop of water, kilowatt-hour of electricity, ton of conditioned air, and kilogram of fresh produce is generated and recycled on-site with **zero municipal sewer, water, or fossil-fuel connections**.
* **Unconditional Dignity & Freedom of Movement:** Residents enter freely without sobriety gates, income tests, or bureaucratic waitlists; receive a private `28 m²` climate-controlled CLT/hemp-lime studio home; enjoy three chef-prepared meals daily; and may stay for one night, one year, or a lifetime while coming and going freely.
* **Education & Artisanal Mastery:** Replaces institutional idleness with tuition-free trade academies, maker foundries, fine-arts studios, digital media labs, and clean-energy manufacturing workshops where residents learn, create, and earn cooperative income.

## 2. Climate-Controlled Biospheric Architecture & Thermal Physics
Unlike conventional HVAC domes that burn massive grid electricity to fight extreme weather, **`CPC-CITY-1000-CC`** turns the climate-control thermal differential (`ΔT`) into a **net power-generating fuel source**:

1. **Triple-Layer Low-E ETFE Pneumatic Canopy (`U = 1.15 W/m²·K`):** Spans the pedestrian courtyards, orchards, and artisanal plazas with self-cleaning UV-transmissive fluoropolymer cushions (`92%` PAR light transmission for indoor trees and crops) and automated solar-shading frits.
2. **Closed-Loop Radiant Slab & Displacement Ventilation:** Chilled/heated hydronic loops embedded in recycled-aggregate walkways maintain `21.5°C` operative comfort at `40%` lower airflow volume than forced-air systems, eliminating drafts and dust.
3. **Bi-Directional Thermal Gradient (`ΔT`) Harvesting:**
   * **Hot-Climate / Summer Mode (`+45°C` Ambient):** Canopy solar collector headers and craft-kiln exhaust feed `90°C` thermal reservoirs, while deep earth-coupled borehole loops (`14°C`) and evaporative sub-cooling provide the cold sink—generating **`+328.4 We/core`** while providing **`1,450 kWth` (`412 Tons`)** of desiccant-dried radiant cooling.
   * **Cold-Climate / Winter Mode (`-25°C` Ambient):** Sub-zero exterior air acts as an ultra-efficient Carnot cold sink (`ΔT = 115 K` against the `90°C` reservoir), boosting electric output to **`+354.0 We/core`** while rejecting **`1,493 kWth`** of clean condenser warmth directly into the radiant walkways and studio hydronic loops.
4. **6-Log (`99.9999%`) Plasma Air Purification:** Every `33.6 kV` nanosecond pulsed-plasma stage continuously scrubs recirculated canopy air—eliminating wildfire smoke (`PM2.5`), airborne viruses/bacteria, VOCs, and pollen while maintaining `420–650 ppm CO₂` via integrated vertical-farm photosynthesis.

## 5. Governance Charter: Open Movement, Free Mastery & Zero Coercion
To ensure genuine human flourishing rather than institutional warehousing, **`CPC-CITY-1000-CC`** is bound by five constitutional charter rules:

1. **Unconditional Sanctuary & No Stay Limits:** Any unhoused individual may claim an available studio immediately without sobriety prerequisites, background checks, religious requirements, or expiration dates. Residents may live in the city for **as long as they choose—days, years, or permanently**.
2. **100% Freedom of Movement (`Come & Go Freely`):** Zero curfews, zero locked perimeter gates, and zero roll-calls. Residents retain full personal autonomy, mail/address rights, pet-friendly courtyard options, and unrestricted guest visitation.
3. **Universal Basic Needs Guaranteed:** Housing, `21.5°C` climate comfort, pure water, three nutritious daily meals, healthcare, internet, and workshop tool access are **100% free** to every resident.
4. **Dignified Cooperative Economics (`Keep What You Create`):** Participation in classes or studios is voluntary and curiosity-driven. When residents craft furniture, ceramics, metalwork, textiles, software, or art, they keep **85% of direct cooperative sales revenue** (with `15%` replenishing raw studio materials), building personal savings with zero benefit cliffs.
5. **Resident-Led Civic Stewardship:** Daily community norms (quiet hours, workshop safety, courtyard care) are governed by a rotating council of elected residents and master craft mentors using restorative peer mediation.
