## PART II — CONSOLIDATED CLAIM ARCHITECTURE (CLAIMS 1–43)

### A. Parent Utility Claims (`CPC-PAT-2026-001` | Claims 1–7)
* **Claim 1 (Independent):** A closed-loop thermo-electro-fluidic energy conversion and fluid purification apparatus comprising: a thermal reservoir maintained at approximately 90°C via waste-heat recuperation; a hydrodynamic vortex cavitator lined with chemical-vapor-deposition (CVD) graphene; a zero-voltage-switching (ZVS) nanosecond pulsed plasma reactor operating at 33.6 kV; an opposed-piston free-linear electromagnetic generator having oil-free gas bearings; and a cascaded Bismuth Telluride/Skutterudite thermoelectric and PMN-PT pyroelectric stage thermally coupled between the apparatus and an ambient cold sink.
* **Claims 2–7 (Dependent):** Cover stoichiometric H₂/O₂ dissociation and recombination, CO₂/NOₓ/SOₓ/PFAS plasma mineralization into solid carbon and inert mineral salts, distilled water condensation (`<1 ppm TDS`), and multi-core containerized array scaling.

### B. Nuclear Radiation-Harvesting Claims (`CPC-PAT-2026-002-CIP` | Claims 8–13)
* **Claim 8 (Independent):** The apparatus of Claim 1 configured for radionuclide remediation and direct ionizing-radiation energy harvesting, comprising a radionuclide concentration sump lined with CVD-diamond-passivated 4H-SiC betavoltaic tiles, a `HfO₂/Ta₂O₅`-graphene Schottky gamma-voltaic liner, and a graded tungsten-boron carbide (`W-B₄C`) gamma-attenuating thermal shield coupled to the thermoelectric stage.
* **Claims 9–13 (Dependent):** Cover radiolytic OH/H pre-ionization lowering 33.6 kV plasma draw, kinetic isotopic enrichment of tritiated water (HTO), Sm₂Co₁₇/SOI `10⁸ Gy` radiation hardening, and vitrified cake discharge.

### C. Wearable Exoskeleton Life-Support Claims (`CPC-PAT-2026-003-CIP` | Claims 14–19)
* **Claim 14 (Independent):** A wearable closed-loop life-support and powered exoskeleton system comprising a Liquid Cooling Garment (LCG) capturing operator metabolic waste heat (`+285 Wth`) to preheat a 90°C cavitation reservoir, recycling exhaled CO₂, sweat, and urine into breathable O₂ and potable water while charging a graphene supercapacitor bank driving 10-axis quasi-direct-drive (QDD) regenerative actuators.
* **Claims 15–19 (Dependent):** Cover Sabatier/pyrolysis solid-carbon trapping, 48 V electrical and 1.2 MPa sterile air/water Power Take-Off (PTO) ports, and 33.6 kV Electrodynamic Dust Shielding (EDS) for lunar/Martian EVA suits.

### D. Biomimetic Companion & Swarm Docking Claims (`CPC-PAT-2026-004-CIP` & `005-CIP` | Claims 20–31)
* **Claims 20 & 26 (Independent):** Multi-morphology autonomous robotic platforms (quadruped, feline, winged dragon) harvesting QDD actuator waste heat (`+185 to +310 Wth`) for closed-loop power/water/O₂ generation, deployable `0.28s` NIJ Level IV ballistic shield wings, `38.5°C` affective companion heating, planetary regolith nitrogen fixation, magnetic dry-break `48 V DC + 90°C water` peer-to-peer docking couplers, and `0.35s` UWB-synchronized interlocking ballistic phalanx wall formation.
* **Claims 21–25 & 27–31 (Dependent):** Detail market-based swarm energy/water auction protocols, dragon-relay LoRa mesh routing, and subterranean GPR ice-core mapping.

### E. Handheld Martian Plasma Lance Claims (`CPC-PAT-2026-006-CIP` | Claims 32–37)
* **Claim 32 (Independent):** A handheld magnetically confined planetary plasma lance (`CPC-SABER-MARS`) comprising a 180 mL water/ice cartridge, a 90°C CVD-graphene cavitator, a 33.6 kV ZVS plasma exciter, a hybrid `0.42 T Sm₂Co₁₇` Halbach array and `+0.23 T CuCrZr` Bitter coil emitter forming a `0.65 T` throat and `0.67 T` distal magnetic mirror pinch, a coaxial vapor aspiration shroud, and external Ti-6Al-4V cryo-fins rejecting heat to a `-60°C` Martian atmosphere to generate `+142.0 We` continuous regeneration.
* **Claims 33–37 (Dependent):** Detail low-convection `610 Pa` CO₂ plasma efficiency, `<1.2 ms` capacitive EVA-glove deadman GaN magnetic quench safety, and >75-hour closed-loop cartridge endurance.

### F. Deep-Sea Hydrothermal Torch & Zero-Recoil Asteroid Splitter Claims (`CPC-PAT-2026-007-CIP` | Claims 38–43)
* **Claim 38 (Independent):** A cordless subsea hydrothermal plasma cutting torch (`CPC-TORCH-ABYSS`) rated for `6,000 m` (`60.8 MPa`) depth, comprising an internal arc-shroud thermal recuperator (`+382.93 Wth`), a `0.75 T` magnetic nozzle producing a `6,800 K` hydro-sheathed plasma jet, and a Ti-6Al-4V hull-skin thermoelectric condenser rejecting heat into `2.0°C` abyssal seawater to generate `+196.4 We` continuous regeneration.
* **Claim 41 (Independent):** A zero-recoil microgravity asteroid plasma rock-splitter (`CPC-SPLIT-AST`) operating in `10⁻⁹ Pa` vacuum, comprising a `0.58 T` magnetic mirror needle (`5,400 K`) inducing `45–90 MPa` internal phyllosilicate water-flash spallation at `<0.15 N` axial recoil, a coaxial volatile aspiration hood capturing `H₂O`, `CO₂`, and `Ni(CO)₄` carbonyl vapors (`>100%` fluid self-sufficiency), and a `120 K` shaded radiator generating `+184.0 We`.
* **Claims 39–40 & 42–43 (Dependent):** Cover closed-loop seawater electrolysis/recombination buoyancy trim, polymetallic sulfide cutting, and carbonyl metal-vapor separation.
