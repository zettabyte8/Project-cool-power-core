### Detailed Specification Paragraphs
**[0240]** Referring to **FIG. 22**, a self-contained linear ridge-and-valley megastructure is constructed directly upon degraded or marginal terrain **(2200)**—including hyper-arid alkali salt playas, abandoned open-pit mine scars, sub-zero permafrost barrens, or coastal sabkha flats—without displacing arable agricultural land or intact old-growth ecosystems. The exterior structural shell forms an aerodynamic natural-hill profile rising at a `22°–26°` slope to a crest height of `115 m` to `165 m`, punctured by sculpted transverse saddle passes that channel prevailing winds and capture seasonal precipitation.

**[0241]** The exterior slopes and terrace decks are covered by a continuous **`2.5 m` to `4.0 m` living soil, basalt root-barrier, and native-forest radiation mantle (2202)** having an areal mass density exceeding `450 g/cm²`, which attenuates galactic cosmic rays and solar particle events by greater than `97.5%` (`<0.35 mSv/yr` interior dose) while providing a thermal transmittance below `0.07 W/m²K`.

**[0242]** Along the open V-shaped interior canyon, `18` to `28` **staggered sunlit residential terraces (2204)** step downward toward a car-free **central valley park (2206)**, ensuring that every residential and hospitality unit (`16,500` to `82,500` units) receives direct solar exposure, a private garden yard, and zero-septic `CPC-RES` utility autonomy.

**[0243]** Beneath the valley floor, an `12`- to `18`-level **subterranean commerce and galleria canyon (2208)** extends to `-72 m` to `-108 m` depth, housing commercial exchanges, **heliostat-illuminated AI data vaults and 8-tier vertical farms (2210)**, a **distributed Cool Power Core™ (CPC) utility gallery (2212)** comprising `65,000` to `350,000` thermo-cavitation plasma cores, and dual-bore **evacuated maglev transit tubes (`CPC-LOOP`, 2214)** linked to vertical freight shafts that deliver groceries and goods directly to residential pantries within `12 minutes`.

**[0244]** Referring to **FIG. 23**, **degraded site inputs (2300)**—comprising Acid Mine Drainage (AMD), heavy-metal tailings leachate, hypersaline sabkha brine, or municipal blackwater—are routed into the **CPC plasma-cavitation array (2302)** operating at `90°C` via CVD-graphene vortex cavitation and `33.6 kV` zero-voltage-switching (ZVS) nanosecond pulsed plasma. Thermal differentials (ΔT = 75 K to 125 K) are bridged against **deep cold-sink piles and brine heat exchangers (2304)** (`15°C` geothermal, `-35°C` Arctic air, or `12°C` deep seawater) to yield **Zero-Liquid-Discharge (ZLD) vitrified slag, battery-grade `Li₂CO₃`, `Mg(OH)₂`, and `<1 ppm TDS` distilled water (2306)**, **`+37.07 MWe` net exportable power and `21.5°C` valley microclimate conditioning (2308)**, and **automated subterranean shuttle logistics (2310)**.

### Claims 62–67 (`CPC-PAT-2026-011-CIP` — PCT Rule 6.2(b) Format)

* **Claim 62 (Independent):** A self-contained linear ridge-and-valley megastructure constructed on degraded or marginal terrain `(2200)`, comprising:
  * (a) an aerodynamic ridge shell having exterior slopes covered by a living soil and vegetation radiation-shielding mantle `(2202)` at least `2.2 meters` thick attenuating cosmic and solar radiation by at least `96%`;
  * (b) an open interior valley flanked by staggered sunlit residential terraces `(2204)` descending to a central valley park `(2206)`;
  * (c) a multi-level subterranean commerce and vertical-farming canyon `(2208, 2210)` disposed beneath said central valley park `(2206)` and housing an evacuated maglev transit and freight conduit `(2214)`; and
  * (d) a distributed array of Cool Power Core™ thermo-cavitation plasma modules `(2212, 2302)` thermally coupled between waste heat from said subterranean canyon `(2208, 2210)` and deep cold-sink heat exchangers `(2216, 2304)` to mineralize site wastewater into `<1 ppm TDS` distilled water `(2306)` while exporting net continuous electrical power `(2308)`.
* **Claim 63 (Dependent on Claim 62):** The megastructure of Claim 62, wherein said degraded terrain `(2200)` comprises an abandoned open-pit mine or tailings basin, and said thermo-cavitation plasma modules `(2302)` mineralize Acid Mine Drainage (AMD) and per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS) into inert vitrified slag `(2306)` with zero liquid outfall.
* **Claim 64 (Dependent on Claim 62):** The megastructure of Claim 62, wherein said degraded terrain `(2200)` comprises a coastal alkali sabkha or salt flat, and said cold-sink heat exchangers `(2304)` couple to deep ocean seawater to co-produce battery-grade lithium carbonate (`Li₂CO₃`) and magnesium hydroxide (`Mg(OH)₂`) `(2306)` without hypersaline brine discharge.
* **Claim 65 (Dependent on Claim 62):** The megastructure of Claim 62, wherein said subterranean freight conduit `(2214)` connects to automated vertical shuttle shafts `(2310)` configured to deliver temperature-controlled food and commerce goods from said vertical farms `(2210)` directly into individual residential pantries along said staggered terraces `(2204)` within `15 minutes`.
* **Claim 66 (Independent):** A method of rehabilitating ecologically degraded or non-arable land `(2200)` into a net-positive human settlement, comprising:
  * (a) erecting a linear ridge-and-valley earth-sheltered superstructure over said degraded terrain `(2200)` with a living soil mantle `(2202)` exceeding `450 g/cm²` areal density;
  * (b) routing contaminated site leachate, saline brine, and domestic blackwater `(2300)` through a `90°C` graphene-vortex cavitator and `33.6 kV` nanosecond pulsed plasma reactor `(2302)`;
  * (c) bridging process heat against sub-surface cold-sink piles `(2216, 2304)` to generate continuous electrical power `(2308)` exceeding total settlement demand; and
  * (d) circulating recovered `<1 ppm TDS` distillate `(2306)` and conditioned `21.5°C` air through subterranean vertical farms `(2210)` and sunlit terraced residences `(2204)`.
* **Claim 67 (Dependent on Claim 66):** The method of Claim 66, wherein said degraded terrain `(2200)` comprises sub-zero permafrost barrens supported on thermal-break foundation piers `(2216)` that prevent permafrost thaw while harvesting ambient `-35°C` cold-sink thermal differentials and oxidizing thermokarst methane plumes.
