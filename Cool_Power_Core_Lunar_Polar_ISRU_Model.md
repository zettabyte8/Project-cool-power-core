## 1. Lunar Polar Boundary Conditions & Cryo-Sink Advantage

Operating at the Lunar South Pole ($89.5^\circ\text{S}$) provides an unmatched thermodynamic environment for the **Cool Power Core**:
* **40 K ($-233^\circ\text{C}$) Cryogenic Cold Sink:** Permanently shadowed crater floors act as passive deep-space radiators, establishing a $\Delta T > 2,900\text{ K}$ thermal gradient across the **8YSZ ceramic liner** ($3,000^\circ\text{C}$ core) and **$\text{Bi}_2\text{Te}_3$/Skutterudite TEG + PMN-PT pyroelectric stack**.
* **Zero-Boil-Off Passive Cryocooling:** Product $\text{O}_2$ ($90.2\text{ K}$ boiling point) and $\text{H}_2$ ($20.3\text{ K}$ boiling point) liquefy with minimal mechanical compressor work by radiating latent heat directly into the $40\text{ K}$ PSR sink.
* **Vacuum-Compatible Gas Bearings:** The **$5.0\,\mu\text{m}$ porous carbon-graphite air bearings** operate in a hermetically sealed $0.15\text{ MPa}$ internal working loop, completely isolated from abrasive lunar regolith dust and external vacuum ($10^{-12}\text{ Torr}$).

## 2. Dual-Feedstock Stoichiometry (Regolith Ice + Ilmenite Reduction)

Rather than relying solely on water ice mining, the **CPC-LUNAR-ISRU** routes high-energy atomic hydrogen radicals ($\text{H}^\bullet$) and $90^\circ\text{C}$ cavitated steam generated by the **$33.6\text{ kV}$ ZVS nanosecond plasma** directly over beneficiated lunar ilmenite ($\text{FeTiO}_3$):

1. **Primary Ice Dissociation ($0.54\text{ L/hr} = 540\text{ g/hr } \text{H}_2\text{O}$):**
   $$2\text{H}_2\text{O} \xrightarrow{33.6\text{ kV ZVS Plasma}} 2\text{H}_2 + \text{O}_2$$
   Yields **$480.0\text{ g/hr } \text{O}_2$** and **$60.0\text{ g/hr } \text{H}_2$** radicals.

2. **Plasma-Assisted Ilmenite Reduction ($2.28\text{ kg/hr } \text{FeTiO}_3$):**
   $$\text{FeTiO}_3 + \text{H}_2\text{ (plasma radicals)} \xrightarrow{900^\circ\text{C}} \text{Fe} + \text{TiO}_2 + \text{H}_2\text{O}$$
   The intermediate $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ is immediately recycled back into the **$90^\circ\text{C}$ CVD graphene vortex cavitator**, unlocking an additional **$240.0\text{ g/hr } \text{O}_2$** from lunar rock while reducing required ice excavation by **33%** per kilogram of oxygen produced.
