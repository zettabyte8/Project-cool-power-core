## Part I: Single-Family Zero-Septic Residential Core (#351 / #435) — Full P&ID & Financial ROI Model

### 1. System Architecture & Process Flow (P&ID Specification)
The **`CPC-RES-2` Residential Tri-Generation Appliance** is a weatherproof, sound-attenuated (`<42 dBA`) exterior utility cabinet (`1.1 m × 0.75 m × 1.4 m`, `145 kg`) housing **two `CPC-GRID` cores** (`0.54 L/hr` each = `1.08 L/hr` total processing capacity = `25.9 L/hr` max peak surge buffer with a `300 L` insulated equalization tank). It completely eliminates conventional septic tanks, leach fields, and municipal sewer hookups.

* **Stream 1 — Equalization & Solids Separation (`TK-101` / `SEP-102`):** Household greywater (showers, laundry, sinks) and macerated blackwater (`450 L/day` average for a 4-person home) enter a sealed `300 L` primary equalization vessel. A `50-micron` rotary self-cleaning screen and hydrocyclone separate `0.42 kg/day` of organic/mineral solids into a heated pyrolysis/mineralization cartridge (`CART-103`), producing sterile, odorless NPK mineral ash emptied twice yearly.
* **Stream 2 — 90°C Solar-Thermal & Heat-Pump Reservoir (`HX-104`):** Roof-mounted evacuated-tube solar collectors paired with a small R290/CO₂ heat-pump desuperheater preheat the clarified water to **90°C** (`+1,098.3 Wth` thermal offset), lowering surface tension and cutting CVD graphene vortex cavitation power by **45%** (`16.5 W` per core).
* **Stream 3 — 33.6 kV Nanosecond Plasma & 3,000°C Thermal Oxidation (`CPC-A` / `CPC-B`):** Cavitated 90°C cold steam enters the dual `33.6 kV` ZVS nanosecond plasma chambers (`20 W/core`) and `50 Hz` opposed-piston `8YSZ` thermal cores. All pathogens, viruses, pharmaceuticals, endocrine disruptors, microplastics, and PFAS are mineralized to `CO₂`, `N₂`, and inert mineral salts (`>6-Log` reduction).
* **Stream 4 — Closed-Loop Condensation, O₃ Polishing & Potable Remineralization (`COND-105` / `POL-106`):** Exhaust vapor condenses through a plate heat exchanger that heats the home's **200 L domestic hot-water tank to 55°C**. Condensate passes through an activated-carbon catalytic ozone destructor, calcite/magnesium remineralizer, and real-time inline turbidity/ORP/TDS sensors (`<15 ppm TDS`, `0 CFU/100 mL`), returning **443.2 L/day (`98.5%` recovery)** of potable water to the home's pressure tank.
