- **33.6 kV Nanosecond Pulsed Plasma & 3,000°C Opposed-Piston Core (`+307.00 We`):** Exposes aerosolized droplets to `15 ns` high-voltage streamer radicals (`⋅OH`, `O(¹D)`, `O₃`) followed by `3,000°C` adiabatic YSZ-liner combustion—achieving **>12-Log (`10⁻¹²`) Sterility Assurance Level (SAL)** and complete organic-to-CO₂/H₂O mineralization.
- **Zero-Sewer WFI Distillate & Vitrified Ash (`12.96 L/core/day`):** Condenses vapor against the `7°C` laboratory chilled-water loop (`+76.00 We` TEG/pyroelectric/VGT recovery) to return `<0.05 ppm TDS` sterile Water-for-Injection (WFI) back to facility autoclaves, leaving `<0.15%` inert, vitrified mineral ash.
