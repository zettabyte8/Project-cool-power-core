## 1. Core Architectural Breakthrough
The **Cool Power Core™ (CPC)** resolves the primary thermodynamic penalty of plasma-assisted water dissociation and hydrocarbon/contaminant mineralization by uniting **waste-heat enthalpy recovery** with **graphene-enhanced hydrodynamic vortex cavitation**, **33.6 kV nanosecond pulsed ZVS plasma**, and a **50 Hz 30:1 opposed-piston ceramic linear alternator**:

* **Thermal & Cavitation Offload:** Capturing **382.93 Wth/core** (`43.93 Wth` sensible preheating to `90°C` + `339.00 Wth` latent vaporization) lowers water surface tension and raises vapor pressure to `70.1 kPa`, cutting ultrasonic/vortex cavitation power by **38–53%** (`30.0 W` down to `14.2–18.5 W`).
* **33.6 kV Nanosecond Plasma & 25,000+ Hour Durability:** A `4.2 V / 20 W` ZVS driver feeding a `1000x` MnZn ferrite U-core transformer and 8-stage Cockcroft-Walton multiplier generates non-equilibrium `⋅OH / H⋅ / O₃` radicals inside a CVD graphene-coated fluidic vortex amplifier, paired with `5.0 µm` oil-free air bearings, `8YSZ` liners, and argon aerogel shielding (`<100°C` at the `N52H` radial magnets).
* **Multi-Modal Cogeneration:** Combines radial `N52H` linear alternators, a micro Tesla biomimetic turbine, a variable-geometry turbocharger, `Bi₂Te₃`/pyroelectric harvesters, `PZT` exhaust pulse transducers, and an electrostatic ion grid to export **+286.4 W to +364.5 W** net continuous electricity per core while distilling pure water (`<5 ppm TDS`).
