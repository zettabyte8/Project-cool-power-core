## SPECIFICATION PARAGRAPHS (`[0155]` – `[0164]`)

**[0155]** This Continuation-in-Part disclosure extends the closed-loop Cool Power Core™ (`CPC`) architecture (`CPC-PAT-2026-001` through `011-CIP`) to personal powered exoskeleton armor (`CPC-PA-MK3`) capable of simultaneously attenuating and harvesting energy from both hypervelocity ballistic impacts and directed-energy weapon (`DEW`) strikes, and routing the harvested electrical and cryogenic energy into a magazine-free tri-mode atmospheric-condensing return-fire system (`CPC-RIFLE-ATMOS`).

**[0156]** Referring to **FIG. 24**, the composite armor wall (`2400`) comprises four integrated functional strata totaling `36.0 mm` in thickness: (a) an outer dielectric Bragg mirror (`2402`, `92.4%` reflectance at `1,064 nm`) bonded over hexagonal Boron Carbide (`B₄C`) ceramic strike tiles (`2404`, `NIJ Level IV+`); (b) a wide-bandgap `4H-SiC` thermophotovoltaic (`TPV`) array and `Bi₂Te₃` micro-thermoelectric generator (`TEG`) stratum (`2406`) converting incident DEW radiation and ceramic strike-face heating (`450°C–900°C`) directly into `48 V DC` electrical current (`+640 We` to `+1,850 We` surge); (c) an `18.0 mm` open-cell viscoelastic polyurethane memory-foam kinetic absorption layer (`2408`, restitution coefficient `e ≤ 0.035`) embedded with a 3D piezoelectric `PVDF-TrFE` transducer lattice (`2410`), attenuating `>180 g` ballistic shock impulses to `<14.5 g` while converting `96.8%` of projectile kinetic energy into harvestable piezo-electric pulses (`+1,180 J/strike`) and low-grade thermal energy; and (d) an inner `37.0°C` `Ti-6Al-4V` micro-channel cooling liner (`2412`) coupled to a dorsal wearable Cool Power Core™ (`2414`).

**[0157]** The dorsal Cool Power Core™ (`2414`) bridges metabolic heat (`37.0°C`) and armor-harvested thermal energy against a `90.0°C` CVD-graphene hydrodynamic cavitation chamber (`2416`) and `33.6 kV` ZVS nanosecond pulsed plasma exciter (`2418`), generating `+198.4 We` to `+246.0 We` continuous baseline power (`480–720 hr` sealed autonomy) and delivering harvested ballistic/DEW surge power into a `420 Wh` (`4.8 kW` pulse) graphene-CNT supercapacitor bank (`2420`) and a `-78.0°C` (`195 K`) Peltier/Brayton cryogenic cold bus (`2422`).

**[0158]** Referring to **FIG. 25**, the return-fire system (`2500`, `4.85 kg`) connects via an armored umbilical (`2502`) to the `48 V DC` supercapacitor bus (`2420`) and `-78.0°C` cryogenic bus (`2422`). In Atmospheric-Condensing Projectile Mode, an `18,000 RPM` radial cyclone intake (`2504`) draws ambient gases (`CO₂`, `H₂O`, `N₂`) and suspended atmospheric particulates/regolith dust (`2506`) into a `-78.0°C` cryogenic condensation and sintering mold (`2508`), where a `33.6 kV` ZVS plasma discharge (`2510`) fuses the condensed cryosolids and particulates into a `14.5 g` (`2.45 g/cm³`, `Ø 8.5 mm`) solid composite slug (`2512`).

**[0159]** A breech actuator (`2514`) indexes the solidified atmospheric slug (`2512`) into a `620 mm` multi-stage linear induction coilgun barrel (`2516`) energized sequentially by the graphene-CNT supercapacitor bank (`2420`), accelerating the slug to `1,420 m/s` (`14.62 kJ` muzzle energy) without chemical propellants or metallic cartridges.

**[0160]** A rotary breech selector (`2518`) alternatively switches the system to: (i) Magneto-Plasma Bolt Mode, wherein `33.6 kV` ZVS plasma (`2510`) ionizes condensed atmospheric gas into a `4,800 K` toroidal plasmoid confined by a `0.55 T` `Sm₂Co₁₇` Halbach nozzle (`2520`) and launched at `1,850 m/s`; or (ii) Pulsed Laser Mode, wherein supercapacitor discharge drives a `1,064 nm` diode-pumped Yb:YAG rod (`2522`) cooled by the `-78.0°C` cryogenic bus (`2422`) to emit `10 Hz` high-brightness optical pulses through a sapphire exit window (`2524`).

## PATENT CLAIMS (`CLAIMS 68–73` | `37 CFR § 1.75` & `PCT RULE 6.2(b)`)

**Claim 68 (Independent).** A kinetic- and directed-energy-harvesting exoskeleton armor system `(2400)`, comprising:
(a) an outer strike face comprising a dielectric Bragg mirror `(2402)` disposed over Boron Carbide (`B₄C`) ceramic tiles `(2404)`;
(b) a thermal-to-electric conversion stratum `(2406)` comprising `4H-SiC` thermophotovoltaic cells and `Bi₂Te₃` thermoelectric generators optically and thermally coupled to the inner surface of the ceramic tiles `(2404)`;
(c) an open-cell viscoelastic memory-foam kinetic absorption layer `(2408)` having a restitution coefficient `e ≤ 0.035` and an embedded piezoelectric `PVDF-TrFE` lattice `(2410)` positioned behind the conversion stratum `(2406)`;
(d) a `Ti-6Al-4V` micro-channel cooling liner `(2412)` thermally coupled to a wearable closed-loop Cool Power Core™ `(2414)` operating a `90°C` CVD-graphene cavitation chamber `(2416)` and a `33.6 kV` ZVS plasma exciter `(2418)`; and
(e) a graphene-CNT supercapacitor bank `(2420)` storing electrical energy harvested from ballistic piezoelectric compression, viscoelastic dissipative heating, and directed-energy thermophotovoltaic conversion.

**Claim 69 (Dependent).** The armor system `(2400)` of claim 68, wherein the viscoelastic memory-foam layer `(2408)` attenuates hypervelocity ballistic impacts exceeding `180 g` down to `<14.5 g` at the cooling liner `(2412)` while recovering at least `96%` of incident kinetic energy as combined piezoelectric pulses and harvestable thermal enthalpy.

**Claim 70 (Dependent).** The armor system `(2400)` of claim 68, wherein the dielectric Bragg mirror `(2402)` reflects at least `92%` of incident `1,064 nm` laser radiation while the `4H-SiC` thermophotovoltaic and `Bi₂Te₃` stratum `(2406)` generates between `+640 We` and `+1,850 We` of surge electrical power during sustained directed-energy irradiation.

**Claim 71 (Independent).** An atmospheric-condensing tri-mode return-fire system `(2500)` powered by the supercapacitor bank `(2420)` and a `-78°C` cryogenic bus `(2422)` of the armor system `(2400)` of claim 68, comprising:
(a) a centrifugal cyclone intake `(2504)` configured to draw ambient atmospheric gases and suspended particulates `(2506)` into a `-78°C` cryogenic condensation mold `(2508)`;
(b) a `33.6 kV` ZVS plasma sintering electrode `(2510)` configured to fuse condensed atmospheric gases and particulates within the mold `(2508)` into a solid composite projectile `(2512)`; and
(c) a multi-stage linear induction coilgun barrel `(2516)` energized by the supercapacitor bank `(2420)` to accelerate the solid composite projectile `(2512)` to hypervelocity without chemical propellants.

**Claim 72 (Dependent).** The return-fire system `(2500)` of claim 71, further comprising a rotary breech selector `(2518)` that selectively diverts supercapacitor power and condensed atmospheric gas to a `0.55 T` `Sm₂Co₁₇` Halbach magnetic nozzle `(2520)` to launch `4,800 K` toroidal plasma bolts at `1,850 m/s`.

**Claim 73 (Dependent).** The return-fire system `(2500)` of claim 71, wherein the rotary breech selector `(2518)` selectively energizes a cryogenically cooled `1,064 nm` Yb:YAG laser resonator `(2522)` to emit `10 Hz` pulsed directed-energy return fire through a sapphire aperture `(2524)`.
