## Part I — Tiny-Home & Backyard ADU Builder CAD Rough-In & MEP Installation Manual (`CPC-RES-1` / `CPC-RES-2`)

The **`CPC-RES-1`** (single-core, 34.5 kg) and **`CPC-RES-2`** (dual-core, 58.0 kg) NEMA 4X exterior utility pods eliminate septic tanks, leach fields, municipal water hookups, and noisy standby generators for tiny-home OEMs, prefab builders, and backyard ADU developers.

### 1.1 Dimensional & Structural Pad Requirements
- **`CPC-RES-1` Envelope:** `680 mm (W) × 480 mm (D) × 760 mm (H)` | Dry Mass: `34.5 kg` | Acoustic Output: `34 dBA @ 1 m`.
- **`CPC-RES-2` Envelope:** `880 mm (W) × 540 mm (D) × 840 mm (H)` | Dry Mass: `58.0 kg` | Acoustic Output: `36 dBA @ 1 m`.
- **Foundation Pad:** 100 mm reinforced concrete slab or galvanized steel trailer tongue cradle; anchor with 4× M10 316SS wedge anchors. Maintain **300 mm** rear condenser airflow clearance and **600 mm** front service access.

### 1.3 Commissioning & Builder Acceptance Sequence
1. **Pressure & Leak Verification:** Hydro-test `MEP-03` potable cold and `MEP-04` DHW PEX-A loops at `690 kPa (100 psi)` for 15 minutes prior to energizing.
2. **Thermal Reservoir Priming:** Fill internal vacuum-insulated reservoir with `4.5 L` (`RES-1`) or `9.0 L` (`RES-2`) clean water; solar-thermal/heat-pump preheater brings reservoir to **90°C** within 14 minutes.
3. **33.6 kV ZVS & 50 Hz Air-Bearing Startup:** Verify `5.0 µm` porous air-bearing lift pressure and initiate `4.2 V / 20 W` ZVS nanosecond plasma cavitation. Confirm `<1 ppt` PFAS/TDS distillate purity via onboard conductivity sensor before opening potable distribution valve.
