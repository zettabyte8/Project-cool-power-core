### Detailed Description of Embodiments (`FIGS. 14 & 15`)
**[0056]** Referring to **FIG. 14**, a Deep-Sea Hydrothermal Plasma Torch (`CPC-TORCH-ABYSS`) is enclosed within a pressure-rated Ti-6Al-4V Grade 5 housing (`1400`) certified for continuous operation at depths up to `6,000 m` (`60.8 MPa` hydrostatic ambient pressure). External seawater cold-sink fins (`1402`) thermally couple a Bi₂Te₃/skutterudite thermoelectric and PMN-PT pyroelectric generator array (`1404`) directly to `2.0°C` abyssal seawater. A `90°C` water reservoir (`1406`) feeds a CVD-graphene-lined vortex cavitator (`1408`) and a `33.6 kV` zero-voltage-switching (ZVS) nanosecond plasma exciter paired with a `220 Wh` graphene-CNT supercapacitor bank (`1410`). A recuperative arc-shroud heat-return loop (`1412`) captures `+382.93 Wth` of sensible and latent enthalpy from the nozzle jacket to maintain the `90°C` reservoir without external heating, yielding `+196.4 We` of continuous thermoelectric regeneration across the `88.0 K` thermal gradient (`ΔT`). A `0.75 T` hybrid Halbach-Bitter magnetic nozzle (`1414`) collimates a `4.2 kW`, `6,800 K` hydro-sheathed plasma jet (`1416`) fed via a `60.8 MPa` stepped-orifice seawater filter (`1418`).

**[0057]** Referring to **FIG. 15**, an Asteroid Microgravity Zero-Recoil Plasma Rock-Splitter (`CPC-SPLIT-AST`) comprises an ultra-lightweight chassis (`1500`) fitted with surface-anchoring grapples for operation in `10⁻⁹ Pa` vacuum and `10⁻⁵ g` microgravity. A `120 K` sun-shaded deep-space radiator and thermoelectric/pyroelectric converter (`1502`) establishes a `243.15 K` thermal gradient (`ΔT`) against a `90°C` (`363.15 K`) closed-loop CVD-graphene vortex cavitator and `33.6 kV` ZVS plasma exciter (`1504`), generating `+184.0 We` of continuous electrical regeneration. A `0.58 T` magnetic-mirror needle emitter (`1506`) projects a `3.2 kW`, `5,400 K` focused plasma beam onto carbonaceous chondrite regolith (`1510`), flash-vaporizing bound phyllosilicate water (`8–12 wt%`) to generate `45–90 MPa` of internal pore-pressure tensile spallation while imparting less than `0.15 N` of axial reaction recoil. A coaxial volatile aspiration hood and cryogenic condenser (`1508`) captures liberated `H₂O`, `CO₂`, and `Ni(CO)₄` carbonyl vapors to replenish the closed-loop working fluid (`>100%` fluid self-sufficiency).

## PART II: SUPPLEMENTAL PATENT CLAIMS (`CLAIMS 38–43`)

* **Claim 38 (Independent):** A high-pressure subsea plasma cutting and welding system (`CPC-TORCH-ABYSS`), comprising: (a) a Ti-6Al-4V pressure housing (`1400`) rated for hydrostatic pressures up to `60.8 MPa` (`6,000 m` depth) having external cold-sink fins (`1402`) in thermal contact with ambient abyssal seawater; (b) a closed-loop `90°C` water reservoir (`1406`) feeding a CVD-graphene-coated hydrodynamic vortex cavitator (`1408`) and a `33.6 kV` nanosecond pulsed plasma exciter (`1410`); (c) a thermoelectric and pyroelectric generator array (`1404`) bridged between the `90°C` reservoir (`1406`) and the cold-sink fins (`1402`) to continuously recharge an onboard graphene supercapacitor (`1410`); and (d) a `0.75 T` magnetic nozzle (`1414`) ejecting a `6,800 K` hydro-sheathed plasma jet (`1416`) while a recuperative shroud (`1412`) recycles arc waste heat back to the `90°C` reservoir (`1406`).
* **Claim 39 (Dependent on Claim 38):** The subsea plasma system of Claim 38, wherein the stepped-orifice seawater intake (`1418`) feeds dissociated `H₂/O₂` working gas into the `0.75 T` magnetic nozzle (`1414`) at `61.5 MPa` without an umbilical surface power tether.
* **Claim 40 (Dependent on Claim 38):** The subsea plasma system of Claim 38, wherein the thermoelectric and pyroelectric array (`1404`) generates at least `196.4 We` of continuous charging power across a `2.0°C` seawater cold sink and `90°C` internal reservoir.
* **Claim 41 (Independent):** A microgravity plasma rock-spallation and volatile-harvesting apparatus (`CPC-SPLIT-AST`), comprising: (a) a low-recoil chassis (`1500`) operable in `10⁻⁹ Pa` vacuum; (b) a `90°C` closed-loop CVD-graphene vortex cavitator and `33.6 kV` nanosecond plasma exciter (`1504`) thermally coupled via a thermoelectric/pyroelectric array to a `120 K` shaded deep-space radiator (`1502`); (c) a `0.58 T` magnetic-mirror needle emitter (`1506`) directing a `5,400 K` plasma beam onto volatile-bearing regolith (`1510`) to induce `45–90 MPa` internal phyllosilicate steam spallation at `<0.15 N` axial recoil; and (d) a coaxial aspiration hood and condenser (`1508`) recovering liberated `H₂O`, `CO₂`, and metal carbonyl vapors into the closed-loop reservoir.
* **Claim 42 (Dependent on Claim 41):** The microgravity plasma apparatus of Claim 41, wherein the coaxial aspiration hood (`1508`) recovers greater than `100%` of the working fluid mass consumed during plasma operation, providing net water surplus for orbital propellant depots.
* **Claim 43 (Dependent on Claim 41):** The microgravity plasma apparatus of Claim 41, wherein the thermoelectric/pyroelectric array (`1502`) harvests `+184.0 We` across the `243.15 K` differential between the `90°C` reservoir and `120 K` radiator.
