## 1. Executive System Overview

The **`CPC-PA-MK1` ("Aegis-Vault")** is a full-body, modular, powered exoskeleton armor system engineered around a dorsal-mounted **Cool Power Core™ (`µCPC-EXO-PA`, `3.10 kg`)** closed-loop metabolic and actuator waste-heat harvesting powerplant. Inspired by the iconic modular frame-and-plate architecture of *Fallout*-class power armor—where a powered mechanical inner chassis accepts tool-free, mission-configurable outer armor plates—`CPC-PA-MK1` solves the two historic barriers to real-world powered armor: **continuous endurance** and **blunt-force / blast-impulse trauma**.

1. **Self-Sustaining Closed-Loop Power (`360–720 hr` Endurance):** Instead of heavy lithium battery packs that deplete in 45–90 minutes, `CPC-PA-MK1` harvests **`+110 to +165 Wth` of human metabolic body heat** (`37.0°C` undersuit liquid cooling garment) combined with **`+185 to +290 Wth` of QDD actuator and hydraulic-servo waste heat** (`+295 to +455 Wth` total) to preheat its closed-loop water reservoir to **`90.0°C`**, generating **`+198.4 to +246.0 We` continuous DC power** (`3,200 We` graphene supercapacitor burst) alongside **`+0.28 L/hr` sterile drinking water** and **`+145 g/hr` medical/breathing `O₂`**.
2. **NASA Open-Cell Viscoelastic Memory-Foam Kinetic Absorption Layer (`TEMPUR`-Class Non-Elastic Damping):** Between the rigid outer ballistic/radiation armor plates and the structural Ti-6Al-4V exoskeleton frame sits a **`28 mm` multi-density open-cell viscoelastic polyurethane-graphene lining**—directly derived from the slow-recovery temper foam originally developed at **NASA Ames Research Center** (1966) for aircraft crash protection and later commercialized globally in sleeping mattresses (famous for absorbing a dropped bowling ball with **zero rebound/bounce**).

## 2. Structural Exoskeleton Frame & Tool-Free Modular Armor Architecture

Just like a tactical power-armor frame, the **`CPC-PA-MK1` Inner Chassis (`34.5 kg`)** is a self-supporting, anthropomorphic Ti-6Al-4V / Carbon-PEEK mechanical skeleton that bears **100% of the outer armor's weight** directly to the ground through articulated foot stirrups. The operator steps in through a **rear-clamshell hydraulic/servo dorsal hatch** that seals shut in **`3.8 seconds`**.

### 2.1 Inner Powered Exoskeleton Frame (`18-DOF` Active Actuation)
* **Joint Architecture:** `18` liquid-cooled Quasi-Direct-Drive (QDD) rotary actuators (`hip 3-DOF × 2`, `knee 1-DOF × 2`, `ankle 2-DOF × 2`, `shoulder 3-DOF × 2`, `elbow 1-DOF × 2`, `lumbar spine 2-DOF`) delivering **`185 N·m` peak torque** at the hips/knees and **`95 N·m`** at the shoulders/elbows.
* **Strength & Mobility Amplification:**
  * **Sustained Carry Capacity:** **`185.0 kg` (`408 lb`)** of armor, heavy tools, or rescued personnel with near-zero perceived weight to the wearer.
  * **Sprint & Vertical Leap:** `26 km/h` (`16.2 mph`) assisted sprint; `1.45 m` (`4.8 ft`) vertical jump; `12.0 m` (`39 ft`) impact-free drop survival.
  * **Fail-Safe Mechanical Clutching:** Zero-power magnetic passive locks prevent joint collapse if a limb sustains catastrophic damage.

### 2.2 Six Tool-Free Interchangeable Outer Armor Modules (`90-Second Swap`)
Outer armor shells lock onto standardized **QD-LOC (`Quick-Disconnect Locking`) dove-tail hardpoints** across six body zones (`Helmet/Cowl`, `Torso Cuirass`, `Left Arm/Pauldron`, `Right Arm/Pauldron`, `Left Leg/Greave`, `Right Leg/Greave`). ## 3. NASA Viscoelastic Kinetic-Absorption Layer (Zero-Bounce Impact Physics)

In conventional rigid armor, stopping a high-velocity projectile, explosion shockwave, or multi-story fall still transmits lethal **Behind-Armor Blunt Trauma (BABT)** and **acceleration shock (`>150 g`)** into the wearer's skeleton and internal organs. `CPC-PA-MK1` eliminates rebound shock using a **3-stage graded kinetic-damping liner** bonded inside every outer armor module:

1. **Stage 1 — Shear-Thickening Fluid (STF) Kevlar Mid-Layer (`4 mm`):** Silica nanoparticles suspended in polyethylene glycol remain flexible during normal movement (`<10 s⁻¹` strain rate) but instantaneously lock into a rigid solid (`<0.4 ms`) upon ballistic or shrapnel impact, spreading point loads across **`450 cm²`**.
2. **Stage 2 — NASA-Derived Open-Cell Viscoelastic Memory-Foam Core (`28 mm`, `85 kg/m³` Density):** Formulated from temperature-stabilized open-cell polyurethane infused with **multi-walled carbon nanotubes (MWCNTs)** to eliminate cold-weather stiffening (`-40°C` to `+65°C`).
   * **Zero-Rebound Energy Dissipation (`Restitution Coefficient e ≤ 0.04`):** Just as a `7.26 kg` (`16 lb`) bowling ball dropped onto a NASA memory-foam pad compresses the open cellular network and comes to a dead stop with **zero bounce**, pneumatic airflow through the tortuous micro-pores converts **96.2% of kinetic impact energy into low-grade heat** (`+15 to +45 Wth` during sustained impacts/running), which is immediately harvested by the suit's liquid cooling loop.
   * **Peak G-Force Attenuation:** Reduces a `12.0 m` (`39 ft`) free-fall landing or `155 mm` artillery blast overpressure wave from **`>180 g` down to `<14.5 g`** at the wearer's sternum and spine—well below human concussion or internal-injury thresholds.
3. **Stage 3 — Phase-Change Micro-Channel Cooling Undersuit (`4 mm`):** Circulation tubes wick both human metabolic heat (`37.0°C`) and damped kinetic heat directly into the dorsal **Cool Power Core™** `90.0°C` heat-pump/cavitation loop, keeping the wearer at a comfortable **`21.5°C` (`71°F`)** skin temperature inside a sealed suit.
