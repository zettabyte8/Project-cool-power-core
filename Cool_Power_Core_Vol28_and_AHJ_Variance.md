## Part I: Municipal Building-Department & Plumbing Inspector Variance Submittal Packet (`CPC-RES-1` / `CPC-RES-2`)

### 1. Executive Code-Equivalence Statement (IPC Sec. 105.2 / UPC Sec. 301.3)
This submittal packet establishes engineering, sanitary, electrical, and fire-safety equivalence for the **`CPC-RES-1`** (`+286.0 We` | `216 L/day`) and **`CPC-RES-2`** (`+580.0 We` | `432 L/day`) closed-loop residential utility pods to replace conventional onsite septic tanks, drainfields, and municipal sewer/water lateral connections on residential, tiny-home, and Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) parcels.

### 2. Core Sanitary & Fail-Safe Architecture
- **3-Stage Thermal & Plasma Mineralization:** Household greywater and blackwater pass through a `50-micron` solids cyclone, a **90°C** preheated reservoir, a **CVD graphene fluidic vortex cavitator**, and a **33.6 kV nanosecond pulsed plasma reactor** (`3,000°C` opposed-piston oxidation), achieving **>6-Log (`99.9999%`) pathogen inactivation** and complete mineralization of PFAS, pharmaceuticals, and organic BOD.
- **Physical Air-Gap Cross-Connection Isolation (ASSE 1013 / IPC Sec. 608):** Distilled water vapor is condensed across an isolated stainless-steel heat exchanger into a dedicated `55°C` potable tank via a **50 mm physical air gap** and dual Reduced Pressure Principle (RPZ) backflow preventers.
- **110% Secondary Containment & Auto-Shutoff:** An integrated leak-tight NEMA 4X pan with redundant ultrasonic level sensors automatically closes the motorized municipal/cistern makeup solenoid valve prior to any overflow condition.
- **Inert Class A EQ Mineral Residue:** Non-volatile minerals (calcium, potassium, silica) precipitate as a sterile, dry mineral cake (`<4.2 kg/quarter`) certified under **EPA 40 CFR Part 503 Class A Exceptional Quality**.
