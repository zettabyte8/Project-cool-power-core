## 1. Case `#1,856`: Subterranean Martian Lava-Tube Luxury Resort & Industrial Metropolis (`CPC-MARS-RIDGE-50K`)

### 1.1 Geological Shielding & Sunlit Canyon Terracing
* **Site Geometry:** Constructed inside an intact `450 m` wide × `18.0 km` long Arsia/Pavonis Mons volcanic lava tube beneath **`55 m` of dense basalt overburden**, attenuating Galactic Cosmic Rays (GCR) and Solar Particle Events (SPE) by **98.6% (`<12.5 mSv/yr`)**.

* **Sub-Floor Industrial & `CPC-LOOP` Spine (`-48 m` Below Tube Floor):** Houses zero-gravity/0.38g metallurgy foundries, Regolith-to-Graphene/CNT additive factories, and an `850 km/h` evacuated **`CPC-LOOP`** maglev line linking Olympus, Tharsis, and Hellas domes.

### 1.2 185,000-Core Closed-Loop Life-Support & ISRU Performance
* **Thermodynamic Cycle:** Bridges `95°C–180°C` industrial/reactor waste heat (`382.93 Wth/core`) and secondary cosmic-ray/betavoltaic flux (`+18.4 We/core`) against **`-60°C` Martian surface cryo-radiators (`ΔT = 155–240 K`)**.
* **Net Outputs:**
  * **Gross / Net Power:** **`+67.49 MWe`** gross (`+364.80 We/core`) | **`+26.29 MWe`** net exportable surplus to external rover/mining grids.
  * **Closed-Loop Water & Oxygen:** **`14,850,000 L/day`** (`99.85%` loop closure + subsurface ice mining) and **`56,400 kg/day`** breathable `O₂` (`128%` colony demand).
  * **Regolith Detoxification & Materials:** Converts toxic Martian soil perchlorates (`ClO₄⁻`) and atmospheric `CO₂/N₂` via `33.6 kV` nanosecond plasma into **`18,200 kg/day` ammonium nitrate fertilizer** and **`12,400 kg/day` structural carbon nanotubes/graphene**.
  * **Economics:** `$42.50B` turnkey CapEx | **`+$6.45B/yr`** EBITDA (orbital tourism, propellant export, rare-earth/platinum refining) | **`6.59-year`** payback | **`$258.00M/yr`** inventor IP royalties (`4.0%`).

## 2. Case `#1,857`: Floating Oceanic Zero-Brine Desalination & Mineral-Harvesting Archipelago (`CPC-OCEAN-ARCH-250K`)

* **Site Geometry:** **36 interconnected Category-5 (`190 mph`) pre-tensioned CFRP/marine-concrete spar atolls** (`1,440 hectares` total footprint) moored along subtropical oceanic gyre crossroads, interconnected by `-180 m` submerged **`CPC-TRANSIT-OCEAN`** (`920 km/h`) maglev tubes.
* **Climate-Controlled Sunlit Atoll Rings:** Each atoll features a stepped `24°` artificial reef-hill profile covered in **`2.5 m` of living mangrove, kelp-compost, and tropical orchard terraces** beneath an automated **ETFE pneumatic storm canopy (`21.5°C / 48% RH`)**, giving all **82,500 residences and resort villas** sunlit ocean-view garden balconies above a `14-level` submerged commerce, marine-biotech, and yacht-marina core.

### 2.2 360,000-Core Zero-Brine Desalination & Critical Mineral Harvest
* **Thermodynamic Cycle:** Bridges `90°C–145°C` solar-thermal and industrial process heat (`382.93 Wth/core`) against **`3.5°C` deep-ocean abyssal water (`ΔT = 86.5–141.5 K`)** with thermal discharge strictly capped at **`≤ +0.08 K` at `1.0 m`**.
* **Net Outputs:**
  * **Gross / Net Power:** **`+126.65 MWe`** gross (`+351.80 We/core`) | **`+42.55 MWe`** net exportable surplus (charging autonomous cargo fleets and green ammonia tankers).
  * **Zero-Brine Pure Water:** **`84,500,000 L/day` (`22.32M gal/day`)** of `<1 ppm TDS` distilled water—supplying `100%` of archipelago demand plus **`52.0M L/day`** exported via subsea pipeline/tanker to coastal drought regions with **zero hypersaline brine discharge**.
  * **Critical Marine Minerals & Ocean Cleanup:** Extracts **`4,850 kg/day` battery-grade `Li₂CO₃`**, **`28,400 kg/day` `Mg(OH)₂` & carbon-negative marine cement**, and **`42.5 kg/day` amidoxime `U₃O₈`**, while plasma-mineralizing **`64.0 TPD`** of harvested ocean gyre microplastics and continental-shelf methane seeps.
  * **Economics:** `$54.80B` turnkey CapEx | **`+$8.92B/yr`** EBITDA | **`6.14-year`** payback | **`$356.80M/yr`** inventor IP royalties (`4.0%`).
