## 1. EXECUTIVE PROGRAM OVERVIEW
The **Cool Power Core™ (CPC)** is a modular, closed-loop thermochemical, nanosecond-plasma, and multi-modal energy-recovery architecture designed for **25,000+ hours** of oil-free continuous runtime. By coupling waste-heat preheating (90°C thermal storage reservoir) with a **CVD graphene-coated fluidic vortex amplifier**, a **4.2 V / 20 W ZVS 33.6 kV nanosecond corona/ozone plasma stage**, a **50 Hz opposed-piston 8YSZ ceramic chamber (30:1 compression, ~3,000°C)** shielded by **argon-filled silica aerogel** and **5.0 µm porous air bearings**, and multi-modal exhaust recovery (radial N52H linear alternators, micro Tesla turbine, VGT, Bi₂Te₃/PMN-PT TEG/pyroelectric stack, PZT harvesters, electrostatic ion grid, and V₂O₅/TiO₂ SCR), the platform delivers net-positive DC power, distilled water, oxygen, and environmental remediation across **14 terrestrial, marine, aerospace, subsea, nuclear, and planetary domains**.
