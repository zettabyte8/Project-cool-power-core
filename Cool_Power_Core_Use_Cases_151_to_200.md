## 1. Executive Summary
This engineering addendum expands the **Cool Power Core™ (CPC)** application library from 150 to **200 distinct commercial, industrial, energy, mobility, defense, and deep-frontier deployments (`#151–#200`)**. Across every sector, the core architecture leverages **waste-heat preheating (`90°C`)**, **CVD graphene vortex cavitation**, **`33.6 kV` nanosecond pulsed plasma dissociation**, **`3,000°C` opposed-piston thermal mineralization**, and **multi-modal cogeneration** to convert thermal and chemical waste streams into net DC electricity, distilled water, and oxygen.

## 2. Engineering Deep-Dives on High-Impact New Sectors

### A. Food & Beverage Processing (`CPC-FOOD`, Cases `#151–#160`)
* **Target Bottleneck:** Breweries, distilleries, dairy spray-dryers, and edible-oil refineries vent massive quantities of `95°C–180°C` moist vapor laden with volatile organic compounds (VOCs), dimethyl sulfide (DMS), hexane, and high-BOD stillage.
* **CPC Integration:** `CPC-FOOD` skids ingest `95°C–100°C` kettle/evaporator vapor directly into the `90°C` thermal reservoir (**zero electrical preheat penalty**, saving `43.93 W/core`), oxidize VOC/odor plumes in the `33.6 kV` corona/O₃ stage, and recover **98% sterile Clean-in-Place (CIP) hot water** alongside **+308 to +334 W/core** net DC electricity (**1.32–1.60 yr payback**).

### B. Heavy Ceramics, Glass, Asphalt & Carbon Black (`CPC-IND / CPC-PETRO`, Cases `#161–#170`)
* **Target Bottleneck:** Tunnel kilns, fiberglass curing ovens, hot-mix asphalt drums, and carbon-black reactors emit `250°C–700°C` exhaust contaminated with HF, SO₂, phenol-formaldehyde resins, polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs), and low-BTU tail gas.
* **CPC Integration:** High-temperature exhaust drives maximum TEG/pyroelectric and micro-Tesla turbine cogeneration (**+335.0 to +344.5 W/core**), while `3,000°C` opposed-piston combustion and V₂O₅/TiO₂ SCR scrubbers mineralize PAHs and acid gases into inert calcium salts and elemental sulfur (**1.26–1.34 yr payback**).

### C. Grid Storage, CSP Molten Salt & Orphan Wellheads (`CPC-GRID / CPC-PETRO`, Cases `#171–#180`)
* **Target Bottleneck:** Concentrated Solar Power (CSP) molten-salt tanks (`565°C`), biomass gasifiers (`naphthalene tars`), SF₆-insulated switchgear (`GWP = 23,500`), and millions of leaking orphan oil/gas wellheads suffer from parasitic thermal losses and fugitive greenhouse-gas leaks.
* **CPC Integration:** `CPC-GRID` modules crack `SF₆` into inert `CaF₂/CaSO₄`, eliminate biomass gasifier tars via `33.6 kV` nanosecond plasma, and deploy as solar-bootstrapped autonomous skids at orphan wellheads that oxidize fugitive `CH₄` (`GWP = 84`) to power continuous valve telemetry.
