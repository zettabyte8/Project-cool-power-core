# Project Cool Power Core

**A quiet, self-powered air purifier and water harvester.** Destroys VOCs, CO, O₃, NO₂ and PM by plasma-thermal oxidation, condenses drinking water out of the airstream, and makes enough electricity to run itself with a small surplus dumped as light and airflow.

Designed by Thomas G. Coleman. Engineered on Venice.ai.

## Priority order

1. Clean air
2. Water
3. Quiet
4. Life
5. Cost
6. Electricity — last, deliberately. Grid suppliers are not the customer.

## Status

Design. **All figures modelled, none measured.** Gate G1 (dissociation fraction) decides the power column and has not been run.

## Consumables, plainly

Water −1.4 L/day. One mineral cartridge a year. One softener per 250 h. Ash drawer annually. No DEF. No air filters. No oil.
