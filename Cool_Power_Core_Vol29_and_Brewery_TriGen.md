## Part 1: Craft & Macro Brewery / Distillery Zero-Sewer Tri-Gen Skid (`CPC-BEV-1351` | Cases `#1,351` & `#1,352`)

### 1.1 Closed-Loop Process & Thermodynamic Architecture
Breweries and distilleries simultaneously discharge **100°C brew-kettle / distillation stillage vapors**, high-BOD spent-wash effluent (`35,000–85,000 mg/L COD`), and **>99% pure biogenic fermentation CO₂**, while purchasing municipal water, grid electricity, food-grade CO₂ for carbonation/purging, and paying high-strength municipal sewer surcharges.

1. **Waste-Heat Enthalpy Offload (`382.93 Wth/core`):** Condensing `100°C` brew-kettle boil-off and column stillage vapors preheats incoming high-BOD washwater to **90°C** (`43.93 Wth/core` sensible + `339.00 Wth/core` latent vaporization offset), slashing CVD graphene vortex cavitation power to **15.8 W/core**.
2. **6-Log Organic & Stillage Mineralization (`33.6 kV ZVS` + `3,000°C` Opposed-Piston Core):** Nanosecond pulsed corona plasma (`4.2 V / 20 W` driver) and `30:1` compression thermal oxidation completely crack ethanol/fusel-oil VOCs, proteins, and organic acids into sterile **<2 ppm TDS brewing distillate** and a **<1.2% dry mineral/potassium-phosphate ash** (sold as organic fertilizer), eliminating municipal sewer discharge.
3. **Glycol Chiller Cold-Sink Cogeneration (`+334.0 We/core`):** Bridging the `90°C–3,000°C` core against the brewery's existing **-4°C to +2°C fermentation glycol chiller loop** boosts `Bi₂Te₃/PMN-PT` thermoelectric and linear alternator output to **+334.0 We/core** (`+167.0 kWe` on a 500-core regional brewery skid).
4. **Biogenic CO₂ Polishing & Sterile O₂ Wort Aeration:** Fermentation off-gas is scrubbed through the `33.6 kV` ozone/plasma stage to remove dimethyl sulfide (DMS) and acetaldehyde, yielding beverage-grade **99.95% CO₂** alongside **USP-grade O₂** for yeast pitching aeration.
