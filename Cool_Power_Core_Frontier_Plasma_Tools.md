## 1. DEEP-SEA HYDROTHERMAL PLASMA TORCH (`CPC-TORCH-ABYSS`)

### 1.1 Operating Environment & Thermodynamic Coupling
* **Depth & Ambient Conditions:** `6,000 m` abyssal seafloor (`60.8 MPa` hydrostatic pressure, `2.0°C` ambient seawater sink) or `350°C` black-smoker hydrothermal vent proximity.
* **Thermal & Fluidic Loop:** Ingests ambient seawater (`35,000 ppm TDS`) through a `Ti-6Al-4V` micro-desalination pre-stage heated to `90°C` via internal arc-shroud recuperation (`+382.93 Wth`), while rejecting condenser heat directly into the **`2.0°C` abyssal seawater sink** (`+196.4 We` continuous self-recharging output).
* **Hydro-Sheathed Arc Physics:** A high-velocity coaxial `H₂O/O₂` vapor sheath stabilizes a **`6,800 K`, `4.2 kW`** transferred/non-transferred plasma jet (`14.5 cm` standoff) confined by a `0.75 T` `Sm₂Co₁₇`/Bitter magnetic nozzle, cutting `50 mm` HY-100 submarine hull steel or polymetallic seafloor sulfide nodules at `28 cm/min` without surface umbilical cables.

### 1.2 Subsea Specifications & Duty Cycle
* **Subsea Mass / Buoyancy:** `4.85 kg` dry (`1.90 kg` neutrally trimmed with syntactic foam collar); ROV manipulator T-handle or abyssal hard-suit wrist mount.
* **Energy Buffer:** `220 Wh` solid-state graphene-CNT supercapacitor (`4.2 kW` peak cutting burst, `67 min` full recharge from `2.0°C` cold-sink TEG).
* **Safety Interlock:** Acoustic/optical proximity sensor + dual-squeeze Hall-effect deadman trigger (`<0.9 ms` GaN magnetic/arc quench).

## 2. ASTEROID MICROGRAVITY PLASMA ROCK-SPLITTER (`CPC-SPLIT-AST`)

### 2.1 Microgravity (`10⁻⁵ g`) Vacuum Physics & Thermal Sink
* **Environment:** Near-Earth Carbonaceous (`C-type`) or Metallic (`16 Psyche`) asteroids (`10⁻⁹ Pa` hard vacuum, `110 K` to `240 K` shaded regolith sink, `~0.0001 g`).
* **Zero-Recoil Thermal-Shock Fracturing:** Rather than mechanical percussive jackhammers (which impart dangerous reaction forces in microgravity), `CPC-SPLIT-AST` projects a **`5,400 K`, `3.2 kW`** magnetically focused `H₂O/CO` plasma needle (`0.58 T` mirror pinch) into a `6 mm` laser-ablated pilot micro-bore, flash-vaporizing trapped phyllosilicate bound water (`8–12 wt%`) to generate **`45–90 MPa` internal pore-pressure spallation** with **near-zero axial recoil (`<0.15 N`)**.
* **In-Situ Volatile Harvesting:** A conical `Ti-6Al-4V`/graphene collection bell seals against the rock face via electro-adhesive pads, aspirating released `H₂O`, `CO₂`, `SO₂`, and `Ni(CO)₄` carbonyl vapors back into the `90°C` CVD-graphene cavitator to replenish the core's working fluid (`100%+` fluid self-sufficiency) while a shaded `120 K` deployable radiator drives TEG regeneration (`+184.0 We` continuous).
