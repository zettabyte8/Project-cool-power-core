## PART II — CONSOLIDATED SPECIFICATION SUMMARY (`¶ [0001]` – `¶ [0148]`)

### A. Parent Core Architecture (`CPC-PAT-2026-001` | `¶ [0001]–[0032]`)
* `[0001]` **Field of Invention:** A compact (`2.55 kg`), oil-free, closed-loop energy conversion, water purification, and gas-mineralization core combining `90°C` hydrodynamic vortex cavitation inside a CVD-graphene-coated nozzle (`14.2–17.5 W`), `33.6 kV` Zero-Voltage Switching (ZVS) nanosecond pulsed plasma dissociation (`15.1–20.0 W`), an `8YSZ` solid-oxide oxygen-ion membrane, an opposed-piston free-piston linear alternator (`Sm₂Co₁₇` magnets, `5 µm` aerodynamic air bearings), and a `Bi₂Te₃` / `PMN-PT` thermoelectric-pyroelectric waste-heat bottoming stack.
* `[0014]` **Enthalpy Offloading:** External thermal sources (`75°C–450°C` industrial exhaust, jacket coolant, solar-thermal, or metabolic heat) supply `382.93 Wth/core` of sensible and latent preheating enthalpy, elevating net continuous electrical export to `+286.4 to +368.5 We/core` while condensing `12.96 L/day` (`0.54 L/hr`) of `<1 ppm TDS` distilled water.

### B. Nuclear Radiation Harvesting (`CPC-PAT-2026-002-CIP` | `¶ [0033]–[0056]`)
* `[0033]` **Multi-Modal Radiolytic & Direct Conversion Sump:** Integrates CVD-diamond-passivated `4H-SiC` betavoltaic tiles (`+16.5 We`), `400 nm HfO₂/Ta₂O₅` + CVD-graphene Schottky gamma-voltaic liners (`+11.2 We`), `28 mm W-B₄C` graded gamma-to-thermoelectric shielding (`+16.1 We`), and radiolytic `·OH / H·` pre-ionization to achieve `+364.5 We/core` and `>10,000:1` solid radionuclide volume reduction.

### C. Wearable Exoskeleton Life Support (`CPC-PAT-2026-003-CIP` | `¶ [0057]–[0080]`)
* `[0057]` **Metabolic-Heat-Driven Closed-Loop Suit & 10-Axis QDD Exoskeleton:** Captures `+285.0 Wth` from a Liquid Cooling Garment (LCG) to preheat urine, sweat, and condensate to `90°C`, regenerating `100%+` metabolic `O₂` and `99.4%` potable water while driving a `48 V` bidirectional bus (`180 Wh` graphene supercapacitor) with 10-axis Quasi-Direct-Drive regenerative actuators and dual electrical/fluidic PTO ports.

### D. Biomimetic Autonomous Companion & Swarm (`CPC-PAT-2026-004-CIP` & `005-CIP` | `¶ [0081]–[0124]`)
* `[0081]` **Multi-Morphology Robot & Peer-to-Peer Docking:** Harvests `+185 to +310 Wth` from QDD joint actuators across quadruped hound, feline, and winged dragon morphologies, featuring `0.28-second` NIJ Level IV `B₄C/UHMWPE` ballistic shield wings, `38.5°C` affective companion heating, planetary regolith nitrogen-fixation tail augers, and magnetic self-aligning `48 V DC + 90°C` water swarm docking couplers (`CPC-DOCK-48`).

### E. Handheld Martian Plasma Lance (`CPC-PAT-2026-006-CIP` | `¶ [0125]–[0148]`)
* `[0125]` **Planetary Low-Pressure Magneto-Confined Plasma Blade:** Utilizes Mars's `610 Pa` `CO₂` atmosphere (`>94%` convective loss reduction) and `-60°C` ambient sink (`+142.0 We` cryo-fin TEG output) to sustain a `0.92 m`, `4,200 K` `CO/O⁺/H⁺` plasma blade inside a `0.42 T Sm₂Co₁₇` Halbach + `+0.23 T CuCrZr`/diamond Bitter coil (`0.67 T` distal magnetic mirror pinch), coaxial vapor recovery (`>75 hr` per `180 mL` ice cartridge), and `<1.2 ms` capacitive EVA-glove deadman GaN quenching.

## PART III — CONSOLIDATED CLAIMS (1–37)

1. **Claim 1 (Independent — Parent):** A closed-loop energy and fluid conversion apparatus comprising: a `90°C` fluid reservoir coupled to an external waste-heat exchanger; a CVD-graphene-coated hydrodynamic vortex cavitator; a `33.6 kV` ZVS nanosecond pulsed plasma reactor; an `8YSZ` oxygen-ion separator; a free-piston linear alternator with oil-free gas bearings; and a `Bi₂Te₃`/`PMN-PT` thermoelectric-pyroelectric stack bridged to a cold sink.
2. **Claims 2–7 (Dependent):** Specifying `382.93 Wth` enthalpy offloading (`Claim 2`), Sabatier/pyrolysis carbon capture (`Claim 3`), `2°C` abyssal subsea condensation (`Claim 4`), `0g` centrifugal vortex phase separation (`Claim 5`), `V₂O₅/TiO₂` catalytic scrubbing (`Claim 6`), and `1,000-core` containerized ISO skid scaling (`Claim 7`).
3. **Claim 8 (Independent — CIP-1):** A radionuclide remediation and energy harvesting system coupling the apparatus of Claim 1 with a `W-B₄C` gamma-shielded concentration sump lined with `4H-SiC`/CVD-diamond betavoltaic tiles and `HfO₂/Ta₂O₅`-graphene Schottky gamma-voltaic collectors.
4. **Claims 9–13 (Dependent):** Specifying radiolytic plasma pre-ionization (`Claim 9`), tritiated water (`HTO`) kinetic enrichment (`Claim 10`), `Sm₂Co₁₇`/SOI `10⁸ Gy` rad-hardening (`Claim 11`), `W-B₄C` thermal-to-TEG coupling (`Claim 12`), and vitrified waste cake discharge (`Claim 13`).
5. **Claim 14 (Independent — CIP-2):** A wearable closed-loop life-support and powered exoskeleton system comprising a Liquid Cooling Garment (LCG) transferring metabolic body heat (`+165 to +285 Wth`) to preheat human metabolic wastewater to `90°C` for plasma/Sabatier oxygen and water regeneration while energizing a `48 V` graphene-supercapacitor bus driving multi-axis QDD joint actuators.
6. **Claims 15–19 (Dependent):** Specifying four-quadrant QDD regenerative braking (`Claim 15`), `1 kHz` admittance human-intent control (`Claim 16`), dual electrical/fluidic PTO ports (`Claim 17`), `33.6 kV` Electrodynamic Dust Shields (`Claim 18`), and zero-prebreathe planetary EVA pressure regulation (`Claim 19`).
7. **Claim 20 (Independent — CIP-3):** An autonomous biomimetic robot powered by a closed-loop core harvesting waste heat from Quasi-Direct-Drive limb actuators (`+185 to +310 Wth`) to generate continuous DC power, potable water, and breathable oxygen while actuating deployable ballistic shield wings.
8. **Claims 21–25 (Dependent):** Specifying `<0.30 s` NIJ Level IV `B₄C/UHMWPE` wing deployment (`Claim 21`), `38.5°C` affective companion thermal skin (`Claim 22`), attachable graphene soaring wings (`Claim 23`), plasma nitrogen-fixing regolith auger (`Claim 24`), and multi-spectral GPR/LiDAR/CBRN sensor fusion (`Claim 25`).
9. **Claim 26 (Independent — CIP-4):** A multi-robot swarm coordination and resource-sharing system comprising magnetic self-aligning dry-break couplers simultaneously transferring `48 V DC` electrical power and `90°C` preheated water between autonomous cores, paired with UWB-synchronized interlocking ballistic shield formation.
10. **Claims 27–31 (Dependent):** Specifying market-based energy/water auction routing (`Claim 27`), `<0.35 s` UWB phalanx alignment (`Claim 28`), check-valve dry-break fluidic isolation (`Claim 29`), aerial-relay LoRa telemetry (`Claim 30`), and emergency medic O₂/water transfusion (`Claim 31`).
11. **Claim 32 (Independent — CIP-5):** A handheld magneto-confined plasma tool for low-pressure planetary atmospheres (`<2,000 Pa`) comprising: a `180 mL` water/ice cartridge; a `90°C` CVD-graphene vortex cavitator and `33.6 kV` ZVS plasma exciter powered by a graphene supercapacitor; a hybrid `Sm₂Co₁₇` Halbach array and `CuCrZr`/CVD-diamond Bitter coil generating a distal magnetic mirror pinch (`0.67 T`) to confine a `0.92 m`, `4,200 K` plasma blade; an external `-60°C` cryo-fin thermoelectric generator recharging the supercapacitor (`+142.0 We`); and a `<1.2 ms` capacitive EVA-glove deadman GaN magnetic quench circuit.
12. **Claims 33–37 (Dependent):** Specifying coaxial vapor recovery and re-condensation (`Claim 33`), `β ≤ 0.20` low-beta toroidal stabilization (`Claim 34`), `2.48 kg` hilt mass (`0.93 kg` Martian weight) (`Claim 35`), `150 W` to `3.5 kW` variable duty modes (`Claim 36`), and `10⁸ Gy` radiation-tolerant SOI control electronics (`Claim 37`).
