## 2. Newly Integrated CIP-14 Claims (Claims 78–83 with PCT Rule 6.2(b) Reference Numerals)

* **Claim 78 (Independent — Briefcase-Deployed Neural Flight Exosuit):** A portable personal flight and physical-assist exosuit system (`2700`), comprising: a collapsible carry-on housing (`2702`) configured to deploy via shape-memory hinges (`2704`) into an articulated multi-axis exoskeleton frame (`2706`); at least one closed-loop thermal-cavitation power core (`2708`) harvesting metabolic and actuator waste heat (`90°C`) to generate continuous electrical output; a plurality of hybrid magnetoplasma and electrohydrodynamic (EHD) ion thrusters (`2710`) coupled to the exoskeleton frame (`2706`) to provide propellantless atmospheric lift; and a non-invasive neural-command helmet (`2712`) locked behind a zero-RF Faraday shield (`2714`) and unidirectional optical data diode (`2716`) to translate wearer cortical intent into flight and limb actuation without vulnerability to external radio-frequency hijacking.
* **Claim 79 (Dependent):** The system of claim 78, wherein the neural-command helmet (`2712`) further comprises a `0.4 ms` somatic electromyographic (EMG) override circuit (`2718`) that immediately severs optical command transmission upon involuntary muscle tension.
* **Claim 80 (Dependent):** The system of claim 78, further comprising dual forearm-mounted atmospheric-condensing projectile/plasma cannons (`2720`) and retractable magnetic-mirror plasma blades (`2722`) energized by the closed-loop thermal-cavitation power core (`2708`).
* **Claim 81 (Independent — Leg-Assisted Neural Quad-Tentacle Exoskeleton):** A load-compensated multi-limbed robotic augmentation system (`2800`), comprising: a dorsal chassis (`2802`) housing dual closed-loop thermal-cavitation power cores (`2804`); four hyper-redundant continuum manipulator tentacles (`2806`) extending from the dorsal chassis (`2802`); an air-gapped wearable neural-command cap (`2808`) communicating via encrypted optical tether (`2810`) to control the four continuum manipulator tentacles (`2806`); and a pair of articulated lower-limb exoskeleton legs (`2812`) mechanically coupled directly to the dorsal chassis (`2802`) to route both system mass and manipulator payload forces directly to a ground surface (`2814`), isolating a human wearer's spine from vertical compressive load.
* **Claim 82 (Dependent):** The system of claim 81, wherein each continuum manipulator tentacle (`2806`) terminates in a multi-fingered omni-claw (`2816`) integrating a micro-plasma cutting torch and high-pressure distilled-water jet fed by the dual closed-loop thermal-cavitation power cores (`2804`).
* **Claim 83 (Dependent):** The system of claim 81, wherein the articulated lower-limb exoskeleton legs (`2812`) harvest heel-strike kinetic energy and joint actuator waste heat back into the `90°C` thermal reservoir of the dual closed-loop thermal-cavitation power cores (`2804`).
