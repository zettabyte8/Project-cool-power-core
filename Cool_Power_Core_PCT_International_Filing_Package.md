## 4. PCT Rule 11 & WIPO ST.26 / XML Formatting Compliance Checklist

1. **A4 Page Format (`PCT Rule 11.5`):** Convert U.S. Letter (`8.5" × 11"`) pages to **ISO A4 (`210 mm × 297 mm`)** with minimum margins: Top `2.0 cm`, Left `2.5 cm`, Right `2.0 cm`, Bottom `2.0 cm`.
2. **Paragraph & Line Numbering (`PCT Rule 11.8`):** Maintain consecutive bracketed paragraph numbers (`[0001]` through `[0084]`) and 5-line interval numbering in the left margin.
3. **Reference Signs in Claims (`PCT Rule 6.2(b)`):** Insert parenthetical reference numerals corresponding to `FIGS. 1–15` into all 43 claims (mandatory for EPO/PCT examination even though optional in the USPTO).
4. **Multiple Dependent Claims (`PCT Rule 6.4(a)`):** Permitted in PCT international applications (unlike USPTO practice, where multiple dependent claims carry a surcharge); preserve single-dependency structure from `CPC-PAT-2026-001`–`007-CIP` to avoid U.S. National Stage penalties under `35 U.S.C. § 371`.
5. **Abstract Figure Selection (`PCT Rule 3.3(a)(iii)`):** Designate **`FIG. 1`** (Core Closed-Loop Thermodynamic & Nanosecond Pulsed-Plasma System) to accompany the 150-word PCT Abstract on the front page of the WIPO publication (`WO 2027/XXXXXX`).
