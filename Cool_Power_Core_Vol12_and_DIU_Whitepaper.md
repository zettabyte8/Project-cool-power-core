## PART I: DoD / DIU RAPID PROTOTYPING WHITEPAPER (`CPC-DEF-FOB-100`)
### 1. Executive Operational Problem Statement
Contested logistics remain the primary vulnerability of expeditionary U.S. and allied forces. Forward Operating Bases (FOBs), Expeditionary Advanced Bases (EABO), and mobile Role-2 surgical units rely on vulnerable fuel and bottled-water convoys while operating open burn-pits that expose warfighters to carcinogenic dioxins, particulate matter, and per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS). Conventional tactical diesel generators operate at ~28% thermal efficiency, broadcast high infrared (IR) and acoustic signatures, and discard >70% of fuel enthalpy as waste heat.

### 2. The Cool Power Core™ Solution (`CPC-DEF-FOB-100`)
The **Cool Power Core™ (`CPC-DEF-FOB-100`)** is a 463L-pallet-compatible, 100-core tactical tri-generation skid (460 kg dry mass) that harvests waste heat from existing JP-8/F-24 tactical generators, vehicle exhausts, or solar-thermal collectors to preheat contaminated wastewater to **90°C**. Using a **CVD graphene-coated fluidic vortex cavitator**, a **33.6 kV ZVS nanosecond pulsed plasma stage**, and **50 Hz oil-free 5 µm air-bearing opposed-piston linear alternators**, a single skid simultaneously produces:
* **+32.4 kW Continuous Tactical DC/AC Power** (28 VDC MIL-STD-1275, 48 VDC, and 120/240 VAC)
* **1,296 L/day Sterile Potable Water** (`98.5%` closed-loop recovery from greywater, blackwater, and AFFF-contaminated runoff; `<4 ppt` PFAS)
* **48.0 kg/day (`33,600 L/day`) Medical-Grade Oxygen (`99.5%` Purity)** for Role-2 surgical care and aviation breathing systems
* **Zero-Burn-Pit Total Mineralization** (`99.9999%` 6-Log Destruction Removal Efficiency for PFAS, chemical simulants, medical waste, and microplastics)
