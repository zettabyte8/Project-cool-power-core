# 20 — Safety

## Hazards

| Hazard | Magnitude | Control |
|---|---|---|
| 33.6 kV DC | Lethal, low current | Interlocked enclosure, discharge resistor, 60 s lockout |
| 900 °C surfaces | Ignition, burns | Double-wall, 25 mm lagging, guards |
| H₂ in chamber | 4–75% flammable | Purge 3 s before arc |
| O₃ | 0.1 ppm TLV | All destroyed thermally; UV monitor at outlet |
| NH₃ slip | 25 ppm TLV | ANR limit 1.15, ASC block |
| 6 bar air | Stored energy | PRV at 8 bar, relief on accumulator |
| Pressure vessel | Chamber + accumulator | Hydro test 1.5× before first run |
| DEF | Mild irritant | Gloves, eye wash |

## Rules
1. Never open the HV enclosure within 60 s of power off.
2. Discharge the 33.6 kV rail to earth and verify before touching.
3. Purge before every arc enable — 3 s minimum.
4. Bearing air before rotor; rotor coast-down before bearing air off.
5. No operation without the outlet O₃ monitor.

## Certification path
EN 60335-1 (appliance), EN 55014 (EMC), EN 1822 (filter), local pressure vessel if accumulator > 1 L.
