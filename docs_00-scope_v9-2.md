# 00 — Scope

## What it is

A quiet floor-standing air purifier for a sealed room or cabin, 10–25 m³. Destroys pollutants rather than trapping them, condenses water, powers itself.

## Priority

Air first, water second, watts last. **If a change gains watts and costs decibels, water or life, it loses.**

## What it is not

- Not a generator. +155 W is dumped as light and fan.
- Not fuel-free. −1.4 L/day water, one cartridge a year.
- Not medical oxygen. 21.5–23% enriched.
- Not whole-house. 23.3 m³/h serves one sealed room.

## Envelope

Ambient −5 to 45 °C · RH 20–99% · altitude 0–2000 m.

Below 40% RH draw rises to 2.6 L/day. Below 20% it needs a reservoir.

## Success criteria

G1 f ≥ 0.15 · G3 VOC DE ≥ 95% · G4 regenerator ≥ 0.75 · G5 noise ≤ 38 dBA auto · G6 1000 h, DE holds 5%.

**Kill:** f < 0.12 → strip the alternator load path, sell as purifier. Noise > 42 dBA → rework mounts before anything else.

## Out of scope rev 5

Deep sea beyond 50 m · space (0 g wicking deferred) · vehicle · oxygen membrane.
