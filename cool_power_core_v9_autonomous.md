## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & HEADLESS ZERO-TOUCH ARCHITECTURE

The **Cool Power Core (V9)** eliminates the external touchscreen and all user-facing configuration menus in favor of a **100% autonomous, self-regulating headless architecture**. The end-user interacts with the machine exclusively via a single **armored physical 3-position rotary/rocker switch**:
1. **`OFF / MAINTENANCE LOCKOUT`:** Safely discharges the graphene supercapacitor bus through a bleed resistor, vents bounce-chamber gas pressure to ambient, and unlocks the solid-state particulate/mineral ash drawer.
2. **`ON — AUTO-ADAPTIVE MODE`:** Full closed-loop autonomous operation. The embedded RISC-V/FPGA controller continuously samples intake humidity, particulate loading, combustion frequency, and thermal gradients, automatically tuning intake fan pitch/RPM, H₂/O₂ pilot dosing, NTP pulse rate, and condenser sub-cooling without human intervention.
3. **`PASSIVE FALLBACK / STEALTH MODE`:** Cuts power to the intake fan and high-output electrolysis bus, allowing the opposed-piston core to run purely on acoustic/thermal self-resonance at ~35% throughput with near-zero acoustic signature.

### System Status Telemetry (Zero-Screen Feedback)
* **Tri-Color Single-Point LED Halo around the Power Switch:**
  * *Solid Cyan:* Optimal autonomous operation (producing clean air, O₂, potable water, and auxiliary DC).
  * *Pulsing Amber:* Auto-regeneration/self-cleaning thermal pulse cycle active (15 minutes every 500 hours).
  * *Solid White:* Maintenance ash/mineral trap full (recommended empty interval: once every 10,000+ hours).
* **Optional Encrypted Near-Field (NFC / BLE) Diagnostic Tap:** Service technicians or spacecraft/submarine telemetry buses can read internal sensor logs passively without adding fragile display hardware.
