## 1. Engineering P&ID & ROI Model: Mixed-Plastic & Wind-Turbine Blade Monomer Depolymerization Skid (#611 / #618)

### 1.1 Closed-Loop Process Architecture (`CPC-POLY-1000`)
Conventional plastic pyrolysis and wind-turbine blade (glass/carbon-fiber epoxy thermoset) recycling suffer from high external fuel requirements, waxy tar fouling, chlorinated dioxin formation (from PVC contamination), and toxic bisphenol-A (BPA) phenolic wastewater. Integrating a **1,000-core Cool Power Core™ (`CPC-POLY-1000`) ISO skid** creates a self-powered, zero-discharge chemical recycling plant:

1. **380°C Catalytic Solvolysis & Pre-Melting:** Shredded mixed polyolefins (PE/PP/PS), PET, PVC, or wind-turbine blade composites enter a 380°C low-pressure depolymerization reactor. Reactor jacket and condenser waste heat (**592.93 Wth/core**) preheats the water reservoir to **90°C** and supplies full latent vaporization enthalpy.
2. **90°C CVD Graphene Vortex Emulsification:** Preheated 90°C water and heavy pyrolysis wax pass through the **1.80 mm CVD graphene-coated fluidic vortex amplifier**, cutting cavitation power by 52% (**14.8 W/core**) and forming a uniform micro-emulsion.
3. **33.6 kV ZVS Nanosecond Radical Cleavage:** The 8-stage Cockcroft-Walton nanosecond plasma (**18.5 W/core**) drives selective C–C and C–O scission into high-value **BTX aromatics, ethylene/propylene monomers, and clean naphtha**, while mineralizing PVC chlorine into inert **CaCl₂** and destroying dioxins/BPA to **>99.9999% (6-Log)**.
4. **Clean Fiber Recovery & Cogeneration:** For wind-turbine blades, thermoset epoxy resin cleanly depolymerizes into reusable phenolic monomers while liberating intact, undamaged **E-glass or carbon fibers** (`1.4–8.4 TPD`). Non-condensable C₁–C₃ tail-gases drive the **50 Hz opposed-piston 8YSZ core** (`+256.0 We/core`), yielding **+332.2 kW net continuous electrical export** per 1,000-core skid.
