### 1.1 Process & Thermodynamic Architecture
Oceans hold ~4.5 billion metric tons of dissolved uranium (`3.3 ppb` as tricarbonatouranyl `[UO2(CO3)3]4-`), over 600× terrestrial reserves. Conventional seawater uranium extraction is bottlenecked by chemical elution energy, eluent degradation, and brine disposal. `CPC-SEA-U-1684` integrates directly onto offshore wind-farm or wave-energy barges harvesting poly(amidoxime) braided polymer moorings:
- **78°C Solar/Marine Thermal Elution Offset (`382.93 Wth/core`):** Low-grade solar-thermal collectors and offshore transformer waste heat warm `0.5 M Na2CO3 + H2O2` eluent to `78°C`, offsetting sensible preheating (`43.93 Wth`) and latent vaporization (`339.00 Wth`).
- **90°C CVD Graphene Cavitation & 33.6 kV ZVS Plasma:** Hydrodynamic cavitation (`14.90 We`) strips uranyl complexes cleanly from amidoxime fibers without acid degradation, while `33.6 kV` nanosecond pulsed plasma (`15.10 We`, assisted by radiolytic ion pre-ionization) decomposes peroxide/organic foulants and precipitates high-purity **uranyl peroxide (`UO4·2H2O`) / yellowcake (`U3O8`)**.
- **Alpha/Beta/Gamma-Voltaic Cogeneration (`+17.00 We/core`):** Decay emissions from concentrated `U-238`, `U-235`, and daughter `Th-234` (`2.33 MeV` max beta) / `Pa-234m` strike **CVD-diamond-passivated 4H-SiC betavoltaic tiles** and **400 nm HfO2/Ta2O5 + graphene Schottky gamma-voltaic sump liners** (`CPC-PAT-2026-002-CIP`), adding `+17.00 We/core` above the baseline marine cycle.
- **4°C Deep-Seawater Cold Sink (`ΔT = 86 K`):** Subsea thermocline heat exchangers condense clean steam into **12.96 L/core/day** of `<0.1 ppm TDS` Ultra-Pure Water while boosting `Bi2Te3/PMN-PT` thermoelectric recovery to `+48.50 We/core`.
