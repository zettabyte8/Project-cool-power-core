## 1. Lighter-Than-Air (`CPC-LTA`) Composite Airship Architecture
At **20 km (65,600 ft)** stratospheric cruise (`-56.5 °C`, `5.47 kPa`), pairing lightweight **2.55 kg** carbon-aerogel composite Cool Power Core modules with composite lighter-than-air (LTA) hulls unlocks three synergistic advantages:
- **+312.0 W Net Continuous Power / Core (`122.4 W/kg`):** Driven by a `146.5 K` thermal gradient (`90 °C` reservoir vs. `-56.5 °C` upper composite skin) yielding `+98.5 W` from the Bi₂Te₃/Skutterudite TEG + PMN-PT pyroelectric stack.
- **Zero-Ballast Thermal Buoyancy Trim (`+1.85 kg lift / core`):** Controlled waste-heat transfer warms internal lifting-gas cells by `+12 K` to regulate altitude without venting helium/hydrogen or dropping expendable ballast.
- **Closed-Loop Mass Conservation:** Condensed water (`0.54 L/hr` closed loop) prevents vehicle weight drift across multi-month station-keeping missions.
