## 2. Non-Invasive Wearable Neural-Command Hat (`CPC-NEURO-CAP`)
To achieve multi-limb dexterity without invasive cranial implants, the **`CPC-NEURO-CAP`** (`310 g` breathable ballistic-knit beanie / hard-hat liner / EVA skullcap) fuses **three complementary non-invasive brain-computer interface (BCI) modalities** with an onboard low-power neuromorphic intent decoder:

### Multi-Modal Neural Sensing Array
1. **64-Channel Dry Graphene-Elastomer High-Density EEG (`hdEEG`):** Captures sensorimotor cortex mu/beta rhythms (`8–30 Hz`), Event-Related Desynchronization (ERD), and readiness potentials (`Bereitschaftspotential`) across primary motor (`M1`), supplementary motor (`SMA`), and posterior parietal (`PPC`) cortices with zero conductive gel.
2. **32-Channel Time-Domain Functional Near-Infrared Spectroscopy (`TD-fNIRS`):** Uses `760 nm / 850 nm` picosecond micro-LEDs and SPAD detectors to map cortical hemodynamic oxygenation changes at `1.5 cm` depth, disambiguating fine finger/claw grasp intent from gross arm trajectory.
3. **16-Channel Magnetomyography & Cranial Micro-EMG Array (`MMG/EMG`):** Detects sub-threshold efferent motor-nerve firing and micro-fasciculations along the mastoid, temporalis, and upper trapezius muscles to trigger instant reflexive stabilization (`<6 ms`) when catching falling loads or bracing against recoil.

### Neuromorphic Shared-Autonomy Control Loop (`<18 ms` Latency)
* **Co-Adaptive Spiking Neural Network (`SNN`):** Onboard `4.5 W` neuromorphic processor continuously calibrates to the wearer's unique neural baseline during a **3-minute guided calibration**, expanding the human body schema ("homunculus") to treat the four dorsal tentacles as natural biological limbs.
* **Hierarchical Shared Autonomy:** The wearer's brain commands high-level **6-DOF end-effector goals, grasp force, and tool activation** (e.g., *"brace left wall, hold steel beam overhead, weld seam with lower-right torch"*), while local optical-tactile reflex loops inside each tentacle handle inverse kinematics, obstacle avoidance, and slip-free micro-grip adjustment at `1,000 Hz`.
* **Bidirectional Haptic & Proprioceptive Feedback:** Micro-piezo vibrotactile and electrotactile transducers inside the hatband and shoulder harness relay contact pressure, surface temperature, and load torque back to the wearer's skin, preventing accidental crushing of fragile objects.

### Convertible 4-Fingered Omni-Claw & Integrated CPC Tool End-Effectors
