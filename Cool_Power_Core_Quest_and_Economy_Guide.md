### 1. How Players Earn Real-Backed `CPC-COIN` Through Skill & Thermodynamics

1. **Competitive Bid Undercutting (Market Haggling):**
   * Legacy chemical-rocket and fossil-ferry NPCs quote inflated fares (`48.0 – 115.0 CPC`) to transport passengers, apprentices, and cargo between worlds.
   * Because the player's **`CPC-LOOP`**, **`CPC-SHUTTLE`**, and **Orbital Shuttle** harvest **382.93 Wth/core** of braking, linear-stator, and atmospheric entry heat via `90°C` CVD-graphene cavitation and `33.6 kV` plasma, marginal transit fuel cost is **75%–88% lower** than legacy NPC competitors.

     * **Aggressive Undercut (`-22%` below rival quote):** `96%` base win probability, fast escrow lock, reliable profit.
     * **Balanced Value (`-12%` below rival quote):** `82%` base win probability, higher margin + reputation boost.
     * **Premium Express (`-4%` below rival quote):** `58%` base win probability, maximum `CPC-COIN` payout.

   * **Tier 0 (Android Mobile Web):** `0%` extra rig discount (standard core efficiency).
