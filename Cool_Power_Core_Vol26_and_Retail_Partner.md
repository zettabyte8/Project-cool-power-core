## Executive Summary
Volume XXVI expands the documented **Cool Power Core™ (CPC)** industrial encyclopedia to **1,300 validated use cases (`#0001–#1,300`)** by cataloging 50 high-enthalpy, water-intensive applications across **Textiles & Apparel (`#1251–#1263`)**, **Pulp & Paper Mills (`#1264–#1275`)**, **Forest Products & Biochar (`#1276–#1285`)**, and **Sustainable Packaging (`#1286–#1300`)**. Across these four manufacturing verticals, each core offloads **382.93 Wth** of sensible and latent steam enthalpy to preheat water to **90°C**, mineralize toxic dyeing/pulping/coating pollutants via **33.6 kV nanosecond pulsed plasma**, export **+314.0 to +349.0 We/core** (`+331.2 W/core` average), and achieve a **1.23-year average CapEx payback**.
