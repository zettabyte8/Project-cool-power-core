## 1. Executive Architectural & Ecological Vision
The **`CPC-CITY-RIDGE-250K`** scales the aerodynamic linear ridge-and-valley megastructure to house **250,000 permanent residents** and **35,000 resort guests** across a **36.0 km (22.37-mile)** curvilinear landform (`540 m` base width; **`1,944 hectares` / `7.51 sq mi`** total footprint) engineered specifically for **land considered uninhabitable or ecologically degraded**—preserving 100% of arable farmland, old-growth forests, and freshwater watersheds.

### Key Design & Environmental Pillars
* **Zero Arable Land Displacement:** Sited exclusively inside excavated open-pit mine scars, hyper-arid alkali salt flats, sub-zero permafrost barrens, or barren coastal sabkhas—turning ecological liabilities into thriving self-contained biomes.
* **Aerodynamic Natural-Hill Geometry (`+165 m` Crest | `22°–25°` Slopes):** Sculpted with 18 saddle wind-passes that channel desert katabatic breezes, harvest flash-flood storm runoff into subterranean cisterns, and eliminate urban heat-island turbulence.
* **28 Sun-Terraced Interior Canyon Levels (`82,500` Residences & Suites):** The open V-shaped central valley steps down across **28 staggered garden terraces**, guaranteeing **every single residence and resort suite** a private sunlit balcony yard, fruit-tree planter, and plunge pool.
* **Thick Living Radiation & Thermal Mantle (`2.5 m – 4.0 m` Depth):** Exterior hill slopes and terrace decks are clad in `2.5 m to 4.0 m` of engineered local regolith/soil, basalt root-barriers, and native xerophyte/halophyte forests—attenuating cosmic and solar radiation by **>97.5% (`<0.35 mSv/yr`)** and slashing HVAC loads by **86%**.
* **18-Level Subterranean Commerce, AI Vault & Entertainment Canyon (`-108 m` Deep):** Beneath the car-free valley park sit 18 climate-controlled subterranean levels housing global financial exchanges, liquid-cooled AI compute vaults, heliostat-lit luxury retail gallerias, a **35,000-seat canyon stadium**, thermal mineral baths, and a dual-bore **`CPC-LOOP` (`920 km/h`) / `CPC-SHUTTLE`** autonomous logistics spine delivering goods directly to residential pantries in **<12 minutes**.
