## 1. Executive Summary
The **Cool Power Core™ (`CPC-TRANSIT`) Intercity Transport Network** links self-sufficient Sanctuary Cities and major metropolitan centers via zero-emission, closed-loop pneumatic-maglev tubes (**`CPC-LOOP`**), superconducting high-speed rail (**`CPC-RAIL`**), and autonomous cold-chain freight shuttles (**`CPC-SHUTTLE`**).

Rather than wasting 65%–78% of propulsion energy as low-grade heat in vacuum roughing pumps, linear induction stators, and dynamic braking resistors, `CPC-TRANSIT` embeds **Cool Power Core™** modules directly into guideway stations and rolling stock. By bridging `90°C–340°C` transit thermal losses against `14°C–16°C` sub-surface geothermal foundation piles (`ΔT ≈ 75 K to 325 K`), the network continuously generates clean DC microgrid power, distills `<1 ppm TDS` pure water, and maintains `21.5°C / 48% RH` closed-loop passenger cabins with zero trackside sewage discharge.

## 3. Closed-Loop Guideway & Cabin Thermodynamics
1. **Vacuum Roughing-Pump & Stator Heat Recovery (`CPC-LOOP`):**  
   Maintaining `100 Pa` tube pressure requires distributed dry-screw vacuum pumps every `1.0 km`. Each station houses **400 CPC cores** coupled to the `95°C` pump cooling jackets (`382.93 Wth/core` offset), generating **+137.60 kWe continuous (`+344.0 We/core`)**—offsetting **84.5%** of steady-state vacuum maintenance power while exporting surplus power to adjacent solar/battery microgrids.
2. **Dynamic Braking & Inverter Harvesting (`CPC-RAIL`):**  
   During deceleration from `420 km/h`, dynamic braking grids and SiC traction inverters reject `340°C` thermal pulses into a pressurized phase-change thermal buffer. **240 onboard CPC cores** convert this stored heat against ambient slipstream air into **+82.56 kWe continuous auxiliary power** for cabin HVAC, lighting, and passenger Wi-Fi.
3. **Zero-Discharge Onboard Cabin ECLSS (`CPC-RES-1` Integration):**  
   Every `CPC-LOOP` pod and `CPC-RAIL` coach integrates a closed-loop life-support unit utilizing `90°C` CVD-graphene cavitation and `33.6 kV` nanosecond pulsed plasma to recycle exhaled CO₂, cabin humidity, and lavatory greywater/blackwater into sterile `21.5°C / 48% RH` cabin air and `<1 ppm TDS` wash water—eliminating septic pump-out stations.
4. **Geothermal Foundation Piles:**  
   Precast concrete guideway piers incorporate closed-loop HDPE geothermal heat exchangers (`15 m` depth at `14.5°C`), guaranteeing a stable cold sink across `+48°C` Mojave Desert summers and `-35°C` Northern winters.
