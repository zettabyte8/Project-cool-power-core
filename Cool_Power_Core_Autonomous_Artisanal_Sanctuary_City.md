## 1. Executive Vision & Civic Philosophy

Traditional homelessness interventions rely on temporary congregate shelters with curfews, rigid sobriety or exit deadlines, high municipal utility hookup fees, and chronic operating deficits. The **Cool Power Core™ Autonomous Artisanal Sanctuary City (`CPC-CITY-1000`)** replaces the shelter-and-subsidy paradigm with a **thermodynamically self-sufficient, prefab modular campus** powered and closed-looped by the **Cool Power Core™ (CPC)**.

1. **Unconditional Basic Needs & Dignity:** Every resident receives a private, climate-controlled, sound-insulated prefab artisanal studio dwelling (`28 m²` / `300 ft²`) equipped with an integrated **`CPC-RES-1`** zero-septic water, power, and domestic hot-water pod—eliminating sewer mains, grid utility bills, and shared dormitory crowding.
2. **Open Movement & Indefinite Residency:** There are no locked gates, curfews, or arbitrary 30/90-day eviction clocks. Residents may enter, depart, travel for external employment, or reside permanently in the sanctuary city for as long as they choose.
3. **Education- & Creativity-First Culture:** Rather than warehousing people, the city is organized as an **open polytechnic atelier and creative commons**—offering tuition-free, self-paced studios in metalworking, woodworking, ceramics, textiles, digital fabrication, robotics, regenerative agriculture, culinary arts, music, and software engineering.
4. **Circular Artisanal & Civic Economy:** Surplus clean electricity, distilled water, organic vertical-farm produce, biochar/mineral fertilizers, and resident-crafted artisanal goods generate self-sustaining civic revenue without ever charging residents rent or tuition.

## 5. Governance, Freedom of Movement & Social Compact

To protect both **individual freedom** and **collective safety**, the city operates on a transparent, restorative self-governance model:

1. **Absolute Freedom to Come, Go & Stay:**
   * Residents receive a biometric/NFC keycard to their private studio. There are **no curfews, no mandatory religious/program attendance, and no maximum stay limits**. A resident may stay for three days, three years, or a lifetime.
   * Residents working external jobs keep **100% of their wages** with zero rent clawbacks.
2. **Three Core Civic Agreements (Non-Negotiable Safety Floor):**
   * **Non-Violence:** Zero tolerance for physical assault, weapons, or intimidation.
   * **Mutual Respect & Quiet Hours:** Respect for studio privacy, shared tools, and nighttime acoustic peace (`22:00 – 07:00`).
   * **Environmental Stewardship:** No open burning or toxic dumping; all waste enters the closed-loop CPC recovery streams.
3. **Restorative Peer Council & Civic Stipends:**
   * Disputes are mediated by a rotating **Peer Restorative Council** supported by trained conflict-resolution facilitators.
   * While housing, food, utilities, healthcare, and education are unconditional, residents who contribute **10–15 hours/week** to campus kitchens, vertical farms, studios, or maintenance earn **Civic Craft Credits** redeemable for discretionary goods, travel passes, or direct USD stipends.
