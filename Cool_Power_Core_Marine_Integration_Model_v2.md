## 1. Executive Summary & Core Specifications
Integrating the **Cool Power Core** with a commercial marine propulsion plant offloads water preheating (20°C → 90°C, **43.93 W/core**) and vaporization enthalpy (**339.0 W/core**) onto existing main-engine jacket cooling water and exhaust gas economizers.

* **Bore & Stroke:** 25 mm bore × 40 mm stroke opposed free-piston configuration (30:1 compression ratio, 50 Hz cycle).
* **Thermal & Magnetic Isolation:** ~3,000°C plasma core lined with **YSZ ceramic** and insulated by **argon-filled silica aerogel**, keeping **radial-magnetized N52H arrays** below 100°C.
* **Zero-Wear Bearings:** 5-micron pressurized air bearings and graphene-coated fluidic vortex amplifiers engineered for **≥25,000 hours** of continuous operation.
* **Net Electrical Output:** **275 W/core** (27.5 kW per 100-core array; 275 kW per 1,000-core array) after deducting 53.5 W/core in parasitic ECU, ZVS plasma, and vortex cavitation loads.
