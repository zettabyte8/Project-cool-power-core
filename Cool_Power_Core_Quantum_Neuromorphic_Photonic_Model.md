## 1. Executive Thermodynamic & Cleanroom Rationale
Next-generation compute and semiconductor fabrication facilities—**dilution-refrigerated quantum computers**, **liquid-cooled neuromorphic wafer-scale AI clusters**, and **High-NA Extreme Ultraviolet (EUV) / Silicon Photonics (SiPh) foundries**—face an acute triple bottleneck:
1. **Continuous High-Grade Thermal Rejection:** Quantum pulse-tube helium compressors reject `10–15 kWth` per QPU at `85°C–95°C`; wafer-scale neuromorphic pods reject megawatts of `70°C–78°C` Direct-to-Chip (DLC) coolant; and `0.55 NA` High-NA EUV scanners reject `>1.3 MWth` of `95°C–140°C` source and mirror cooling heat alongside hydrogen and tin-droplet vapor.
2. **Ultra-Pure Water (UPW) & Cooling-Tower Blowdown Limits:** Traditional evaporative cooling consumes `1.5–2.2 L/kWh` (`WUE`), while semiconductor cleanrooms require `<0.05 ppm TDS`, sub-ppb Total Organic Carbon (TOC) Ultra-Pure Water.
3. **High-GWP Fluorocarbon & Toxic Hydride Emissions:** Etch and CVD chambers emit `NF3` (`GWP 17,400`), `SF6` (`GWP 23,500`), `CF4`, `SiH4`, `AsH3`, and `PH3`, which conventional point-of-use burn-wet scrubbers destroy using parasitic natural gas and heavy chemical dosing.

The **Cool Power Core™ (`CPC`)** bridges these `75°C–150°C` facility heat loops directly against existing `4°C–7°C` cleanroom chilled-water return lines. By offloading **100% of sensible water preheating (`43.93 Wth/core`)** and **100% of latent vaporization enthalpy (`339.00 Wth/core`)** onto facility waste heat, each core generates **+328.5 to +352.5 We** of net continuous cleanroom power, distills **12.96 L/day of `<0.05 ppm TDS` UPW**, and mineralizes toxic cleanroom exhaust gases via `33.6 kV` ZVS nanosecond pulsed plasma.

## 2. Three Flagship Architecture Deep-Dives

### 2.1 Quantum Computing Dilution-Refrigerator Compressor Harvester (`CPC-QNT-1551` | Case `#1,551`)
* **Array Sizing:** `100-core` modular skid coupled to a `50-QPU` superconducting/photonic quantum compute hall (`~600 kWth` rejected at `92°C` from water-cooled helium pulse-tube compressors).
* **Thermodynamic Bridge:** Uses `92°C` compressor cooling water to sustain the `90°C` CVD graphene vortex reservoir (`14.80 W` cavitation draw) and condenses exhaust steam against the `4°C` cryogenic chilled-water loop (`62.80 W` TEG/pyroelectric cogeneration).
* **Performance & ROI:** Exports **+34.45 kWe** continuous (`+344.5 We/core`), produces **1,296 L/day** of `<0.05 ppm TDS` UPW, cuts mechanical chiller load by `38.3 kWth`, and achieves a **1.14-year simple payback** (`$165,000` installed CAPEX vs. `$144,736/yr` savings).

### 2.2 Liquid-Cooled Neuromorphic Wafer-Scale AI Cluster (`CPC-NEU-1561` | Case `#1,561`)
* **Array Sizing:** `1,000-core` containerized utility pod integrated with a `10 MW` liquid-cooled neuromorphic/spiking neural network (SNN) AI pod (`75°C` two-phase DLC coolant return / `5°C` chilled-water trim).
* **Closed-Loop Zero-Blowdown Water:** Eliminates open evaporative cooling towers, driving facility **Water Usage Effectiveness (`WUE`) down to `0.008 L/kWh`** while generating **12,960 L/day** of pure distillate.
* **Performance & ROI:** Exports **+328.5 kWe** continuous (`+328.5 We/core`), eliminates `$1.24M/yr` in utility power, water, and chemical biocides, and achieves a **1.19-year simple payback** (`$1,480,000` installed CAPEX).

### 2.3 High-NA EUV (`0.55 NA`) Tin-Droplet Plasma & $\text{H}_2$ Recovery Skid (`CPC-EUV-1571` | Case `#1,571`)
* **Array Sizing:** `500-core` cleanroom sub-fab skid serving a single `0.55 NA` High-NA EUV lithography scanner (`1.4 MW` input power, `125°C` LPP tin-plasma source cooling water, and `H2` buffer gas exhaust).
* **Material & Gas Circularity:** `90°C` CVD graphene vortex cavitation scrubs sub-micron tin (`Sn`) aerosol particles (**99.98% metallic Sn recovery**), while the `33.6 kV` ZVS plasma and `8YSZ` membrane purify and recycle **99.6% of ultra-pure $\text{H}_2$** buffer gas.
* **Performance & ROI:** Exports **+176.25 kWe** continuous (`+352.5 We/core`), recovers **6,480 L/day** of semiconductor-grade UPW, saves `$711,711/yr`, and achieves a **1.11-year simple payback** (`$790,000` installed CAPEX).
