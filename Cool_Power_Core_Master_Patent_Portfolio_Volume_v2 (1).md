## ABSTRACT OF THE CONSOLIDATED DISCLOSURE
A closed-loop thermo-chemical-electrical energy conversion, remediation, and autonomous life-support system (**Cool Power Core™ / CPC**) utilizes recovered waste heat stored in a thermally insulated reservoir at **90°C** to lower water surface tension by ~17% and reduce cavitation power requirements by 30%–45% prior to passing fluid through a **chemical-vapor-deposited (CVD) graphene-coated fluidic vortex amplifier** (`1.80 ± 0.005 mm` throat). The resulting mist is dissociated by a **33.6 kV nanosecond pulsed corona/ozone plasma** driven by a `4.2 V / 20 W` zero-voltage-switching (ZVS) `1000×` primary MnZn ferrite U-core transformer and 8-stage Cockcroft-Walton multiplier, feeding a **50 Hz opposed-piston linear alternator** (`25 mm` bore, `40 mm` stroke, `30:1` compression, `~3,000°C` peak combustion) lined with **8YSZ ceramic**, **5 µm porous air bearings**, and an **8 mm argon-filled silica aerogel barrier** maintaining radial-magnetized **N52H** magnets below `100°C`. Multi-modal exhaust harvesting via a micro Tesla turbine, VGT, `Bi₂Te₃/PMN-PT` thermoelectric/pyroelectric stack, PZT pressure harvesters, electrostatic ion grid, and `V₂O₅/TiO₂` SCR catalyst yields net DC electricity, distilled water, and oxygen, with specialized continuations-in-part covering nuclear radiation harvesting (`CIP-2`), wearable metabolic-heat exoskeleton life support (`CIP-3`), biomimetic autonomous robotics (`CIP-4`), and peer-to-peer magnetic energy/thermal docking swarms (`CIP-5`).

## COMPLETE CONSOLIDATED CLAIMS (CLAIMS 1–31)

### Part I — Parent Utility Claims (`CPC-PAT-2026-001`)
1. **A closed-loop thermo-chemical-electrical energy conversion and fluid processing system comprising:** (a) a thermally insulated liquid reservoir maintained at approximately 90°C using recovered waste heat; (b) a fluidic vortex amplifier coated internally with chemical-vapor-deposited (CVD) graphene receiving preheated liquid from said reservoir and shaped high-temperature exhaust gas to induce cavitation; (c) a nanosecond pulsed plasma stage comprising a 4.2 V, 20 W zero-voltage-switching (ZVS) driver, a 1000× primary MnZn ferrite U-core transformer, and an 8-stage Cockcroft-Walton multiplier producing a 33.6 kV corona and ozone arc across said cavitated mist; (d) an opposed-piston linear alternator assembly operating at 50 Hz with a 25 mm bore, 40 mm stroke, and 30:1 compression ratio, featuring an yttria-stabilized zirconia (8YSZ) combustion liner, 5 µm air bearings, and an argon-filled silica aerogel thermal barrier isolating radial-magnetized N52H permanent magnets below 100°C; and (e) a multi-modal exhaust recovery train comprising a micro Tesla turbine, a variable-geometry turbocharger (VGT), a thermoelectric and pyroelectric stack, piezoelectric (PZT) pressure harvesters, an electrostatic ion grid, and a vanadium/titanium dioxide SCR catalyst block.
2. The system of claim 1, wherein maintaining said reservoir at 90°C via exhaust and engine-block waste heat reduces electrical cavitation power consumption by 30% to 45% relative to ambient 20°C feedwater.
3. The system of claim 1, wherein said CVD graphene coating provides a multi-layer boundary-layer slip interface enabling a continuous service life of at least 25,000 operating hours without cavitation pitting.
4. The system of claim 1, further comprising a 15 W electronic control unit (ECU) and automated fluidic valves regulating water injection into the vortex amplifier to maintain optimum intake humidity for plasma cracking while splitting exhaust into high-temperature cavitation-driving and low-temperature condensing streams.
5. The system of claim 1, wherein said 8YSZ liner and argon-filled silica aerogel thermal barrier sustain an internal combustion peak temperature of approximately 3,000°C while maintaining said N52H magnets below 100°C.
6. The system of claim 1, wherein said 33.6 kV corona and ozone arc atomizes and oxidizes urban smog particulates, volatile organic compounds (VOCs), and carbonaceous soot to supply supplemental chemical enthalpy.
7. **A method of operating a closed-loop plasma-assisted opposed-piston generator comprising:** preheating water to 90°C using captured exhaust heat; cavitating said preheated water in a graphene-coated vortex amplifier; dissociating the resulting mist with a 33.6 kV nanosecond ZVS plasma arc; compressing and reacting the radicalized mixture at 30:1 and 50 Hz inside an aerogel-shielded opposed-piston linear alternator; and harvesting residual exhaust energy across thermoelectric, pyroelectric, piezoelectric, ion-grid, and micro-turbine stages while condensing distilled water back to said reservoir.

### Part II — Nuclear Radiation Harvesting Claims (`CPC-PAT-2026-002-CIP`)
8. **A radiation-harvesting nuclear water remediation apparatus** comprising the closed-loop plasma cavitation system of claim 1 modified with: (a) a Ti-6Al-4V concentration sump lined with CVD-diamond-passivated 4H-SiC betavoltaic tiles capturing beta particles from concentrated ¹³⁷Cs and ⁹⁰Sr; (b) a 400 nm HfO₂/Ta₂O₅ and CVD-graphene Schottky gamma-voltaic liner; (c) a 28 mm tungsten-boron-carbide (W-B₄C) graded gamma shield coupled to a Bi₂Te₃/PMN-PT thermoelectric collar; and (d) radiation-hardened Sm₂Co₁₇ alternator magnets and Silicon-on-Insulator (SOI) electronics rated to 10⁸ Gy.
9. The apparatus of claim 8, wherein radiolytic ·OH and H· radicals generated inside contaminated feedwater pre-ionize the 33.6 kV plasma gap, reducing ZVS driver power draw by at least 15%.
10. The apparatus of claim 8, wherein 90°C graphene vortex cavitation and 33.6 kV plasma oxidation mineralize organic chelating agents prior to phase separation, achieving >10,000:1 solid radionuclide volume reduction.
11. The apparatus of claim 8, further comprising a counter-current vapor-phase reflux column utilizing kinetic isotope fractionation during nanosecond plasma dissociation to enrich tritiated water (HTO) by >40:1.
12. The apparatus of claim 8, wherein said W-B₄C shield attenuates ≥99.4% of incident gamma flux while converting absorbed gamma photon energy into auxiliary thermoelectric power.
13. **A method for simultaneous radioactive wastewater decontamination and off-grid power generation** using the apparatus of claim 8.

### Part III — Wearable Metabolic-Heat Life Support & Exoskeleton Claims (`CPC-PAT-2026-003-CIP`)
14. **A wearable closed-loop life-support and powered exoskeleton system comprising:** (a) a Liquid Cooling Garment (LCG) capturing at least 165 W of human metabolic waste heat to maintain a miniaturized 90°C wastewater reservoir; (b) a wearable micro-Cool Power Core (µCPC) dissociating exhaled CO₂, sweat, urine, and fecal organics via 90°C graphene vortex cavitation, 33.6 kV nanosecond plasma, an 8YSZ oxygen-ion separator, and a Sabatier/pyrolysis carbon reactor to output breathable O₂, sterile potable water, and net DC electricity; and (c) a multi-axis quasi-direct-drive (QDD) exoskeleton frame powered by said net DC electricity and a graphene supercapacitor bank.
15. The system of claim 14, wherein said QDD exoskeleton actuators operate in four-quadrant regenerative braking mode during eccentric descent to feed kinetic energy back into said graphene supercapacitor bank.
16. The system of claim 14, further comprising a 48 V / 650 W electrical Power Take-Off (PTO) port and a 1.2 MPa sterile air / 90°C ozonated spray fluidic PTO port.
17. The system of claim 14, further comprising an Electrodynamic Dust Shield (EDS) energized by a 33.6 kV slip-ring tap to repel lunar or Martian regolith dust from rotary exoskeleton bearings.
18. The system of claim 14, wherein solid carbon precipitated by methane pyrolysis is stored in a replaceable canister enabling at least 368 hours of zero-discharge isolated life support.
19. **A method of sustaining human respiration, hydration, and load-bearing locomotion** in sealed extreme environments using metabolic waste heat via the system of claim 14.
