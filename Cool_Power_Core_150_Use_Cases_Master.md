## Executive Overview

This Master Encyclopedia consolidates **150 distinct commercial, defense, heavy-manufacturing, chemical, circular-economy, medical, wearable-exoskeleton, autonomous-robotics, and deep-space applications** of the **Cool Power Core™ (CPC)**.

Across every deployment, the architecture exploits three foundational thermodynamic and plasma-chemical advantages:
1. **Waste-Heat Enthalpy Offloading (`382.93 Wth/core`):** Utilizes ambient process heat (`85°C–450°C`) or metabolic Liquid Cooling Garment (LCG) heat (`165–285 Wth`) to preheat the water reservoir to **90°C** (`43.93 W` sensible saved) and supply latent vaporization (`339.0 W`), cutting CVD graphene vortex cavitation power by **38%–52%**.
2. **33.6 kV Nanosecond ZVS Plasma Dissociation (`20 W` nominal):** Generates hydrated electrons ($e^-_{aq}$), hydroxyl radicals ($\cdot\text{OH}$), and ozone ($\text{O}_3$) to mineralize PFAS (`6-Log DRE`), PFCs (`CF4/SF6`), dioxins, H2S, siloxanes, and pathogens without open combustion.
3. **Multi-Modal Cogeneration & 25,000+ Hour Durability:** Pairs 50 Hz `30:1` opposed-piston `8YSZ` liners, argon-aerogel-shielded radial `N52H`/`Sm2Co17` linear alternators, micro Tesla turbines, `TEG/PMN-PT` thermal harvesters, and oil-free `5 µm` air bearings to export **+81.5 W to +364.5 W net continuous DC electricity per core** alongside pure distilled water and oxygen.
