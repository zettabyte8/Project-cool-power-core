## 1. DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF ADDITIONAL EMBODIMENTS (`[0185]–[0194]`)

* **`[0185]` Briefcase-Deployed Neural-Controlled Flight & Combat Exosuit (`CPC-SUIT-MK6` | FIG. 27):** In a further embodiment (`2700`), a portable rigid briefcase shell (`2702`, `560 × 380 × 168 mm`, `17.45 kg`) houses a collapsed `22-DOF` Titanium-6Al-4V and carbon-nanotube exosuit frame (`2704`) joined by shape-memory Nitinol deployment hinges (`2706`) that actuate within `4.2 seconds` upon biometric thumbprint verification. Dual dorsal closed-loop Cool Power Core™ units (`2708`, `µCPC-FLIGHT-2`) harvest metabolic heat (`37.0°C`) and actuator/thruster waste enthalpy (`+765.86 Wth`) against ambient slipstream air (`-55°C` to `+35°C`) via `90°C` CVD-graphene hydrodynamic cavitation and `33.6 kV` ZVS nanosecond plasma to export `+482.0 to +748.0 We` continuous power and recharge a `420 Wh` graphene-CNT supercapacitor bank (`2710`, `32.0 kW` burst).
* **`[0186]` Hybrid Magnetoplasma & EHD Ion Propulsion (`FIG. 27`):** Quad thruster pods (`2712`) mounted on the dorsal wings and plantar boots combine `0.55 T` `Sm₂Co₁₇`-confined `4,600 K` magnetoplasma thrusters (`1,420 N`) with `48 kV` silent electrohydrodynamic (EHD) ion stages (`460 N`) for `1,880 N` (`191.6 kgf`) total vertical lift without liquid chemical propellant. Dual forearm pods (`2714`) integrate tri-mode atmospheric-condensing Vortex-Condenser cannons (`1,420 m/s` cryo-sintered slugs, `1,850 m/s` plasma bolts, `1,064 nm` laser pulses) alongside `0.42 m` retractable magnetic-mirror plasma blades (`0.64 T` distal pinch).
* **`[0187]` 7-Layer Zero-RF Anti-Hijack Neural Helmet (`2716` | `CPC-NEURO-HELM`):** An articulated helmet (`2716`) houses a non-invasive `64-channel` dry high-density EEG and `32-channel` time-domain fNIRS neural decoder (`2718`, `<16 ms` intent latency). To prevent external interception, jamming, or remote takeover, the neural decoder (`2718`) is physically devoid of any RF/Wi-Fi/Bluetooth transceivers (`0 mW` RF emission), encased in dual-wall `Mu-Metal` / copper Faraday shielding (`2720`, `>115 dB` attenuation), coupled exclusively via a one-way physical optical fiber diode (`2722`) to a PUF-locked post-quantum (`NIST ML-KEM-1024`) cryptographic enclave (`2724`), and gated by a `<0.4 ms` somatic piezo-EMG muscle-override kill switch.
* **`[0188]` Leg-Assisted Neural Quad-Tentacle Exoskeleton (`CPC-OCTO-EXO` | FIG. 28):** In a companion embodiment (`2800`), a dorsal chassis (`2802`, `27.80 kg` pack mass) mounts four `2.45 m`, `9-DOF` hyper-redundant continuum manipulator tentacles (`2804`) driven by braided carbon-PTFE agonist-antagonist tendons (`85.0 kg` lift per arm; `340.0 kg` combined payload) terminating in four-fingered omni-claws (`2806`) with integrated micro-plasma torches and `120-bar` distilled water/oxygen jets.
* **`[0189]` Integrated 12-DOF Load-Bearing Exoskeleton Legs (`FIG. 28`):** The dorsal chassis (`2802`) is structurally continuous through a sacral pelvic yoke (`2808`) into bilateral `12-DOF` `Ti-6Al-4V` exoskeleton legs (`2810`, `420.0 kg` total structural rating) equipped with quasi-direct-drive (`QDD`) hip/knee/ankle actuators (`2812`) and ground-contact force plates (`2814`). All static and dynamic loads from both the `27.80 kg` pack and the `340.0 kg` tentacle payload bypass the human operator's spine directly into the ground (`<2.5 kg` perceived spinal load), while dual `µCPC-OCTO-2` cores (`2816`) harvest QDD actuator waste heat to export `+548.0 to +682.0 We` continuous power and `14.8–19.8 L/day` `<1 ppm TDS` water, controlled by an air-gapped zero-RF neural cap (`2818`).

## 2. FORMAL CLAIMS (`CLAIMS 78–83` | `37 CFR § 1.75` & `PCT RULE 6.2(b)`)

* **Claim 78 (Independent — Briefcase-Deployed Zero-RF Neural Flight Exosuit):** A portable briefcase-deployed flight and physical-assist exosuit system (`2700`), comprising:
  * (a) a rigid briefcase enclosure (`2702`) housing a collapsible multi-degree-of-freedom (`22-DOF`) exoskeleton frame (`2704`) interconnected by shape-memory alloy deployment hinges (`2706`) configured to deploy into a wearable sealed exosuit lined with a viscoelastic zero-bounce kinetic-and-directed-energy-harvesting armor layer;
  * (b) at least one dorsal closed-loop thermodynamic power core (`2708`) utilizing `90°C` graphene-coated hydrodynamic cavitation and `33.6 kV` zero-voltage-switching nanosecond pulsed plasma to convert metabolic and actuator waste heat against ambient slipstream air into continuous electrical power (`+482.0 to +748.0 We`) and distilled water;
  * (c) a plurality of hybrid magnetoplasma and electrohydrodynamic (`EHD`) ion thrusters (`2712`) powered by said power core (`2708`) and a supercapacitor buffer (`2710`) to generate vertical and vectorable flight thrust (`1,880 N`) without liquid chemical propellants; and
  * (d) an air-gapped neural-command helmet (`2716`) comprising a non-invasive `EEG/fNIRS` brain-computer interface (`2718`) physically isolated from radio-frequency (`RF`) transceivers within a `>115 dB` `Mu-Metal` Faraday shield (`2720`) and coupled exclusively through a unidirectional optical diode (`2722`) and post-quantum cryptographic enclave (`2724`) to prevent external takeover.

* **Claim 79 (Dependent on Claim 78):** The briefcase-deployed flight exosuit system (`2700`) of claim 78, further comprising dual forearm-mounted weapon pods (`2714`) each integrating a tri-mode atmospheric-condensing projectile launcher and a retractable `0.42 m` magnetically confined plasma blade (`0.64 T` distal mirror pinch) powered directly from said dorsal closed-loop power core (`2708`) and supercapacitor buffer (`2710`).

* **Claim 80 (Dependent on Claim 78):** The briefcase-deployed flight exosuit system (`2700`) of claim 78, wherein said air-gapped neural-command helmet (`2716`) further includes a somatic piezo-electromyography (`EMG`) muscle-override circuit configured to hardware-gate motor and thruster commands within `<0.4 ms` upon involuntary muscle flinch or cryptographic key mismatch.

* **Claim 81 (Independent — Leg-Assisted Air-Gapped Neural Continuum-Tentacle Exoskeleton):** A load-bearing neural-controlled multi-tentacle exoskeleton system (`2800`), comprising:
  * (a) a dorsal chassis (`2802`) supporting four hyper-redundant continuum manipulator tentacles (`2804`), each having `9 degrees of freedom` (`2.45 m` reach, `85.0 kg` lift capacity; `340.0 kg` combined payload) and terminating in a multi-fingered omni-claw (`2806`);
  * (b) bilateral `12-DOF` load-bearing exoskeleton legs (`2810`) structurally coupled to said dorsal chassis (`2802`) through a sacral pelvic yoke (`2808`) and terminating in ground-contact force plates (`2814`), wherein said exoskeleton legs (`2810`) route combined chassis and tentacle payload forces (`up to 420.0 kg`) directly to the ground to maintain `<2.5 kg` perceived load on a human operator's spine;
  * (c) dual closed-loop thermodynamic power cores (`2816`) mounted in said dorsal chassis (`2802`) configured to harvest quasi-direct-drive (`QDD`) actuator waste heat (`2812`) via `90°C` cavitation and `33.6 kV` nanosecond plasma to generate `+548.0 to +682.0 We` continuous electrical power and `14.8–19.8 L/day` of distilled water; and
  * (d) a wearable air-gapped neural-command cap (`2818`) devoid of wireless RF transceivers and coupled via a shielded optical link to translate operator brain intent (`<18 ms` latency) into coordinated multi-tentacle and bipedal leg trajectories.

* **Claim 82 (Dependent on Claim 81):** The load-bearing neural-controlled multi-tentacle exoskeleton system (`2800`) of claim 81, wherein each multi-fingered omni-claw (`2806`) integrates a magnetically focused micro-plasma cutting/welding torch and a `120-bar` high-pressure nozzle fed by distilled water and oxygen generated by said dual closed-loop power cores (`2816`).

* **Claim 83 (Dependent on Claim 81):** The load-bearing neural-controlled multi-tentacle exoskeleton system (`2800`) of claim 81, wherein said dorsal chassis (`2802`) further comprises deployable ballistic and thermal shield panels driven by said continuum tentacles (`2804`) and cooled by closed-loop `37°C` micro-channel fluid circulation.
