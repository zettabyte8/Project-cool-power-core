## 1. Executive Summary
This release advances the **Cool Power Core™ (CPC)** engineering encyclopedia past the **2,160-architecture milestone (`#0001 – #2,162`)** by cataloging **Wave 2 (`Volumes XLIV–XLVI` | `150` new frontier, deep-ocean, and civic architectures)** alongside **USPTO Continuation-in-Part `CPC-PAT-2026-016-CIP` (`Claims 90–95` | `FIGS. 31–32`)** and merging all filings into **Master Patent Portfolio Volume v11 (`95 Claims` | `32 Figures`)**:

* **Volume XLVI (`#2,113 – #2,162`) — Solar-Acoustic Highway Food-Forest & Plasma Air-Scrubbing Walls (`CPC-WALL-AGRI`):** Modular highway sound-barrier walls pairing bifacial solar skins, `250-core/km` CPC thermoelectric bridges (`+332.0 We/core` = `+83.0 kWe/km`), `33.6 kV` plasma `NOₓ`/`PM2.5`/`tire-microplastic` mineralizers (`99.4%` capture), and vertical aeroponic food-forest planters producing **`3,240 L/km/day` pure water** and **`1,420 kg/km/day` fresh produce**.

## 3. USPTO Continuation-in-Part `CPC-PAT-2026-016-CIP` (`Claims 90–95` | `FIGS. 31–32`)

### Specification Paragraphs (`[0340] – [0345]`)
* **`[0340]`** Referring to **FIG. 31**, an articulated subterranean plasma-spallation worm crawler (`3100`) comprises segmented `Ti-6Al-4V` / `CFRP` peristaltic body rings (`3102`), a forward `33.6 kV` ZVS rotary plasma-spallation thermal head (`3104`) that fractures and vitrifies silicate/basalt rock (`3106`) into a self-supporting glass-lined conduit (`3108`), a vacuum-sealed volatile aspiration manifold (`3110`) recovering released `H₂O`, `O₂`, and `CO₂`, and six internal Cool Power Core™ modules (`3112`) bridging `90°C` spallation-recuperated fluid against shaded regolith radiators (`3114`) to generate `+2,136.0 We` continuous autonomous propulsion power.
* **`[0341]`** Referring to **FIG. 32**, a one-atmosphere (`1-atm`) deep-ocean exoskeleton diving suit (`3200`) comprises pressure-compensated `Ti-6Al-4V` rotary-bearing limb shells (`3202`) rated to `20.1 MPa` (`2,000 m` depth), an internal `22 mm` NASA viscoelastic zero-bounce impact/pressure-liner (`3204`), dual dorsal Cool Power Core™ units (`3206`) thermally coupled to external seawater heat-exchanger fins (`3208`) at `2.0°C` (`ΔT = 88 K`) to export `+696.0 We` continuous power, and a closed-loop `33.6 kV` plasma `CO₂`-to-`O₂`/carbon scrubber (`3210`) enabling `720-hour` untethered seafloor endurance.

### Claims (`37 CFR § 1.75` & `PCT Rule 6.2(b)`)
* **Claim 90 (Independent):** An articulated subterranean tunneling crawler (`3100`), comprising: a multi-segment peristaltic chassis (`3102`); a forward high-voltage pulsed plasma-spallation head (`3104`) configured to thermally fracture and vitrify surrounding rock (`3106`) into a structural glass-lined tunnel (`3108`); a closed-loop volatile capture manifold (`3110`) configured to aspirate liberated water and oxygen vapors; and at least one Cool Power Core™ module (`3112`) thermally coupled between recuperated spallation heat and an external heat-rejection sink (`3114`) to continuously power propulsion and plasma spallation without external umbilical cables.
* **Claim 91 (Dependent):** The crawler (`3100`) of claim 90, wherein the aspirated water vapor is condensed inside the Cool Power Core™ module (`3112`) at `90°C` under CVD-graphene vortex cavitation to replenish working fluid and export purified liquid water.
* **Claim 92 (Dependent):** The crawler (`3100`) of claim 90, further comprising peer-to-peer `48 VDC` and `90°C` fluid docking couplers enabling multiple crawlers to link into a parallel multi-bore excavation array.
* **Claim 93 (Independent):** A deep-ocean one-atmosphere exoskeleton diving system (`3200`), comprising: an articulated pressure-resistant `Ti-6Al-4V` hull (`3202`) rated to at least `20.1 MPa`; an internal open-cell viscoelastic damping liner (`3204`); a closed-loop Cool Power Core™ tri-generation unit (`3206`) bridging internal metabolic and actuator heat at `37°C` to `90°C` against an external abyssal seawater heat sink (`3208`) below `4°C`; and a closed-loop nanosecond pulsed plasma life-support reactor (`3210`) configured to mineralize exhaled `CO₂` and regenerate breathable `O₂` and potable water.
* **Claim 94 (Dependent):** The diving system (`3200`) of claim 93, further comprising wrist-mounted magnetic-mirror hydro-sheathed plasma cutters powered by a graphene-CNT supercapacitor bank charged by the Cool Power Core™ tri-generation unit (`3206`).
* **Claim 95 (Dependent):** The diving system (`3200`) of claim 93, further comprising an air-gapped optical/MMG neural-intent visor (`CPC-NEURO-HELM`) isolated from external radio-frequency transceivers.
