## 1. FIELD OF THE INVENTION
The present invention relates generally to thermodynamic energy conversion, electro-fluidic propulsion/generation, and closed-loop exhaust gas remediation. More specifically, the invention relates to an integrated opposed-piston free-piston linear alternator system incorporating waste-heat-assisted ultrasonic and fluidic vortex cavitation, nanosecond pulsed high-voltage corona/ozone plasma dissociation, yttria-stabilized zirconia (YSZ) thermal containment with argon aerogel magnetic shielding, and multi-modal exhaust energy and condensate harvesting.

---

## 2. BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION
Conventional internal combustion engines and auxiliary power units suffer from substantial Carnot thermal losses, high parasitic friction from crankshaft linkages, incomplete hydrocarbon/particulate oxidation, and high nitrogen oxide ($	ext{NO}_x$) and sulfur oxide ($	ext{SO}_x$) emissions. Furthermore, prior-art water-injection and plasma-assisted combustion architectures expend excessive electrical energy to overcome the latent heat of vaporization and surface tension of ambient liquid water, often resulting in a net parasitic energy loss. Additionally, high-temperature combustion chambers operating near $3,000^\circ	ext{C}$ rapidly demagnetize rare-earth permanent magnets (such as N52H neodymium-iron-boron) in direct-drive linear alternators unless thermally decoupled.

There exists a need for an integrated, compact, long-life ($\ge 25,000$ operating hours) energy conversion and emissions remediation core that recycles exhaust thermal enthalpy to preheat and cavitate water at reduced electrical load, cracks hydrocarbons, urban smog, and water vapor via nanosecond pulsed high-voltage plasma, converts high-compression expansion directly into electrical energy via thermally shielded linear alternators, and harvests residual exhaust kinetic, thermal, acoustic/pressure, and ionic energy while recovering clean condensed water and oxygen.

## 3. SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION
The present invention ("Cool Power Core") overcomes the limitations of prior-art systems by combining a thermally decoupled 50 Hz free-piston opposed-piston linear alternator core with a waste-heat-driven graphene-coated fluidic vortex cavitator and a 33.6 kV nanosecond pulsed plasma dissociation stage.

In a preferred embodiment, the system comprises:
1. **Thermally Regulated 90°C Reservoir & Cavitation Stage:** A vacuum-insulated water reservoir maintained at approximately $90^\circ	ext{C}$ via recycled exhaust and block waste heat. Preheating water from $20^\circ	ext{C}$ to $90^\circ	ext{C}$ reduces sensible heating requirements by $pprox 43.93	ext{ W}$ at a $0.54	ext{ L/hr}$ ($0.15	ext{ g/s}$) feed rate, raises vapor pressure to $70.1	ext{ kPa}$, and reduces surface tension by $pprox 17\%$, cutting ultrasonic/fluidic cavitation power demand from $30	ext{ W}$ to $pprox 18.5	ext{ W}$.
2. **Graphene-Coated Fluidic / Vortex Amplifier:** A solid-state fluidic vortex amplifier coated with multi-layer chemical vapor deposition (CVD) graphene to prevent cavitation erosion over $\ge 25,000	ext{ hours}$. High-temperature shaped exhaust gas serves as the tangential control jet to induce low-pressure hydrodynamic cavitation in incoming ambient air (or urban smog) mixed with metered $90^\circ	ext{C}$ water, generating cold steam enriched with hydroxyl ($\cdot	ext{OH}$), hydrogen ($\cdot	ext{H}$), and oxygen ($\cdot	ext{O}$) radicals.
3. **Nanosecond Pulsed 33.6 kV Corona / Ozone Plasma Stage:** A $4.2	ext{ V}$, $20	ext{ W}$ zero-voltage switching (ZVS) nanosecond pulse driver coupled to a $1000	imes$ primary MnZn ferrite U-core transformer and an 8-stage Cockcroft-Walton voltage multiplier outputting $33.6	ext{ kV}$ across an optimized spark/corona gap. The plasma generates ozone ($	ext{O}_3$), atomizes incoming airborne particulates and volatile organic compounds (VOCs), and initiates radical chain branching prior to and during compression.
4. **30:1 Opposed-Piston Core & Argon Aerogel Magnetic Shielding:** Dual opposed $25	ext{ mm}$ bore pistons operating at a $40	ext{ mm}$ stroke and $50	ext{ Hz}$ ($3,000	ext{ cycles/min}$) within an yttria-stabilized zirconia (8YSZ) ceramic liner at a $30:1$ compression ratio and peak combustion temperatures approaching $3,000^\circ	ext{C}$. Pistons are supported on $5	ext{ }\mu	ext{m}$ porous air bearings and coupled directly to radial-magnetized N52H linear alternators thermally isolated by an argon-filled silica aerogel barrier maintaining magnet temperatures below $100^\circ	ext{C}$.
5. **Split-Exhaust Multi-Modal Harvesting & Closed-Loop Water Recovery:** Exhaust gas is bifurcated via automated fluidic valves controlled by a $15	ext{ W}$ electronic control unit (ECU). A high-temperature exhaust stream drives the vortex cavitator and Variable Geometry Turbocharger (VGT), while the remaining exhaust traverses a vanadium/titanium dioxide ($	ext{V}_2	ext{O}_5/	ext{TiO}_2$) selective catalytic reduction (SCR) honeycomb block, piezoelectric (PZT) exhaust pressure pulse harvesters, a biomimetic micro steam Tesla turbine, an electrostatic ion capture grid, a thermoelectric generator (TEG) and pyroelectric thermal stack, and a condenser returning purified liquid water to the $90^\circ	ext{C}$ reservoir.

## 5. DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE PREFERRED EMBODIMENTS

### 5.1 Thermal Reservoir and Reduced-Power Cavitation Subassembly
A vacuum-insulated reservoir stores recovered condensate and maintains the liquid water inventory at a regulated temperature of $90^\circ	ext{C} \pm 2^\circ	ext{C}$ using waste heat harvested from the engine block and exhaust heat exchanger. At a nominal feed rate of $0.54	ext{ L/hr}$ ($0.15	ext{ g/s}$), maintaining the reservoir at $90^\circ	ext{C}$ supplies $43.93	ext{ W}$ of sensible thermal energy continuously without drawing electrical power. Because water at $90^\circ	ext{C}$ exhibits a saturation vapor pressure of $70.1	ext{ kPa}$ (compared to $2.3	ext{ kPa}$ at $20^\circ	ext{C}$) and a $pprox 17\%$ reduction in surface tension, the acoustic and hydrodynamic cavitation threshold is substantially lowered, reducing electrical cavitation input from a nominal $30	ext{ W}$ baseline to approximately $18.5	ext{ W}$.

### 5.2 Graphene-Coated Fluidic Vortex Amplifier and Humidity Control
Metered $90^\circ	ext{C}$ water is injected into a solid-state fluidic vortex amplifier whose internal surfaces are coated with a multi-layer CVD graphene film ($5	ext{ to }10	ext{ atomic layers}$) to eliminate cavitation pitting and reduce boundary-layer shear drag over a service life exceeding $25,000	ext{ hours}$. Automated fluidic valves governed by a $15	ext{ W}$ ECU split the engine exhaust into a high-temperature control stream and a low-temperature recovery stream. The high-temperature exhaust stream is shaped through tangential nozzles into the vortex amplifier chamber, inducing a low-pressure vortex core that passively cavitates the injected water into ambient air or polluted urban air/smog, producing a cold steam aerosol while regulating relative humidity to the optimal threshold for plasma radical generation.

### 5.3 33.6 kV Nanosecond Pulsed Corona / Ozone Plasma Generator
A $4.2	ext{ V}$ DC input rail powers a $20	ext{ W}$ zero-voltage switching (ZVS) nanosecond pulse generator coupled to a $1000	imes$ step-up manganese-zinc (MnZn) ferrite U-core transformer. The transformer secondary feeds an 8-stage Cockcroft-Walton diode-capacitor voltage multiplier delivering $33.6	ext{ kV}$ across a precision corona/arc electrode gap positioned at the intake-to-chamber transition. Nanosecond pulsing prevents thermal arc runaway while generating dense non-thermal plasma streamers, ozone ($	ext{O}_3$), and dissociated $\cdot	ext{OH}$, $\cdot	ext{H}$, and $\cdot	ext{O}$ radicals, simultaneously cracking airborne carbonaceous particulates and VOCs into reactive light fuels.

### 5.4 Opposed-Piston Core, Thermal Barrier, and Linear Alternators
Two opposed free pistons ($25	ext{ mm}$ diameter, $40	ext{ mm}$ stroke, $39.27	ext{ cm}^3$ combined swept volume) reciprocate at $50	ext{ Hz}$ ($3,000	ext{ cycles/min}$) inside an yttria-stabilized zirconia (8YSZ) ceramic combustion liner. The opposed pistons achieve a $30:1$ volumetric compression ratio, igniting the radical-enriched cold steam and hydrocarbon/smog mixture at peak chamber temperatures up to $3,000^\circ	ext{C}$. Each piston shaft is supported radially on $5	ext{ }\mu	ext{m}$ porous air bearings for zero-contact operation and carries a radial-magnetized N52H permanent magnet rotor reciprocating within copper stator coils. An annular argon-filled silica aerogel thermal barrier ($\lambda pprox 0.014	ext{ W/m}\cdot	ext{K}$) isolates the 8YSZ liner from the N52H magnets, holding magnet temperature below $100^\circ	ext{C}$ to prevent irreversible thermal demagnetization.

### 5.5 Multi-Modal Exhaust Harvesting, SCR Remediation, and Dual-Bus Architecture
Exhaust gases exiting the $30:1$ opposed-piston ports pass through a Variable Geometry Turbocharger (VGT) loop and a vanadium pentoxide / titanium dioxide ($	ext{V}_2	ext{O}_5/	ext{TiO}_2$) selective catalytic reduction (SCR) honeycomb monolith, where residual $	ext{O}_3$ and $\cdot	ext{OH}$ radicals oxidize $	ext{NO}_x$, $	ext{SO}_x$, and carbon monoxide. Downstream transducers harvest residual energy across four parallel modalities:
* **Piezoelectric (PZT) Pressure Harvesters:** High-Curie-temperature bismuth titanate / PZT elements convert acoustic and exhaust pressure pulses into AC electrical energy.
* **Biomimetic Micro Steam Tesla Turbine:** Boundary-layer viscous drag disks extract residual kinetic enthalpy from the expanding steam-gas mixture.
* **Electrostatic Ion Capture Grid:** Collects charged plasma species and ionized exhaust particulates directly as DC current.
* **TEG & Pyroelectric Thermal Stack:** Bismuth telluride / skutterudite thermoelectric modules paired with pyroelectric elements convert steady and transient thermal gradients across the engine block and exhaust manifold into electricity.

A dual-bus power management architecture (high-voltage DC bus for linear alternator and plasma storage; low-voltage regulated bus for the $15	ext{ W}$ ECU, fluidic valves, and sensors) routes harvested power to loads while a downstream condenser liquefies exhaust water vapor back into the $90^\circ	ext{C}$ reservoir, yielding clean water and excess oxygen.

## 6. PATENT CLAIMS (DRAFT)

**Claim 1 (Independent System Claim):**  
An integrated closed-loop energy conversion and exhaust remediation system, comprising:  
(a) an opposed-piston free-piston core comprising two opposed pistons reciprocating at $50	ext{ Hz}$ within an yttria-stabilized zirconia (YSZ) ceramic combustion liner at a compression ratio of approximately $30:1$;  
(b) a pair of direct-drive linear alternators coupled to the opposed pistons, each linear alternator comprising a radial-magnetized N52H permanent magnet assembly thermally isolated from the YSZ ceramic liner by an argon-filled aerogel thermal barrier, wherein the opposed pistons are supported by $5	ext{ }\mu	ext{m}$ air bearings;  
(c) a thermal storage water reservoir maintained at approximately $90^\circ	ext{C}$ using waste heat recovered from the opposed-piston core and an exhaust loop;  
(d) a graphene-coated fluidic vortex amplifier receiving metered $90^\circ	ext{C}$ water from the thermal storage water reservoir, ambient intake air, and a shaped high-temperature exhaust gas control stream to generate cavitated cold steam enriched with hydrogen and oxygen radicals;  
(e) a nanosecond pulsed plasma generator comprising a $4.2	ext{ V}$ zero-voltage switching (ZVS) driver, a $1000	imes$ manganese-zinc (MnZn) ferrite U-core transformer, and an 8-stage Cockcroft-Walton voltage multiplier configured to generate approximately $33.6	ext{ kV}$ across a corona discharge gap to produce ozone ($	ext{O}_3$) and atomize intake particulates prior to compression;  
(f) a multi-modal exhaust recovery assembly comprising a Variable Geometry Turbocharger (VGT) loop, a vanadium/titanium dioxide ($	ext{V}_2	ext{O}_5/	ext{TiO}_2$) selective catalytic reduction (SCR) honeycomb block, piezoelectric (PZT) exhaust pressure harvesters, a biomimetic micro steam Tesla turbine, an electrostatic ion capture grid, and a thermoelectric generator (TEG) and pyroelectric heat recovery stack; and  
(g) a condenser configured to capture water from exhaust gases and return said captured water to the thermal storage water reservoir, alongside an electronic control unit (ECU) and automated fluidic valves regulating intake humidity and exhaust stream splitting across a split high/low voltage electrical bus.

**Claim 2 (Dependent — Waste-Heat Cavitation Power Reduction):**  
The system of claim 1, wherein maintaining the thermal storage water reservoir at $90^\circ	ext{C}$ using recycled waste heat reduces sensible heating demand by approximately $43.9	ext{ W}$ at a $0.54	ext{ L/hr}$ water feed rate and reduces electrical cavitation power consumption by at least $30\%$ relative to ambient $20^\circ	ext{C}$ water injection.

**Claim 3 (Dependent — Graphene Vortex Amplifier Geometry & Durability):**  
The system of claim 1, wherein internal flow channels of the fluidic vortex amplifier are coated with chemical vapor deposition (CVD) graphene to prevent cavitation erosion and sustain continuous operation for at least $25,000	ext{ hours}$.

**Claim 4 (Dependent — Exhaust Splitting & Humidity Control):**  
The system of claim 1, wherein the ECU and automated fluidic valves bifurcate exhaust gas exiting the opposed-piston core into a high-temperature tangential control stream routed into the fluidic vortex amplifier to induce hydrodynamic cavitation and a low-temperature stream routed through the TEG/pyroelectric stack and condenser, while modulating water injection to maintain optimum intake humidity for plasma cracking.

**Claim 5 (Dependent — Thermal Shielding of N52H Magnets):**  
The system of claim 1, wherein the YSZ ceramic liner and the argon-filled aerogel thermal barrier maintain a thermal gradient between a peak combustion chamber temperature of approximately $3,000^\circ	ext{C}$ and an operating temperature of the radial-magnetized N52H permanent magnet assembly below $100^\circ	ext{C}$.

**Claim 6 (Dependent — Urban Smog & Particulate Oxidation):**  
The system of claim 1, wherein the intake air comprises polluted ambient air or urban smog, and wherein the $33.6	ext{ kV}$ corona discharge and ozone stream atomize and oxidize airborne carbonaceous particulates and volatile organic compounds into combustible radicals within the opposed-piston core.

**Claim 7 (Independent Method Claim):**  
A method for closed-loop power generation and exhaust remediation, comprising:  
(a) preheating recovered liquid water in a thermal storage reservoir to approximately $90^\circ	ext{C}$ using waste heat from an opposed-piston engine block and exhaust stream;  
(b) injecting the $90^\circ	ext{C}$ water and intake air into a graphene-coated fluidic vortex amplifier driven by a shaped high-temperature exhaust gas jet to generate radical-enriched cold steam at reduced cavitation power;  
(c) exposing the cold steam and intake air to a $33.6	ext{ kV}$ nanosecond pulsed corona discharge generated by a $4.2	ext{ V}$ ZVS driver, a MnZn ferrite U-core transformer, and an 8-stage Cockcroft-Walton multiplier to generate ozone and dissociate water and airborne particulates;  
(d) compressing and igniting the resulting mixture at a $30:1$ compression ratio and $50	ext{ Hz}$ cycle rate inside a YSZ ceramic liner between opposed pistons supported on $5	ext{ }\mu	ext{m}$ air bearings;  
(e) converting piston reciprocation directly into electrical power via radial-magnetized N52H linear alternators shielded by an argon aerogel barrier;  
(f) harvesting residual exhaust energy via a VGT loop, a $	ext{V}_2	ext{O}_5/	ext{TiO}_2$ SCR catalyst block, PZT pressure transducers, a micro steam Tesla turbine, an ion capture grid, and a TEG/pyroelectric stack; and  
(g) condensing exhaust water vapor back into the $90^\circ	ext{C}$ thermal storage reservoir while outputting purified water, oxygen, and dual-bus electrical power.

## 7. ABSTRACT
An integrated closed-loop energy conversion and exhaust remediation system ("Cool Power Core") comprises a $50	ext{ Hz}$ free-piston opposed-piston core ($25	ext{ mm}$ bore, $40	ext{ mm}$ stroke, $30:1$ compression ratio) housed within an yttria-stabilized zirconia (YSZ) ceramic liner and coupled to direct linear alternators utilizing radial-magnetized N52H magnets thermally shielded by an argon aerogel barrier and supported on $5	ext{ }\mu	ext{m}$ air bearings. Waste heat maintains a water reservoir at $90^\circ	ext{C}$, reducing cavitation power demand as water and intake air/smog are cavitated into radical-rich cold steam inside a graphene-coated fluidic vortex amplifier driven by shaped high-temperature exhaust gas. A $4.2	ext{ V}$, $20	ext{ W}$ ZVS nanosecond pulse circuit with a $1000	imes$ MnZn ferrite U-core transformer and 8-stage Cockcroft-Walton multiplier applies $33.6	ext{ kV}$ corona/ozone plasma to atomize particulates and crack vapor. Exhaust energy is harvested via a VGT, $	ext{V}_2	ext{O}_5/	ext{TiO}_2$ SCR block, PZT pressure transducers, micro Tesla turbine, ion grid, and TEG/pyroelectric stack, while condensed water recycles to the $90^\circ	ext{C}$ reservoir for $\ge 25,000$-hour operation.
