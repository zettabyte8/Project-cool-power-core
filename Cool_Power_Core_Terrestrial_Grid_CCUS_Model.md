## 1. Executive Summary
Integrating modular **Cool Power Core (`CPC-GRID`)** arrays directly onto terrestrial industrial exhaust stacks, municipal waste-to-energy boilers, and biogas digesters transforms waste thermal energy and unburned flue-gas pollutants into dispatchable base-load electricity, ultra-pure distilled water, and mineralized carbon/urea precursors.

By tapping existing **140°C–450°C industrial flue-gas streams** and **85°C–95°C cooling-tower return loops**, a single `CPC-GRID` core eliminates all electrical water-preheating loads (**43.93 W saved**) and vaporization enthalpy (**339.0 W saved**). When fed with industrial flue gas containing **10%–18% CO₂**, unburned VOCs/syngas, and particulate matter (PM2.5/PM10), a **1,000-core containerized microgrid skid (20-ft ISO container)** delivers **+315.0 kW to +410.0 kW net continuous dispatchable electricity** while scrubbing **>98.5% of NOₓ, SOₓ, and particulates** and capturing **1.12 metric tons/day of CO₂** into solid graphitic carbon or ammonium bicarbonate fertilizer.

## 3. Flue-Gas Carbon Capture & Pollutant Mineralization Chemistry
Unlike amine-based carbon capture (which consumes 2.5–3.5 GJ/ton CO₂ in reboiler steam), the `CPC-GRID` module uses non-thermal **33.6 kV nanosecond corona plasma** and **8YSZ oxygen-ion separation** at 3,000°C peak opposed-piston compression:

1. **Direct Plasma-Thermal CO₂ Splitting & Carbon Precipitation:**
   * High-energy electrons from the 33.6 kV corona discharge excite CO₂ vibrational modes, splitting $\text{CO}_2 \rightarrow \text{CO} + \frac{1}{2}\text{O}_2$, followed by catalytic disproportionation ($2\text{CO} \rightarrow \text{C}_{(s)} + \text{CO}_2$) over the graphene-coated vortex walls to yield **high-purity carbon black / graphene nanoplatelets** (~12.6 g/hr per core; **302 kg/day per 1,000-core skid**).
2. **NOₓ, SOₓ, and VOC/Soot Destruction:**
   * Ozone ($\text{O}_3$) and $\cdot\text{OH}$ radicals generated in the cavitator oxidize insoluble $\text{NO}$ to $\text{NO}_2$ and $\text{SO}_2$ to $\text{SO}_3$.
   * Downstream **V₂O₅/TiO₂ SCR honeycomb** and cold-condensate scrubbing convert residual acids into dilute industrial-grade nitric/sulfuric salts while oxidizing **99.2% of PM2.5/PM10 soot and unburned hydrocarbons** into shaft/linear-alternator work.
3. **Net Atmospheric Water Harvesting:**
   * Cooling the moist combustion/flue stream below its dew point in the economizer condenses both the injected 0.54 L/hr process water and endogenous flue-gas moisture, yielding **+0.24 L/hr net distilled water per core** (**5,760 L/day per 1,000-core skid**).
