# PROJECT COOL POWER CORE
## Rev 4.0 — Headless Autonomous
### Designer: Thomas G. Coleman

Adopts V9 architecture. Adds the thermal cracking stage V9 omits. Restores core geometry.

**Not a generator.** Net ≥ 0 is a requirement; surplus is dumped as light and airflow.

---

## 1. How and why it works

Water is not fuel. But if you crack it with **waste heat** rather than electricity, the cycle returns more than it costs, because most of the cracking enthalpy is heat you were throwing away.

At 3000 °C the Gibbs term is 27% of enthalpy. 73% comes free from the previous cycle's exhaust. That is the whole machine. Below 2500 °C it stops.

**The electrical share must stay under 0.5 or net goes negative.** Arc: 0.27, works. Electrolysis: 1.18, never. NTP: ~1.0, never alone.

So: **arc cracks, NTP destroys, electrolyser guarantees ignition.** Three jobs, three devices, not one.

### 1.1 Stage by stage

| Stage | Why |
|---|---|
| LIG intake grid, 40 kHz self-clean | Charges and holds PM; ultrasonically sheds it |
| Intake orifice 20° | 45 m/s, no separation |
| EGR 20%, high leg | Returns hot vapour and radicals; −100 W vaporisation, −45 g/h draw |
| Cavitator σ 0.2 | 5000 K bubble collapse pre-splits the water |
| **Thermal arc 193 W, 3000 °C** | **Supplies 73% of cracking enthalpy as heat** |
| NTP 22 kV / 35 ns / DBD | ·OH, H·, O₃; destroys VOCs without heating |
| 30:1 compression | 861 K on a steam charge — three kelvin under autoignition |
| Electrolyser pilot, 30 W | Guaranteed H₂ for the kernel; the three kelvin of margin |
| Opposed free pistons, air bearings | No crank, no side load, no oil |
| Pyro + TEG + PZT | Thermal pulse, gradient, pressure pulses |
| Turbine | Pressure recovery, 25 W compound |
| 45° Y split | Hot leg to intake, cold leg to cleaning |
| SCR + DEF + ASC | Machine is a net NOx source |
| Tesla, vane heater, recuperator | Second stage, last heat, preheat closure |
| Condenser 25 °C | 58% recovery |
| Ti₄O₇ + UV-C + MnO₂ | Polish, sterilise, quench O₃ to <0.005 ppm |

### 1.2 Fanless

The pistons are a 118 L/min pump. Flow never came from the fan. The fan supplies pressure for mixture and quench velocity. Lose it and quench moves to the bearing compressor, frequency drops to 25 Hz, output falls ~35%, machine keeps cleaning. Tach loss for 500 ms latches non-critical FAULT to Passive Fallback.

## 7. Controls

**No screen. One armoured 3-position rotary switch.**

| Position | Action |
|---|---|
| OFF / LOCKOUT | Bleed supercap bus, vent bounce chamber, unlock ash drawer |
| ON — AUTO | Full closed loop, no user input |
| PASSIVE FALLBACK | Fan off, 25 Hz, natural aspiration |

**Halo:** solid cyan = nominal. Pulsing amber = 500 h self-clean running. Solid white = ash trap full.

**Self-clean:** 40 kHz transducer sweep + steam purge, 15 min every 500 h. Inaudible carrier; the hiss is audible.

**NFC/BLE:** read-only, encrypted, for service and for spacecraft/submarine buses.

Interlocks live in firmware and cannot be overridden from any control.

## 8. Build

1. Barrel: YSZ 0.4 mm, hone 25.000 +0.005, air gauge 3 stations, jets 12 × 0.3 mm, ultrasonic clean, 1.5 NL/min ±15%, jacket 2 bar zero drop.
2. Pistons: crown 0.02 mm proud, magnets 200 °C epoxy, gauss 1.2–1.35 T, balance 0.05 g·mm, **thermal break at 20 mm**.
3. Alternators: 42T class H, varnish, >100 MΩ, gap 0.6 mm.
4. Turbo: balance 0.2 mg·mm, tip 0.15 mm, spin to 240 000, **coast-down >25 s**, prove accumulator.
5. Cavitator and arc: 0.8 mm at 60°/20°, σ 0.2, arc gap 1.5 mm.
6. **NTP: DBD barrier fitted, 4 mm gap, 22 kV, 35 ns, 10/10 at 20 kHz.**
7. Quench: 1.8 mm, 60° cone, 15°, 8 mm standoff.
8. Exhaust: 45° Y, high leg up, SCR no bypass, ASC behind, Tesla gaps free.
9. Recuperator: braze, 2 bar both sides, drainable, counterflow.
10. Polish train: Ti₄O₇ biased, UV-C, MnO₂ mesh.
11. Aerogel 6 mm encapsulated on hot nodes. Not over fan or condenser.
12. Final: TDC 2.759 mm ±0.05, bore-scope, 6 bar decay <0.1 bar/min.

## 11. Failure modes, top six

Recuperator perforation · piston contact · wrong alloy acid attack · **spent softener** · crown liberation · SCR below light-off.

Detection gaps that must close first: conductivity cell (300 µS/cm), barrel accelerometer 12–18 kHz, continuous DE estimate, pressure-trace insert signature.

## 13. Environments

**Deep sea:** 2–4 °C sink raises condenser sub-cooling 38%, cuts draw. Works inside a 1 atm hull. Pressure-equalised pod limited to ~50 m.

**Space:** 0 g needs the fluorographene wicking condenser. 100% closed water. N₂ for bearings. Radiator 1.0 m²/kW at 400 K.

## 14. Honest limits

**105 W base, band −45 to 250.** f unmeasured. Below 0.15 the halo goes dark.

**Water is a consumable: 4.7–5.2 L/day** at fallback throughput. Gross condensate is 0.31 L/h; the engine eats it.

**Net NOx source.** Catalyst must not go cold.

**$210 at 10 M units, not $88.** Inconel barrel, turbo and paired gas sensors set the floor.

**45 000 h is a target, not a claim.** 8 000 h MTBO until measured. NTP electrode life is the real limiter at 20 kHz — 3.2 × 10¹² pulses.

Three physical limiters: piston tail film, crown inserts, turbo bearing. Air bearings only, accumulator mandatory.
