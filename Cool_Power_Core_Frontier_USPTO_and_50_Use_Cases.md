## Part 1: Autonomous Underwater-to-Air Transmedium Drone (`CPC-TRANS`)

### 1.1 Architectural Overview
The **`CPC-TRANS`** adapts a lightweight **2.35 kg** `µCPC` core into a hybrid variable-sweep **autonomous underwater-to-air transmedium unmanned vehicle (UUV/UAV)** capable of seamless vertical air-water interface breaching, deep littoral loitering (**down to 1,500 m**), and stratospheric transit (**up to 18 km**).

* **Dual-Sink Thermodynamic Switching:**
  * **Submerged Mode (`0–1,500 m`, `2°C–12°C` seawater):** Routes exhaust steam directly into a conformal **Ti-6Al-4V keel skin condenser** (`0.12 MPa` internal pressure), eliminating overboard exhaust compression penalties (`-310 W` saved at `1,000 m`) and boosting Bi₂Te₃/PMN-PT TEG recovery to **+68.0 W** for **+178.4 We** net continuous power (`100%` silent electromagnetic/pump-jet cruise).
  * **Airborne Mode (`0–18 km`, `-56.5°C` to `+35°C` ambient):** Diverts exhaust through an aft micro-Tesla/VGT vectored thrust nozzle while using leading-edge carbon-carbon wing skins as a convective cold sink, generating **+162.0 We** net power to drive coaxial foldable ducted rotors.
* **Buoyancy & Ballast Control via Closed-Loop Cavitation:** Uses the `90°C` CVD graphene vortex cavitator and `33.6 kV` ZVS plasma stage to dissociate onboard seawater distillate into `H₂/O₂` gas to displace variable-buoyancy bladders for silent depth hovering, recombining the gas in the `50 Hz` opposed-piston core for high-speed surface breach (`38 knots` exit velocity).
* **Endurance:** **420+ continuous hours** (`17.5 days`) multi-domain autonomous patrol without refueling or surfacing for snorkel air.
