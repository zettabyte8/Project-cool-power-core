## 1. Executive System Overview

The **`CPC-RIFLE-ATMOS-B` ("Vortex-Bayonet" Mk. II)** is an evolutionary close-quarters and planetary-breaching variant of the `CPC-RIFLE-ATMOS` atmospheric-condensing tri-mode rifle. By integrating an under-barrel, quick-detach **`0.38 m` (15.0 in) magnetically confined plasma bayonet emitter** derived directly from the `CPC-SABER-MARS` Halbach-Bitter mirror architecture, the weapon unifies long-range atmospheric-condensing kinetic/directed-energy fire with zero-recoil close-quarters cutting, airlock/hull breaching, and defensive melee capability.

* **Total Weapon Mass (Dry / Unloaded):** `5.24 kg` (`11.55 lb`, including `0.39 kg` integrated under-barrel plasma bayonet emitter)
* **Overall Length:** `845 mm` (bayonet retracted/stowed) / `1,185 mm` (plasma blade ignited at `380 mm` extension)
* **Primary Power Interface:** `48 VDC` armored smart-grip induction link to `CPC-PA-MK3` Power Armor dorsal capacitor bus (`185 Wh` graphene-CNT buffer) + onboard `32 Wh` weapon supercapacitor + `65 mL` micro-cartridge closed-loop fluid buffer (`>42 hr` standalone standby via ambient `ΔT` micro-core).

## 2. Under-Barrel Plasma Bayonet Architecture (`CPC-BAYO-380`)

Unlike conventional steel bayonets that dull, snap, or fail against ceramic/composite armor, hull plating, or frozen planetary regolith, the **`CPC-BAYO-380`** projects a tightly bounded, `380 mm` (`15.0 in`) high-density plasma blade anchored to a `Ti-6Al-4V / CVD-Diamond` under-barrel rail lug.

### 2.1 Magnetic Bottle & Blade Geometry
* **Emitter Envelope:** `118.0 mm` length × `34.0 mm` outer diameter (`0.39 kg` total mass), mounted beneath the `520 mm` linear-induction barrel via a dual-lug `MIL-STD-1913 / QD-MAG` locking collar.
* **Hybrid Magnetic Mirror Confinement:**
  * **Proximal Throat (`z = 0 mm`):** Miniaturized `Sm₂Co₁₇` 8-segment Halbach permanent-magnet ring (`0.38 T`) paired with a micro-channel liquid-cooled `CuCrZr` Bitter coil (`+0.24 T`) yielding **`0.62 T` peak throat flux**.
  * **Blade Spine & Distal Pinch (`z = 380 mm`):** Shaped quadrupole-poloidal field taper terminating in a **`0.64 T` magnetic mirror pinch** (`plasma beta β = 0.16`), preventing axial plasma blow-off and giving the blade a crisp, tapered chisel-point profile (`28 mm` width × `9 mm` thickness).
* **Plasma Core Parameters:**
  * **Core Temperature:** `4,450 K` (`4,177°C`) ionized `CO/O⁺/H⁺/N₂⁺` plasma generated from condensed atmospheric gases or the onboard `65 mL` closed-loop water/CO₂ micro-reservoir.
  * **Steady-State Sustainment Power:** **`1,450 We`** in Earth sea-level atmosphere (`101.3 kPa`, convective-sheath stabilized) and **`580 We`** in thin Martian CO₂ atmosphere (`610 Pa`, `>94%` lower convective loss).
  * **Pulse Overdrive Mode ("Breach-Cleave"):** **`2,800 We`** for `4.0 s` bursts (`5,600 K` core), capable of severing `18 mm` hardened AR500 steel plate, `25 mm` Ti-6Al-4V hull bulkhead, or frozen basaltic regolith in `<1.8 seconds` with **zero mechanical pushback/recoil**.

### 2.2 Coaxial Gas-Curtain & Vapor Recovery Shroud
* **Dual-Stage Vortex Shroud:** An outer annular nozzle injects a high-velocity laminar sheath of chilled ambient air/CO₂ (`-18°C` from the rifle's CPC cryo-stage), thermally insulating the barrel and operator's support hand (`<36°C` grip temperature) while recovering **84% of ionized working fluid** back into the cyclone condenser on Earth and **96% on Mars**.
* **Parry & Deflection Coupling:** When the `0.62 T` magnetic bottle intersects an opposing conductive blade or incoming low-mass metallic shrapnel, induced eddy currents and Lorentz magnetic pressure create a solid electromagnetic resistance barrier without blade collapse.

3. **Thermal-Drop Prevention:** When the plasma bayonet is ignited in close-quarters combat, barrel and Bitter-coil waste heat (`+620 Wth`) is routed directly into the `µCPC-WPN` core, accelerating cryogenic slug condensation so a `14.5 g` atmospheric slug is pre-molded and ready for immediate follow-up fire the instant the bayonet disengages.
