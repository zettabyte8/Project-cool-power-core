## PART I: WELCOME TO YOUR SANCTUARY CITY

Welcome home. This campus was engineered and chartered on a simple, non-negotiable premise: **every human being deserves a safe, climate-controlled private home, abundant clean food and water, world-class creative tools, and total freedom over their own time and movement.**

### 1. The Five Irrevocable Guarantees (Your Bill of Rights)
1. **Unconditional Entry & Zero Means-Testing:** You do not need sobriety prerequisites, credit checks, background clearances, or government vouchers to enter. A simple biometric or RFID smart-key pass is issued upon arrival.
2. **Total Freedom of Movement (Zero Curfews):** Come and go 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Visit family, work outside the campus, travel, or return whenever you wish. Gates never lock residents in.
3. **Unlimited Stay Duration:** There is no 30-day, 90-day, or 2-year clock. You may live in your private studio home for a week, a decade, or the rest of your life.
4. **All Basic Needs Covered Free of Charge:**
   * **Private Studio Dwelling (`28 m²` / `301 sq ft`):** Acoustic-insulated Cross-Laminated Timber (CLT) & hemp-lime residence with private bathroom, shower, induction kitchenette, queen bed, high-speed fiber Wi-Fi, and dedicated `CPC-RES-1` zero-septic utility pod.
   * **Nutritious Daily Meals:** Three chef-prepared meals daily plus 24/7 pantry access harvested from the campus's 8-tier hydroponic/aquaponic vertical farms (`1,560 kg/day` fresh produce).
   * **Healthcare & Wellness:** On-site primary medical clinic, dental care, trauma-informed counseling, recovery support (strictly voluntary), and pharmacy services.
   * **Climate-Controlled Comfort:** Year-round **`21.5°C (71°F)`** and **`48% RH`** beneath the `4.2-hectare` triple-layer ETFE canopy with `6-Log` plasma air purification.
5. **Tuition-Free Education & Creative Mastery:** Full access to all six Artisanal & Technical Academies with zero tuition, zero material fees, and no prerequisites.

To keep the sanctuary peaceful for all 1,000 neighbors, residents agree to three basic community norms:
1. **Non-Violence & Zero Harassment:** Physical violence, threats, theft, or weapons inside the campus result in immediate restorative mediation or temporary cooling-off separation.
2. **Quiet Residential Wards (`22:00 – 07:00`):** Late-night music, woodworking, and metal fabrication remain in acoustically isolated Agora studios after 10:00 PM.
3. **Harm-Reduction & Personal Autonomy:** Personal choices are respected; substance use is prohibited in shared youth/dining halls and heavy-machinery workshops for safety, with voluntary medical detox and peer recovery available on demand.

## PART IV: ARTISANAL COOPERATIVE GUILD CHARTER & 85% / 15% REVENUE SPLIT

### Article 1: Ownership of Creative Work
Every physical item, digital design, software tool, recipe, or artwork created by a resident in campus studios is **100% the intellectual and physical property of its creator**. The campus claims zero ownership over resident creations.

### Article 2: Transparent 85% / 15% Cooperative Sales Split
When a resident chooses to sell their work through the **Sanctuary Artisanal Cooperative** (online marketplace, weekend Agora public market, or wholesale corporate gifting contracts):
* **85.0% Direct to Creator:** Deposited directly into the resident's personal bank account, debit card, or cooperative credit union wallet within 48 hours of sale—with **zero deductions** for rent, food, utilities, or tuition.
* **15.0% Guild Materials & Civic Endowment:** Replenishes raw hardwoods, metals, clay, organic fabrics, packaging, payment processing fees, and funds replication of new Sanctuary Cities.

### Article 3: Cooperative Governance & Maker Council
* Each of the six guilds elects **two Resident Guild Stewards** every six months via one-resident, one-vote secret ballot.
* The 12-member **Maker Council** manages studio tool upgrades, raw-material bulk purchasing, gallery curation, and restorative peer mediation.
